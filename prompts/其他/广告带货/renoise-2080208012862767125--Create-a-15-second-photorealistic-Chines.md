# Create a 15-second photorealistic Chinese mandarin-orange commercial using…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second photorealistic Chinese mandarin-orange commercial using @image1 as the exact reference for the female model, traditional market, fruit display, and visual flow. Convert it into normal full-screen live-action footage without showing the storyboard grid, panel numbers, borders, or text from the reference.

A young Chinese woman walks through a bright open-air market in warm morning sunlight. She notices a beautiful stall filled with fresh mandarin oranges, smiles, and gently selects several fruits from the woven baskets. Show premium close-ups of the natural orange peel, green leaves, her hands opening the fruit, and the juicy segments sparkling in the sunlight.

She tastes one segment and reacts with genuine delight. End with her holding a mandarin near her face beside the market stall, followed by a clean hero shot of fresh mandarins arranged in a basket.

Mandarin narration:

「清晨的市场里，藏着最自然的新鲜。」
「一眼就能看见，阳光孕育的饱满蜜橘。」
「轻轻剥开，清甜的果香立刻散开。」
「咬下一口，清甜多汁，满满都是鲜活的好味道。」

Final model dialogue:

「这一口，就是市场里最新鲜的甜。」
```
