# Create a 15-second photorealistic Korean Butter Tteok commercial using @image1…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second photorealistic Korean Butter Tteok commercial using @image1 only as a visual reference for the Korean female model, Butter Tteok package, ingredients, cooking process, food styling, kitchen, restaurant, and scene progression. Convert it into continuous full-screen live-action footage. Do not reproduce the storyboard grid, panel labels, timecodes, headings, Chinese captions, borders, or graphic annotations.

Open with an extreme macro shot of glossy Butter Tteok bubbling gently in a pan. A golden butter cube begins to melt over the orange-red sauce as warm steam rises.

A young Korean woman in a cream knit sweater sits near a bright kitchen window and remembers a comforting Korean flavor from a past trip. She opens the cream-colored Butter Tteok package on a wooden counter, revealing the rice cakes, sauce packet, and seasoning.

Show the rice cakes cooking in gently boiling water, then being coated evenly with a thick glossy sauce. Place one golden butter cube in the center and show it melting slowly while sesame seeds and herbs fall naturally over the dish.

Korean narration:

「오늘은, 조금 특별한 한국의 맛.」
「여행에서 만났던 그 따뜻한 한입이 생각날 때.」
「버터 떡볶이면, 집에서도 간편하게.」
「쫄깃한 떡을 부드럽게 익히고.」
「진한 소스로 맛있게 감싸면.」
「마지막 버터 한 조각으로, 더 깊고 부드럽게.」

The woman lifts one rice cake with chopsticks, tastes it naturally, and says:

「음, 이 맛이었어!」

Transition to a cozy Korean restaurant at night. She places the Butter Tteok in the center of the table while three adult friends reach in with chopsticks. She says:

「맛있는 건 같이 먹어야지!」

End with a premium product hero shot of the cream Butter Tteok package beside a black pan filled with glossy rice cakes, melting butter, sesame, and herbs. Final Korean narration:

「간편하게 즐기는 따뜻한 행복, 버터 떡볶이.」
```
