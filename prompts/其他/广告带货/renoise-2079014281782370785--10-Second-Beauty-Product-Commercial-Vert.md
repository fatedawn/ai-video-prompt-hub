# 10-Second Beauty Product Commercial (Vertical 9:16)

## 提示词（English）

```text
10-Second Beauty Product Commercial (Vertical 9:16)
Style: Ultra-realistic luxury beauty commercial, soft pink aesthetic, premium cosmetic advertising, clean studio lighting, cinematic, high-end skincare/makeup brand, shallow depth of field, smooth transitions, 4K HDR.
Scene 1 (0–2s) – Hook
Shot: Close-Up | Front Angle | Fast Push-In
A beautiful young woman looks directly into the camera with a surprised and curious expression while holding a matte lip cream beside her face. She slightly raises her eyebrows and opens her lips naturally. Dynamic bold text appears: "Lip Cream That Lasts All Day?!" Soft pink background with glowing highlights.
Scene 2 (2–4s) – Product Hero
Shot: Hero Shot | Slight Low Angle | Smooth Dolly-In
The lip cream bottle and premium packaging stand upright on a glossy reflective surface. Elegant light streaks create luxurious reflections across the product. Floating particles shimmer around the bottle. Animated text appears: "NEW COLOR" and "Matte Last Lip Cream."
Scene 3 (4–6s) – Texture Demo
Shot: Extreme Macro Close-Up | Macro Push-In
The applicator slowly glides across soft lips, revealing an ultra-smooth matte finish with rich pigment and detailed texture. The lipstick blends evenly without streaks. A glowing badge appears: "12 HOURS" with subtle sparkle animation.
```
