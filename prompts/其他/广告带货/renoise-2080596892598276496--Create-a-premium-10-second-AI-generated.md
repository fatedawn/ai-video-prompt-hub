# Create a premium 10-second AI-generated UGC-style product commercial in a 1:1…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a premium 10-second AI-generated UGC-style product commercial in a 1:1 aspect ratio featuring gray sports sneakers. No face visible—only hands and feet. Use soft natural lighting, a clean neutral background, and smooth cinematic camera movements. Showcase realistic textures, breathable mesh fabric, cushioned sole, premium stitching, and modern sporty design.
Scene 1 (0–1s): Open a gray shoe box revealing neatly packed gray sneakers.
Scene 2 (1–2s): Close-up handheld rotation highlighting premium materials and details.
Scene 3 (2–3s): Bend the shoe to demonstrate flexibility and lightweight comfort.
Scene 4 (3–4s): On-feet walking shot showing a stylish everyday look.
Scene 5 (4–5s): Close-up of the anti-slip outsole with textured grip.
Scene 6 (5–6s): Front and side angles emphasizing the modern sporty design.
Scene 7 (6–7s): Macro shot of the soft padded interior and tongue.
Scene 8 (7–8s): Wipe the shoe with a cloth to show easy cleaning.
Scene 9 (8–9s): Display multiple color variants with gray as the hero product.
Scene 10 (9–10s): Place the gray sneakers back into a SPORT branded box for a clean premium closing shot with the text: "Comfort in Every Step."
Style: Ultra-realistic, AI-generated, 4K, cinematic, smooth transitions, commercial quality, highly detailed product photography, premium e-commerce advertisement.
```
