# Video Generation Prompt: Animating Mobile Screens

> A prompt for Grok Imagine focused on digital product video generation, instructing the AI to animate a hand holding a phone and switching between two specified images (presumably screenshots of mobile screens).

## 提示词（English）

```text
These are mobile screens. Animate a hand holding a phone and switching between @ image1  and @ image2
```
