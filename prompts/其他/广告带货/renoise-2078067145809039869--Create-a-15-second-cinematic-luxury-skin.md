# Create a 15-second cinematic luxury skincare commercial with a warm beige and…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second cinematic luxury skincare commercial with a warm beige and cream aesthetic, soft golden-hour lighting, elegant minimalist interior, and premium beauty advertisement style. Ultra-realistic, 4K, shallow depth of field.
Scene 1 (0–3s):
A beautiful young Asian woman sits in front of a mirror in soft morning sunlight, gently touching her face with a thoughtful expression. Slow cinematic push-in.
Scene 2 (3–6s):
Close-up of elegant hands picking up a luxurious red skincare cream jar from a vanity table. The lid opens slowly, revealing a rich, creamy texture. Macro beauty shot with soft reflections.
Scene 3 (6–10s):
She applies the cream gently to her cheeks using smooth circular motions. Close-up of glowing skin and the cream blending naturally. Soft smile appears as her skin looks fresh and radiant.
Scene 4 (10–13s):
She stands by a bright window, smiling confidently while holding the cream jar beside her face. Warm sunlight creates a healthy, natural glow.
Scene 5 (13–15s):
Hero product shot on a luxurious beige marble surface surrounded by white flowers and pearl accents. Slow camera rotation around the jar. End with the tagline:
"Reveal Your Natural Glow."
Style: Luxury skincare commercial, cinematic beauty advertisement, premium product photography, soft creamy color grading, elegant camera movements, realistic skin texture, HDR, 4K, smooth transitions, highly detailed.
```
