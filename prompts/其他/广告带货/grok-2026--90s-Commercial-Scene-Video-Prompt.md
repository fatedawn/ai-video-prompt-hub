# 90s Commercial Scene Video Prompt

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to generate a video scene reminiscent of a 90s commercial, featuring a woman in a white shirt eating a hamburger, with the juice dripping onto her shirt.

## 提示词（English）

```text
A commercial tv in the 90s wearing white shirt eating hamburger, as she bites the juicy dripping on her shirt. You guys remember seeing it back in those days?
```
