# Create a 15-second ultra-luxury jewelry commercial featuring a beautiful female…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second ultra-luxury jewelry commercial featuring a beautiful female model wearing an elegant diamond necklace. Warm golden luxury lighting, premium boutique atmosphere, soft cinematic bokeh, realistic skin texture, natural makeup, champagne satin dress, photorealistic 8K quality, shallow depth of field, high-end fashion editorial style.

Scene 1 (0–3s): Extreme close-up of the diamond necklace. Soft fade-in as the diamonds sparkle with realistic light reflections. Slow cinematic push-in.

Scene 2 (3–6s): Medium portrait of the model looking confidently into the camera. Gentle smile, graceful posture, subtle hair movement, warm golden lighting.

Scene 3 (6–9s): Close-up as she delicately touches the necklace with her fingertips while looking down. Smooth camera movement, elegant expression.

Scene 4 (9–12s): Macro shot of the necklace pendant. Brilliant diamond fire, crisp reflections, luxurious sparkle, rotating camera with cinematic focus pull.

Scene 5 (12–15s): Final centered portrait of the model facing the camera with the necklace perfectly framed. Premium glow, elegant background bokeh, luxury brand aesthetic. End with the text: “Elegance you can wear. Shine without saying a word.”

Style: Hyper-realistic, cinematic, luxury fashion advertisement, premium jewelry campaign, HDR, soft golden tones, volumetric lighting, flawless composition, smooth transitions, no watermark, no distortion, no extra jewelry, no text until final scene.
```
