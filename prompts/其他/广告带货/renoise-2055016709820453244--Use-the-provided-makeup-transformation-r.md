# Use the provided makeup transformation reference sheet as the complete visual…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the provided makeup transformation reference sheet as the complete visual guide for a 15-second realistic beauty tutorial video.

The top row is the step-by-step motion guide. Follow the makeup sequence visually:

prep and prime, base, contour and bronze, blush and highlight, brows, eyes, liner and lashes, lips, setting spray, final look.

The middle section is the character reference. Keep the same woman consistent throughout the video: medium olive skin tone, long dark wavy hair, refined glam features, gold hoop earrings, black outfit, elegant beauty-influencer presence, polished but natural expressions.

The right and lower sections are the location and product references. Recreate the warm luxury vanity setup with mirror lights, makeup brushes, foundation bottles, palettes, lipsticks, setting spray, soft warm room lighting, and glamorous beauty-studio atmosphere.

Create a realistic 16:9 cinematic beauty video, 15 seconds total.

Show the woman transforming from fresh prepared skin into a polished glam makeup look. Use smooth, elegant, close-up beauty shots that emphasize skin texture, brush motion, product application, blending, shimmer, eyeliner detail, lip color, setting mist, and the final confident beauty pose.

Camera style:
premium beauty tutorial meets luxury cosmetics campaign. Use soft close-ups, clean front-facing shots, gentle push-ins, shallow depth of field, mirror-light bokeh, smooth transitions, and stable camera movement. Avoid fast chaotic cuts. Each makeup action should be readable but cinematic.

Lighting:
warm vanity light mixed with soft studio fill, glowing skin highlights, subtle shimmer, flattering shadows, refined golden-beige color palette.
Movement quality:

gentle, precise, polished, realistic hand motions. Brushes should touch the skin naturally, powders and creams should blend smoothly, setting spray should create a soft mist, and the final look should feel elegant and aspirational.

Do not animate the infographic or text. Use the sheet only as reference. Create a live-action-style cinematic makeup transformation scene.

End frame:
final glam look, hair softly styled down, woman looking into camera with a subtle confident smile, polished glowing skin, balanced beauty portrait composition.
```
