## 提示词（English）

```text
Subject & Setting: High-end cinematic commercial in a total black-void studio. A visionary female lead in a crisp, sterile white designer coat over black professional attire. Her expression is stoic, commanding, and hyper-focused.

Action & Narrative: She holds a premium book titled "ALMOST HUMAN" with extreme care. The sequence features slow, intentional macro-zooms on her eyes and a subtle, knowing smile. She turns the pages slowly to reveal intricate, surrealist anatomical sketches. She maintains direct, piercing eye contact with the lens.

Visual Style: Prestige "dark-noir" aesthetic. Intense Chiaroscuro lighting creating a sharp silhouette against the void. Palette: Stark monochrome with a single accent of deep crimson on the lips. Fluid, high-fashion movement.

VO & Atmosphere: Clinical, whispered, and rhythmic pacing. Opens with a whisper "'Almost Human' by Lola Viscera." Ends with another whisper "Open yourself up."

Technical Direction: 8k, cinematic film grain, 24fps, hyper-sharp focus on textured paper and ink details, psychological and avant-garde atmosphere.
```
