# Create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic football advertisement set in a…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic football advertisement set in a massive futuristic stadium during a FIFA World Cup 2026 atmosphere.

Scene 1 (0-3 sec):
Aerial drone shot outside a huge illuminated football stadium at night, thousands of fans arriving, stadium lights glowing, cinematic atmosphere, camera slowly flies toward the stadium entrance. Epic scale, Hollywood sports commercial style.

Scene 2 (3-6 sec):
The drone smoothly enters inside the stadium through the players’ tunnel. A young female football player walks through the entrance with her team behind her. Slow-motion hero moment, determined face, emotional eyes, teammates walking together, crowd sounds building, dramatic stadium lights reflecting on their faces.

Scene 3 (6-10 sec):
The match begins. The female player controls the ball with incredible skill, runs through defenders from the opposite team, intense football action, dynamic tracking camera following her movement, close-up shots of her boots, face, and the ball. She shoots and scores a powerful winning goal.

Scene 4 (10-13 sec):
The entire stadium explodes with emotion. Fans cheering, people celebrating, teammates running toward her, fireworks and stadium celebration lights, emotional victory moment. Camera rises upward showing the massive crowd and the energy of the stadium.

Scene 5 (13-15 sec):
The drone flies out of the stadium into the sky, revealing the full glowing stadium from above. Epic final shot with fireworks around the arena. Transition to a powerful cinematic end screen showing:

FIFA WORLD CUP 2026
with the FIFA World Cup trophy appearing beside the text.

Style: ultra-realistic, cinematic Hollywood sports advertisement, emotional storytelling, epic drone cinematography, dynamic camera movement, slow motion, 8K realism, realistic crowd, dramatic lighting, inspiring and unforgettable football moment.

Music mood: emotional orchestral build-up + powerful stadium energy.

Negative prompt: cartoon, animation, unrealistic faces, bad anatomy, extra limbs, distorted players, fake crowd, low quality, blurry, bad text, broken trophy.
```
