# Create a clean, modern infographic poster on a white background titled “HOW TO…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a clean, modern infographic poster on a white background titled “HOW TO CREATE AN AI CARTOON” in huge bold red uppercase typography at the top. Subtitle underneath: “a step-by-step route from idea to finished film”.

In the center, place a cute realistic orange ginger cat standing upright like a human, holding a treasure map. The cat should wear a bright red hoodie and white cargo trousers with white sneakers. Expression should look focused and adorable.

A thick winding black road/path snakes around the poster from top to bottom, connecting 5 numbered stages with beige paper-style cards pinned beside the road.

Stage 1: “Idea” with tools listed: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude
Stage 2: “Characters” with tools listed: NanoBanana, Midjourney
Stage 3: “Storyboard” with tools listed: ChatGPT, NanoBanana, Seedream
Stage 4: “Animation” with tools listed: Kling, Veo, Seedance 2, Grok
Stage 5: “Editing” with tools listed: CapCut, ElevenLabs

Add cute hand-drawn doodles around the page: clouds, mountains, signposts, trees, film icons, sketch notes, arrows. Include floating minimal tech logos/icons near related sections.

Style: playful, premium infographic, soft shadows, minimal color palette (white, black, red, beige), highly polished social-media poster aesthetic, balanced composition, modern typography, ultra detailed, realistic cat, clean layout, vertical poster format 4:5.
```
