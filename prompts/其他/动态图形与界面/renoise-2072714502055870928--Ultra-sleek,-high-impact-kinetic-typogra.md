# Ultra-sleek, high-impact kinetic typography sequence set inside a dark…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra-sleek, high-impact kinetic typography sequence set inside a dark cybernetic projection chamber. The background is a deep navy space featuring a pulsing 3D holographic grid, floating code strings, and subtle digital particle dust that reacts dynamically to the motion of the text. High-contrast specular strobe lighting is emitted directly from the holographic lasers.
0-1s: Bold, semi-transparent 3D letters spelling "FLOW" materialize inside a cylindrical blue laser projection. Volumetric streams of glowing, liquid-cyan data slam together from opposite sides of the grid, instantly assembling the letterforms with a violent digital splash that flings bright pixels outward.
1-2s: The liquid letters instantly crystallize into highly refractive, diamond-cut glass typography with visible golden-wireframe circuitry running through the core. A fast, intense green scanner line sweeps down the text as the camera does a rapid, aggressive 90-degree whip-pan orbit.
2-3s: An ultra-bright orange electric arc whip-cracks directly through the letters. The typography glitches and splits momentarily into sharp red-and-blue chromatic aberrations, shaking violently on a heavy, syncopated electronic beat and leaving holographic trails.
4-5s: The text suddenly collapses into a single microscopic point of light before instantly detonating outward in a blinding, volumetric shockwave of cyan light rings and floating data voxels that sweep across the background grid.
5-6s: The exploded voxels and particles instantly reverse their physics, snapping violently back together to reform the pristine "FLOW" geometry. A heavy cybernetic lock sound rings out as the letters solidify.
6-8s: The camera pulls back quickly to a stable front-on framing. The holographic background grid pulses once in sync as a dramatic white light sweeps across the faces of the letters, highlighting the inner circuits and casting brilliant, sharp lens flares off the glass edges.
```
