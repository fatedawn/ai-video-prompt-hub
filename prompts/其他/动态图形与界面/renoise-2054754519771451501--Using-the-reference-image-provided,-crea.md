# Using the reference image provided, create a [your favorite game] style cosplay…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Using the reference image provided, create a [your favorite game] style cosplay loading screen video — the subject environment transforms and the [your favorite game] game UI overlays on top of everything. Cinematic, dramatic. Total duration 10 seconds.
The Concept — Cosplay Not Transformation: The subject stays completely in their original form and body and face only adding [your favorite game] armor plates. What changes is everything around them and the UI overlaid on top. They are transported into a post-apocalyptic medieval ruins environment — crumbling stone walls, overgrown with dark moss and vines, ash and embers floating through the air, dramatic dark sky with rays of golden light breaking through storm clouds. The environment feels like the [your favorite game] — ancient, destroyed, beautiful and haunting. The subject stands naturally in this world as if they have always been there.
The Environment: Dark atmospheric ruins — broken stone arches, collapsed pillars, smoldering embers on the ground, dark ash floating upward like snow, dramatic God rays cutting through storm clouds above. The color palette is deep charcoal, dark navy and burnt gold — moody and cinematic. Occasional fire and embers at the bottom of the frame bleeding upward — [your favorite game] aesthetic.
The Animation — First 5 Seconds (Multi Shots): Three quick cinematic shots of the subject in the ruins environment — no UI yet, just pure atmosphere:
📸 Shot 1 (0–2 seconds) Wide establishing shot — subject standing small against the massive crumbling ruins, dramatic God rays behind them, ash floating around them, embers glowing at their feet. Cinematic and epic.
📸 Shot 2 (2–3.5 seconds) Medium shot — subject from the waist up, ruins visible behind them, embers and ash swirling around dramatically. Their expression is calm and powerful — like they belong in this world.
📸 Shot 3 (3.5–5 seconds) Close up portrait — subject's face dramatically lit by the golden God rays and ember glow from below. Dark ruins barely visible behind them. Intense, cinematic, beautiful.
The UI Overlay — Final 5 Seconds: At exactly 5 seconds the [your favorite game] main menu UI fades in over the ruins environment — the subject remains visible behind the semi-transparent menu overlay, still in the ruins:
[your favorite game] logo in the iconic thin elegant serif font fades in at the top — gold and white, with the signature rotating symbol spinning slowly beside it
Animated fire effect bleeds upward from the very bottom of the screen in deep orange and red — signature [your favorite game] menu aesthetic
Dark semi-transparent menu panel appears center screen with the classic menu list in elegant FF typography:
▶ NEW GAME
```
