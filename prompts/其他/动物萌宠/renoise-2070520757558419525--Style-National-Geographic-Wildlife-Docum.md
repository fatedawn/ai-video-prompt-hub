# [Style] National Geographic Wildlife Documentary, IMAX film quality, 4K…

## 提示词（English）

```text
[Style] National Geographic Wildlife Documentary, IMAX film quality, 4K ultra-HD, photorealistic, high-frame-rate slow-motion

[Duration] 10 seconds

[Scene] Vast golden desert at sunrise, endless rolling sand dunes sculpted by the wind, scattered weathered sandstone formations, shimmering heat haze near the horizon, fine grains of sand drifting through warm amber light beneath a dramatic sky streaked with soft clouds.

[00:00–00:04] Shot 1: The Hunter (Telephoto Close-Up, 600mm)

Super telephoto close-up: A majestic male African lion crouches behind a windswept sandstone outcrop, revealing only half of its face and powerful front paws. The warm desert breeze ripples through its thick golden mane, with tiny grains of sand clinging to the darker strands around its face. Its amber eyes narrow as the pupils contract—it has locked onto distant prey. Massive paws press silently into the soft sand, every pad and scar rendered in extraordinary detail.

[00:04–00:07] Shot 2: The Charge (High-Speed Tracking, 240fps Super Slow-Motion)

Side high-speed tracking shot: The lion explodes from cover with breathtaking power, muscles rippling beneath its tawny coat as its body stretches into a full sprint. The instant its hind legs drive into the dune, an explosive fan of golden sand erupts behind it. Super slow-motion captures the heavy mane flowing dramatically in the rushing air while its long tail sweeps gracefully for balance, every flex of muscle visible beneath the sunlit fur.

[00:07–00:10] Shot 3: Impact (Extreme Close-Up, Macro Lens)

Extreme macro close-up: The lion’s massive front paw crashes into the desert floor. Its powerful toes spread wide as thick black claws extend and dig deep into the loose sand, launching thousands of glittering grains directly toward the lens in shallow depth of field. Freeze-frame at the instant the claws bite into the dune, sunlight catching the polished curves of each claw while swirling golden dust fills the frame in cinematic slow motion.
```
