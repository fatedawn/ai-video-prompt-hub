# 0-3 sec:

## 提示词（English）

```text
0-3 sec:
Aerial view of a futuristic city at golden hour. Skyscrapers pulse gently, rising and falling in slow, synchronized rhythm. Each building inhales upward, then exhales downward.

3-6 sec:
Close up on a single tower. Its surface ripples like skin. Windows brighten slightly with each rise. The city breathes as one organism.

6-9 sec:
Sunset deepens. Lights begin to dim across every building. The dimming spreads in a coordinated wave from east to west. The city exhales slowly.

9-12 sec:
The buildings settle into their lowest position. Lights soften to a warm amber glow. The skyline appears drowsy, peaceful. A few windows flicker like closing eyes.

12-15 sec:
The city rests in stillness. One final soft pulse moves through the towers. Then complete stillness. Night settles. Fade to black.

Sound: Slow inhale, gentle exhale, dimming hum, settling silence.
```
