# Create a cinematic single continuous shot inside an enormous floating library…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a cinematic single continuous shot inside an enormous floating library above the clouds.

A young archivist walks along a narrow bridge between endless shelves suspended in open sky. The camera slowly cranes upward while drifting forward in one unbroken take, revealing books floating gently around the archivist as sunlight pours through massive broken windows.

The archivist opens a glowing book, and golden pages spiral upward like birds into the clouds.

Style: epic fantasy realism, warm sunrise, volumetric cloud light, graceful camera movement, rich wood and stone textures, soft wind, page flutter, distant bell echoes
```
