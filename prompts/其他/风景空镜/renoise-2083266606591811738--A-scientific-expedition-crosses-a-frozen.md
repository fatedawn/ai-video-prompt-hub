# A scientific expedition crosses a frozen arctic landscape toward a black alien…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A scientific expedition crosses a frozen arctic landscape toward a black alien monolith buried in the ice. Snow blows sideways under pale blue daylight, and the object absorbs light instead of reflecting it. The camera tracks the team from behind, then moves into wide shots showing the enormous scale of the monolith against the white wasteland. At the 2-second mark, the ice around it begins to levitate in perfect geometric fragments. The scientists stop moving. The sound drops into deep sub-bass. Minimalist sci-fi mystery, cold realism, cinematic 4K.
```
