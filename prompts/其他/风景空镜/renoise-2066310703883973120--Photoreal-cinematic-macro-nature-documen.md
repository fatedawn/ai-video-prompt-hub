# Photoreal cinematic macro nature documentary, mysterious dreamlike volcanic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Photoreal cinematic macro nature documentary, mysterious dreamlike volcanic night, molten lava transforming into obsidian glass in real time. Deep blacks, glowing orange lava, cool blue starlight, sharp star reflections, subtle heat shimmer, faint steam wisps. Minimum 8K quality, ultra sharp, high detail, clean textures, deep macro detail, no blur, no compression artifacts. Clean cinematic audio, low lava rumble, soft crackle, gentle night wind, no distortion.

00:00–00:02: Begin low and close over volcanic rock at night. Molten lava flows slowly across rough black terrain, glowing orange and red through cracks. Camera starts a slow push-in tracking the lava’s forward motion. Heat shimmer bends the air subtly.

00:02–00:04: The lava surface begins cooling in real time. Incandescent orange fades into deeper red, edges harden first, and thin dark crust forms across the molten surface. Keep macro perspective close, smooth dolly forward.

00:04–00:06: Cooling intensifies. The red glow dims to charcoal, cracks shrink, and the surface becomes smoother. Tiny embers remain visible beneath the darkening crust. Steam rises in faint wisps, crackling softens.

00:06–00:08: Newly formed obsidian emerges. The surface turns jet black, polished, glassy, and reflective. Camera pushes into extreme macro, revealing liquid-dark texture frozen into a mirror-like sheen.

00:08–00:10: Hold steady on the obsidian surface. Pinpoints of starlight from the night sky reflect perfectly in the black glass. The reflection is crisp and tiny, like stars trapped beneath the surface. Ambient sound becomes quieter.

00:10–00:12: Final seconds of cooling in slow motion. Last ember glow fades beneath the obsidian, steam thins and drifts upward. The surface remains glossy and black, reflecting cool blue-white starlight.

00:12–00:15: Camera pulls back slightly to reveal contrast: rough matte volcanic rock surrounding a smooth, star-flecked obsidian pool. Residual orange lava glows faintly nearby while the obsidian gleams like frozen liquid darkness. End in stillness and elemental wonder.

Camera: slow push-in, extreme macro focus, steady hold, gentle pullback reveal. Lighting: cool starlight from above, residual warm lava glow from below and edges. Negative: no people, no tools, no modern objects, no daylight, no rain, no water, no fantasy creatures, no exaggerated sparks, no broken lava physics, no muddy reflections, no shaky camera, no blur, no compression artifacts.
```
