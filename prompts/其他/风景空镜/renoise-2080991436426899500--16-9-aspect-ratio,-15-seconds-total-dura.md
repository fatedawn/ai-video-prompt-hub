# 16:9 aspect ratio, 15 seconds total duration, 6-shot cinematic sequence…

## 提示词（English）

```text

Shot 1 (0–3s):
Chaotic street-level view in downtown Manhattan, New York City, on a busy intersection with yellow taxis, black SUVs, panicked pedestrians running and screaming. A massive 200-foot-long prehistoric Titanoboa-style giant snake with dark gray-black scaly skin, glowing orange eyes, and an enormous head suddenly bursts out of thick dust clouds, smashing through cars and buildings, debris flying everywhere, high-speed dynamic camera.

Shot 2 (3–5s):
Low-angle tracking close-up as the giant snake's massive head smashes into a modern glass skyscraper, glass shattering, sparks and fire exploding, orange embers raining down, motion blur on debris, intense destruction.

Shot 3 (5–7s):
Extreme low-angle worm's-eye view climbing up the snake's huge scaly body wrapped around the skyscraper, sunlight glinting off wet scales with strong lens flares and blue horizontal flares, building reflections visible.

Shot 4 (7–9s):
Epic wide aerial shot — the enormous snake coils tightly around the top of One World Trade Center (Freedom Tower) in a bright sunny New York City skyline with river and other skyscrapers visible, black smoke rising in the distance, multiple military helicopters flying around it.

Shot 5 (9–12s):
Dramatic close-up on the snake's menacing head and upper body wrapped around the tower spire, looking down at the chaotic city below, sharp fangs visible, intense orange eyes staring forward.

Shot 6 (12–15s):
Final epic wide cinematic shot. Massive fiery explosion erupts at the top of the tower where the snake is coiled, huge orange fireball and thick black smoke billowing outward, snake head roaring, helicopters scattering, intense sun flare and god rays, high-stakes disaster movie climax.
```
