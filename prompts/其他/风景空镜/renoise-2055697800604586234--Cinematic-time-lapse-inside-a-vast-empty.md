# Cinematic time-lapse inside a vast empty Gothic cathedral at night. Towering…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic time-lapse inside a vast empty Gothic cathedral at night. Towering stone arches disappear into darkness above. Moonlight filters faintly through massive stained-glass windows, casting dim colored patterns across ancient marble floors. Dust drifts slowly through the cold air. The entire cathedral is silent except for the soft crackle of a single candle burning at the center of the enormous space.

The camera remains locked in one elegant, symmetrical composition facing the candle from a distance, emphasizing the scale and loneliness of the cathedral around it. The atmosphere feels sacred, eternal, melancholic, and slightly supernatural.

Time begins accelerating.

Hours compress into seconds.

The candle flame flickers as night shifts into dawn and back into darkness repeatedly. Faint changing light moves through the stained glass windows. Shadows crawl across pillars and pews. The tiny candle slowly melts down while the cathedral remains perfectly still and indifferent.

Eventually the flame weakens.

The final moments are stretched dramatically:

flicker
dim
flicker again
one last glow

Then total darkness.

Silence.

The screen remains black for a long beat.

From the cooling pool of melted wax, something begins to move.

No flame.

No sound.

Just the new candle standing upright where the old one died.

Perfect. Unburned. Waiting.

Cut to black.
```

```text
摄像机保持在一个优雅、对称的构图中，从远处面对蜡烛，强调教堂周围的规模和孤独感。氛围神圣、永恒、忧郁，略带超自然色彩。

时间开始加速。

数小时压缩成数秒。

蜡烛的火焰闪烁，夜晚反复变为黎明又回到黑暗。微弱的变化光线穿过彩色玻璃窗。阴影在柱子和长椅上爬行。小蜡烛慢慢融化，而教堂依然完美静止且漠然。

极致微距细节：

蜡缓缓滴落在侧面
融化的蜡形成的小河在下方汇聚
火焰在慢动作中舞动
烟雾轨迹向上卷曲
烛芯变黑并缩小
火焰的反射在抛光的石地板上闪烁

随着蜡烛变短，音乐以安静的情感强度逐渐增强。教堂感觉古老而有生命。尘埃颗粒如星星般在黑暗中移动。

最终火焰减弱。

最后的时刻被戏剧性地拉长：

闪烁
变暗
再次闪烁
最后一抹光芒

然后完全黑暗。

寂静。

屏幕长时间保持黑暗。

从冷却的融化蜡池中，某物开始移动。

非常缓慢地，不可思议地，一根全新的蜡烛从蜡中向上升起，完美光滑且未受损。运动微妙而几乎有机，如重生或复活。蜡以不自然的精确度重新塑形。

没有火焰。

没有声音。

只有新蜡烛在旧蜡烛消逝的地方直立。

完美。未燃烧。等待。

一束微弱的月光终于穿过彩色玻璃，直接落在新蜡烛上，摄像机缓缓推进。

切至黑屏。
```
