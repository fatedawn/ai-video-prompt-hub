# The reference image is a route guide placed over a real-looking Shanghai scene…

## 提示词（English）

```text
The reference image is a route guide placed over a real-looking Shanghai scene. Use the route as the motion path for an invisible FPV flying camera, not as a visual element to render. Do not show route lines, arrows, markers, cursor, text, map interface, labels, or any UI overlay in the final video.

Create a 16:9 photorealistic cinematic drone POV / FPV city-flight video. Start near the riverside route entry, fly smoothly across the Huangpu River, then approach Lujiazui from the waterfront side.

Move close to the Oriental Pearl Tower at realistic scale, preserving its structure: three supporting columns, large lower sphere, smaller upper sphere, and thin antenna. Keep Shanghai Tower, Jin Mao Tower, and Shanghai World Financial Center in the correct skyline relationship.

The camera movement should feel like real aerial FPV footage: continuous forward motion, smooth speed changes, stable horizon, natural banking during turns, no jump cuts, no chaotic spinning, no visible drone or camera rig.

End by pulling upward and outward into a wide reveal of Lujiazui, the Huangpu River, and the Shanghai skyline.
```
