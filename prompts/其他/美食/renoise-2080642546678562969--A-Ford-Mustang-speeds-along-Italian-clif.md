```text
1- A Ford Mustang speeds along Italian cliffs and launches off a ramp toward the ocean at high speed;

 while airborne, the car undergoes a high-tech, fluid metamorphosis, transforming into a speedboat that races across the water.

Fast-paced action scene, rapid motion blur.
No music, ASMR sound.

2- A speedboat, traveling at high speed, leaps out of the water and dodges a megalodon;

the boat undergoes a fluid metamorphosis, transforming into a sleek black luxury helicopter that speeds past the cliffs of the Italian coast—captured with a rapid motion-blur effect.

3- A helicopter flies rapidly toward a cliff and fluidly transforms into a futuristic black luxury bungalow perched on a white rock cliff along the Italian coast.

Sunny summer day. No music, ASMR sound.

[Hilo 2] workflow:

Usé imágenes con una estética similar.

Si le proporcionas a Seedance cada vehículo específico, el resultado será mucho mejor.

Además, simplifica el prompt de forma significativa; esto permite que el generador asigne más potencia de procesamiento a las transformaciones.

Recomiendo crear tres videos cortos separados; es mucho más efectivo que hacer un solo video largo y complejo.
---
```
