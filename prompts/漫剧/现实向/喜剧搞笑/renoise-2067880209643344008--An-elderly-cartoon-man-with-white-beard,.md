The character slides backwards from the right edge of the screen to the left edge, as if the floor is made of ice. He glides on the icy floor like an ice skater moving backwards. His face points to the right the entire time. His feet never lift off the ice — they glide flat along the surface. One foot glides back while the other balances on tiptoe, then they alternate smoothly. The motion is effortless and frictionless, like sliding on a frozen surface. Plain light grey background. Full body visible. Steady wide shot. No text, no logo. 10 seconds.
