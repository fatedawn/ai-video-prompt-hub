# Create a hilarious 15-second stop-motion / handmade puppet style animation of…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hilarious 15-second stop-motion / handmade puppet style animation of Kroaky the naughty frog.

STYLE: Realistic stop-motion / handmade clay-felt puppet style like the character sheet. Visible texture, fingerprints, soft handmade details, warm lighting, charming imperfections. Wallace & Gromit aesthetic.

SCENE WITH TIMING:

0-3 seconds:
Drone shot slowly descending over a big wide green leaf. Kroaky is sitting on top looking very hungry with a sad, droopy face. His big yellow eyes look tired and desperate.

3-6 seconds:
A juicy fly flies into the frame. Kroaky’s face instantly changes from hungry to super excited — eyes widen, big mischievous grin. He sticks out his tongue to catch the fly… but his tongue is ridiculously SHORT. It only reaches halfway and flops uselessly. Kroaky looks shocked and disappointed.

6-9 seconds:
Kroaky gets a cheeky idea. He grabs his tiny wooden slingshot, loads it with small pebbles, and aims carefully at the fly with one eye closed and tongue out in concentration.

9-12 seconds:
He shoots the slingshot! The pebble flies toward the fly… but the fly easily dodges it. The pebble misses completely.

12-15 seconds:
Camera pulls back to a wide shot. Kroaky is now standing on the leaf looking EXTREMELY GIGIL and angry. His face turns red, eyes bulging, mouth wide open in a funny frustrated scream. He stomps his feet, throws his slingshot on the ground, and kicks it in rage while the fly happily flies away. Very exaggerated and hilarious angry reaction.

MOOD: Very funny, playful, and charming. Emphasize the short tongue fail and his over-the-top gigil reaction for maximum comedy. Keep authentic stop-motion feel with slight jerky movements.

Sound: Funny cartoon sound effects — fly buzzing, tongue flop, slingshot twang, angry frog grunts, and a final funny “boing” when he kicks the slingshot.

Total length: exactly 15 seconds. High quality, detailed textures, warm natural lighting.
```
