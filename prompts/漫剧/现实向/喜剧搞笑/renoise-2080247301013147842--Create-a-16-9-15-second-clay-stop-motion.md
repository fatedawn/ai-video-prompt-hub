# Create a 16:9 15-second clay stop-motion animation style video. A cartoon…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 16:9 15-second clay stop-motion animation style video. A cartoon character wearing a red No. 9 jersey, resembling Harland, walks into a sunny meadow with a huge onion in his hands. The camera zooms in: the character uses a small shovel to dig a hole in the soil with both hands. The character plants the giant onion vertically and presses the soil down. Finally, he takes out his smartphone, winks at the camera, and makes a "Yay" gesture, completing a selfie with the planted onion. The scene is bright and has a comedic tone.
```
