## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second wide cinematic landscape video intro, 16:9, using Seedance 2.0 style motion.

A seamless one-take vintage paper-collage animation inspired by 1950s–1960s international travel posters, classic football tournament artwork, illustrated atlases, and mid-century editorial sports design.

The film celebrates the beginning of the 2026 World Cup through anthropomorphic animal football players representing nations from around the world.

Each animal wears a football uniform inspired by its national team colors, simplified into vintage travel-poster aesthetics. No official federation crests, trademarks, or copyrighted logos.

Use realistic magazine cutouts combined with hand-painted gouache illustration, torn paper edges, paper grain, halftone printing textures, pastel pencil marks, offset-print imperfections, and layered stop-motion collage animation.

The mood is optimistic, adventurous, nostalgic, international, and celebratory.

Timeline:

0–4 seconds

Warm off-white paper background.

A graceful Japanese crane wearing a blue football jersey emerges from layered paper cherry blossom scenery.

The crane dribbles a football across a stylized paper landscape inspired by Japan.

Paper clouds, travel stamps, folding fans, and illustrated map fragments animate into view.

The camera follows the crane smoothly.

4–8 seconds

The landscape unfolds like a giant vintage atlas.

An orange-uniformed Dutch lion bursts from tulip fields.

A French rooster appears from elegant paper streets.

A Brazilian jaguar sprints through tropical foliage.

A Spanish bull charges across decorative paper architecture.

Each animal briefly interacts with the football before the camera continues its journey.

8–12 seconds

The world map unfolds across the screen.

Animal football players from many nations begin converging toward a central destination.

Kangaroos, eagles, wolves, cranes, lions, jaguars, bulls, roosters, and other national animal players run, leap, glide, and sprint across layered paper continents.

Vintage directional arrows, travel labels, football graphics, and stadium illustrations appear throughout the composition.

12–15 seconds

A grand paper stadium rises from the center of the world map.

All animal players rush toward the stadium together.

Paper confetti, footballs, travel poster fragments, maps, flags, and illustrated crowd elements swirl into a giant paper vortex.

The entire composition transforms into the title:

WORLD CUP 2026

The final frame becomes a beautiful mid-century international sports poster with warm paper texture and celebratory atmosphere.

Visual Style:

Vintage travel poster, 1950s editorial illustration, handmade paper collage, gouache textures, magazine cutouts, stop-motion animation, warm off-white paper, optimistic international football celebration, premium motion-design aesthetic.
```
