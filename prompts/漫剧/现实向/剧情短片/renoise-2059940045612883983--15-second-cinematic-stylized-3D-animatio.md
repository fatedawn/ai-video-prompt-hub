# 15-second cinematic stylized 3D animation, 16:9, clean outdoor neighborhood…

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second cinematic stylized 3D animation, 16:9, clean outdoor neighborhood park setting, warm late-afternoon sunlight, emotional storytelling, wholesome tone, polished family-friendly look.

A small boy around 6 years old is playing football on a clean paved path beside a neat neighborhood park. He has soft dark hair, big brown eyes, a round expressive face, and wears a yellow t-shirt, blue shorts, white socks, and small sneakers. He kicks a black-and-white football while playing alone in a clean open area with trimmed grass, benches, trees, a low fence, and soft golden sunlight. The setting must feel bright, clean, safe, and tidy.

Nearby is a group of 4 teenage boys with a classic animated tough-kid vibe. They wear varsity jackets, hoodies, jeans, sneakers, and wristbands. One is taller and looks like the leader. Another has folded arms, one has messy hair, and one carries a skateboard. They look intimidating at first with stern expressions, confident body language, and slow approach, but they are not evil. Their behavior turns kind and protective.

0–2s:
Wide side-tracking shot. The little boy happily plays football alone on the clean park path, lightly dribbling and kicking the ball. Warm golden sunlight, soft shadows, trimmed grass, trees, benches, and a neat neighborhood background. The teen group stands nearby in the distance, casually watching.

2–4s:
The boy runs after the ball, stumbles, and trips forward. The football rolls away and lightly bumps into the shoe or leg of the tallest teen. The boy lands on the clean pavement and gets a little dust on his knee and shin. His face changes from playful to scared and embarrassed. Camera moves closer to the boy on the ground and the ball near the teen gang.

4–6s:
Dramatic medium close-ups of the teen gang noticing him. The tallest teen looks down seriously. Another teen steps forward. Use slightly tense framing and subtle motion emphasis so they feel intimidating at first. The boy looks up nervously, expecting trouble. Keep the environment clean and bright, not dark or gritty.

6–8s:
Wide shot. The teen group walks toward the boy in a slow, slightly intimidating way. The boy stays frozen, nervous, sitting or kneeling on the ground. Then the mood begins to soften as the lead teen crouches down in front of him instead of acting mean. The others gather around calmly.

8–10s:
Close emotional interaction shot. The lead teen gently reaches down and helps the little boy stand up. Another teen picks up the football and holds it nearby. One brushes a little dust off the boy’s knee and lower leg in a careful older-brother way. Their faces shift from stern to warm and reassuring.

10–12s:
Medium close-up. The lead teen gives the boy his football back and lightly taps or pats him on the head with a soft smile, like a protective big brother. The boy looks surprised, then relieved. Another teen gives a small encouraging nod. Warm sunlight glows around them.

12–14s:
Close-up of the boy’s face. His expression changes from fear and embarrassment to happiness and comfort. He smiles shyly, then more openly. The emotional payoff should feel gentle, wholesome, and sincere.

14–15s:
Final wide shot. The teen gang walks away casually, one waving slightly or glancing back. The little boy stands holding the football, smiling and watching them leave. He looks down at the ball, then back up with gratitude. Warm golden light, soft lens flare, clean park background, wholesome ending.

Style: polished cinematic stylized 3D animation, expressive facial acting, smooth character motion, clean environment, warm golden-hour lighting, soft realistic textures, emotional body language, clear continuity, no text, no dialogue.
```
