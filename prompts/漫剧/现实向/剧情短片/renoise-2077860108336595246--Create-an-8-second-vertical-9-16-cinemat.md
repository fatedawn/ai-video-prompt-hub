# Create an 8-second vertical (9:16) cinematic short animation as one continuous…

## 提示词（日本語）

```text
Create an 8-second vertical (9:16) cinematic short animation as one continuous uncut shot.

One single take only.
No cuts.
No scene transitions.
No edits.
No camera movement.
No zoom.
No pan.
No tilt.
No angle changes.
The camera remains completely fixed from beginning to end.
Everything happens naturally within the same single shot, like watching a miniature diorama come to life in real time.

Style: Cute, high-quality 3D CGI with a handcrafted miniature diorama aesthetic.

A tiny village inhabited only by small cats. The village center contains a large flower garden cared for every day by the cats.

The camera is placed in a slightly elevated wide long shot showing the entire flower garden and village. Keep this exact framing for the entire video.

Use four different kittens with fixed appearances throughout the entire video:

• White kitten
• Orange tabby kitten
• Gray kitten
• Black-and-white kitten

Keep every kitten's face, fur pattern, proportions, clothing, and appearance perfectly consistent throughout the video.

The kittens should remain mostly facing toward the camera while naturally moving.

A full-sized human never appears.
Only one life-sized human hand holding a household spray bottle enters from above.

No narration.
No dialogue.

Include:
Cute kitten meows.
Surprised kitten voices.
Happy cheering kitten voices.
Footsteps.
Water spray sounds.
Flower blooming sounds.
Soft village ambience.
Bright cheerful background music.

Do not display:
Readable text.
Japanese text.
Subtitles.
Titles.
Captions.
Signs.
Logos.
UI elements.

Story progression happens continuously without any cuts:

0–2 seconds:
The flower garden is completely wilted.
Brown flowers hang downward.
The four kittens sit sadly in front of the flower bed.
The white kitten gently touches one wilted flower with its paw, but nothing happens.

2–4 seconds:
Without changing the camera, a life-sized human hand slowly enters from above holding a spray bottle.
The kittens look upward with surprise and hope.
The hand sprays a fine mist across the entire flower garden.
Water droplets sparkle in the sunlight.

4–6 seconds:
The flowers instantly revive from front to back.
Brown flowers transform into vibrant pink, yellow, red, blue, and purple blossoms.
The soil becomes fresh and moist.
The kittens happily jump and run among the flowers while keeping their appearances perfectly consistent.

6–8 seconds:
The flower garden is now fully blooming.
The white kitten politely bows toward the human hand.
The human hand gently pets the white kitten's head once.
The other kittens happily celebrate and dance among the flowers.
End with a gentle natural fade-out during the final moments.

Lighting:
Warm natural sunlight.
Soft cinematic lighting.
Rich miniature details.
High-quality 3D CGI.
Strong miniature scale.
Stable composition.
Smooth natural animation.
Cute facial expressions.

Strict rules:

One continuous uncut shot only.
Everything happens in real time.
No cuts.
No transitions.
No montage.
No additional scenes.
No camera movement.
No character redesign.
No extra animals.
No readable text.
No Japanese text.
No logos.
No titles.
No captions.
```
