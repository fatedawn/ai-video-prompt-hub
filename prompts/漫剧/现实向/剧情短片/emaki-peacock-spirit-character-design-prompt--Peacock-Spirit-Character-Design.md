# Peacock Spirit Character Design

> Animate an existing character in full-color theatrical anime style while preserving the original design and costume; keep “retain character design” to prevent facial and accessory drift.

```text
1 male (A), male peacock spirit, long bob hair, retain character design, retain costume and decoration design, high quality, Japanese full-color anime film, dramatic lighting, cinematic, many cuts
```
