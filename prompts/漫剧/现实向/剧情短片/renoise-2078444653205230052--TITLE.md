TITLE

The Forgotten Kite

REFERENCE

Use the uploaded storyboard as the main visual reference.
Keep the girl, outfit, kite, town, stairs, ocean view, and lighting consistent in every shot.
Do not add extra characters.
Do not change the story.
Keep full bright daylight from start to finish.
Do not morph the kite or change the outfit.

SUBJECTS

Girl:
Original anime girl, around 11 years old. Short dark hair, large brown eyes, soft expressive features.
Outfit: light cream blouse, faded blue skirt, small red ribbon on wrist, white socks, brown sandals.
Her acting is curious, gentle, and quietly determined.

Kite:
Handmade diamond-shaped kite. Off-white paper, thin wooden frame, long blue ribbon tail.
One corner torn, later repaired with the girl's red wrist ribbon.
The red ribbon repair must stay visible on the kite for the rest of the film.

ENVIRONMENT

Bright coastal hill town. White walls, stone streets, flower balconies, outdoor stairways,
blue ocean, cliffside overlook. Warm afternoon sun, light wind that grows stronger toward the sea.

STYLE

Premium cinematic anime. Original character design. Painterly backgrounds.
Warm afternoon sunlight with crisp shadows. Natural readable acting.
Wind is a visible force in every shot: moving hair, ribbon, flowers, laundry, grass, and the kite.
Avoid: extra characters, object morphing, outfit changes, comedy exaggeration,
subtitles, logos, text overlays, UI elements, watermarks.

AUDIO

No music. Natural ambience only, as specified per shot.
Wind presence grows from light breeze to open sea wind across the film.

CAMERA AND FRAMING

16:9 cinematic framing. Soft depth of field.
Camera movement is motivated by the girl or the wind, never decorative.
Shadows and sunlight direction stay consistent across all shots.

SHOT-BY-SHOT TIMELINE

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:01.5) — The Discovery
- Wide shot of a sunlit white-walled street. Camera pushes in slowly toward a flower balcony
  where the torn kite hangs, its blue ribbon tail swaying in the breeze.
- Foreground flowers drift across the lens edge for depth.
- The girl enters the frame from below and stops, looking up. Her hair lifts slightly in the wind.
- SFX: light wind, distant seabirds.

SHOT 2 (00:01.5-00:03) — Freeing the Kite
- Close shot from behind the kite. Rack focus from the torn paper corner to the girl's face
  as she reaches up and gently pulls it free.
- Sunlight glows through the off-white paper, showing the wooden frame in silhouette.
- Her hands move at approximately 70% speed, careful and deliberate.
- SFX: soft paper flex, fabric rustle, wind.

SHOT 3 (00:03-00:04.5) — The Ribbon Repair
- Macro close-up. She slips the red ribbon off her wrist and ties it around the torn corner.
- Slow motion at approximately 60% speed. The ribbon ends flutter in the breeze as she pulls the knot tight.
- Shallow depth of field, sunlit bokeh from the flower balcony behind.
- Exit: the knot cinches, and on that exact beat the film cuts to motion.
- SFX: ribbon slide, soft knot pull, wind.

SHOT 4 (00:04.5-00:06.5) — Street Sprint
- Side tracking shot with gentle handheld energy. She runs through narrow stone streets holding the kite.
- Footage accelerates from about 85% to full speed across the shot.
- Sunlight strobes across her as she passes through alternating bands of light and building shadow.
- The kite's blue ribbon tail streams behind her with slight motion blur.
- SFX: quick footsteps on stone, breeze, ribbon flutter.

SHOT 5 (00:06.5-00:08) — The Wind Catches
- Low angle at the stairway overlooking the sea. She climbs into frame against open blue sky.
- The wind suddenly grabs the kite: it yanks upward in a brief slow-motion snap,
  approximately 40% speed for half a second, string pulling taut, her arm jerking up with it.
- Then time returns to normal speed as she grips the string and smiles.
- SFX: strong gust, sharp kite flutter, string tension.

SHOT 6 (00:08-00:09.5) — Cliff Run
- Wide tracking shot. She runs along the cliffside path, the kite airborne behind her,
  ocean filling the background.
- Camera tracks with her, then cranes upward at the end of the shot, letting the kite rise
  through the frame.
- Grass and flowers bend in waves under the wind.
- SFX: footsteps, open wind, kite tension.

SHOT 7 (00:09.5-00:11) — The Release
- Medium shot from behind her shoulder. She lets string slide through her fingers.
- Slow motion, approximately 50% speed. The kite climbs away over the water,
  red ribbon repair catching the sunlight, blue tail drawing a curve against the sky.
- Her hair and skirt stream in the wind at the frame's edge.
- SFX: string sliding, kite fluttering smaller, sea wind.

SHOT 8 (00:11-00:13) — Ascent With the Kite (SIGNATURE MOMENT)
- The camera leaves the girl and flies upward WITH the kite, tracking alongside it as it climbs.
- The blue ribbon tail ripples just ahead of the lens with slight motion blur.
- As the camera rises, the view opens up: the stairway, the white town, the flower balconies,
  and the whole coastline revealing themselves below in one continuous ascent.
- The girl becomes a small figure on the cliff, string glinting in the sun.
- SFX: rushing wind, ribbon ripple, seabirds closer now.

SHOT 9 (00:13-00:15) — The Hold
- Final extreme wide shot, camera completely static. The girl stands on the cliff above
  the seaside town, kite flying high and steady over the ocean.
- All the film's motion resolves: only the kite drifting, the ribbon tail waving,
  and her hair moving gently.
- Warm afternoon light across the whole frame. Hold to the end.
- SFX: sea breeze settling, distant seabirds, soft kite flutter.

ENERGY ARC

Act 1 (0-4.5s): Care. Small, close, delicate. The wind is only a whisper.
Act 2 (4.5-9.5s): Momentum. Running, climbing, the wind takes over as the driving force.
Act 3 (9.5-15s): Flight. The camera leaves the ground with the kite, then lands on total stillness.

- SFX: pasos, viento abierto, tensión de la cometa.

- As mãos dela se movem a aproximadamente 70% da velocidade, com cuidado e intenção.
- SFX: leve flexão do papel, farfalhar do tecido, vento.

CENA 3 (00:03-00:04.5) — O Reparo com a Fita
- Close macro. Ela tira a fita vermelha do pulso e a amarra no canto rasgado.
- Câmera lenta a aproximadamente 60% da velocidade. As pontas da fita tremulam na brisa enquanto ela aperta o nó.
- Profundidade de campo rasa, bokeh iluminado pelo sol vindo da varanda florida ao fundo.
- Saída: o nó se ajusta, e exatamente nesse beat o filme corta para movimento.
- SFX: deslizamento da fita, aperto suave do nó, vento.

CENA 4 (00:04.5-00:06.5) — Corrida pela Rua
- Plano lateral em tracking com leve energia de câmera na mão. Ela corre pelas ruas estreitas de pedra segurando a pipa.
- A filmagem acelera de cerca de 85% até a velocidade total ao longo da cena.
- A luz do sol pisca sobre ela enquanto passa por faixas alternadas de luz e sombra dos prédios.
- A cauda de fita azul da pipa se estende atrás dela com leve motion blur.
- SFX: passos rápidos na pedra, brisa, tremular da fita.

CENA 5 (00:06.5-00:08) — O Vento Pega
- Ângulo baixo na escadaria com vista para o mar. Ela sobe para o quadro contra o céu azul aberto.
- O vento de repente agarra a pipa: ela puxa para cima em um breve estalo em câmera lenta,
  aproximadamente 40% da velocidade por meio segundo, a linha ficando tensa, o braço dela sendo puxado junto.
- Depois o tempo volta à velocidade normal enquanto ela segura a linha e sorri.
- SFX: rajada forte, tremular agudo da pipa, tensão da linha.

CENA 6 (00:08-00:09.5) — Corrida na Encosta
- Plano geral em tracking. Ela corre ao longo do caminho na encosta, a pipa voando atrás dela,
  o oceano preenchendo o fundo.
- A câmera acompanha o movimento dela e depois sobe em crane no final da cena, deixando a pipa subir
  pelo quadro.
- Grama e flores se curvam em ondas sob o vento.
- SFX: passos, vento aberto, tensão da pipa.

CENA 7 (00:09.5-00:11) — A Soltura
- Plano médio por trás do ombro dela. Ela deixa a linha escorregar pelos dedos.
- Câmera lenta, aproximadamente 50% da velocidade. A pipa sobe sobre a água,
  o reparo com a fita vermelha captando a luz do sol, a cauda azul desenhando uma curva contra o céu.
- O cabelo e a saia dela se estendem ao vento na borda do quadro.
- SFX: linha deslizando, tremular menor da pipa, vento do mar.

CENA 8 (00:11-00:13) — Ascensão com a Pipa (MOMENTO ASSINATURA)
- A câmera deixa a menina e sobe JUNTO com a pipa, acompanhando-a enquanto ela ganha altura.
- A cauda de fita azul ondula logo à frente da lente com leve motion blur.
- À medida que a câmera sobe, a vista se abre: a escadaria, a cidade branca, as varandas floridas,
  e toda a costa se revelam abaixo em uma única ascensão contínua.
- A menina vira uma pequena figura no penhasco, com a linha brilhando ao sol.
- SFX: vento cortante, ondulação da fita, aves marinhas mais próximas agora.

CENA 9 (00:13-00:15) — O Plano Final
- Plano final extremamente aberto, câmera completamente estática. A menina está no penhasco acima
  da cidade litorânea, a pipa voando alta e firme sobre o oceano.
- Todo o movimento do filme se resolve: apenas a pipa derivando, a cauda de fita ondulando,
  e o cabelo dela se movendo suavemente.
- Luz quente da tarde em todo o quadro. Segure até o fim.
- SFX: brisa do mar se acalmando, aves marinhas distantes, leve tremular da pipa.

ARCO DE ENERGIA

Ato 1 (0-4.5s): Cuidado. Pequeno, próximo, delicado. O vento é apenas um sussurro.
Ato 2 (4.5-9.5s): Impulso. Corrida, subida, o vento assume o papel de força motriz.
Ato 3 (9.5-15s): Voo. A câmera deixa o chão com a pipa e então pousa em total imobilidade.

标题

16:9 电影感构图。柔和景深。
镜头运动必须由女孩或风驱动，绝不做装饰性运动。

- 音效：强风阵、风筝急促抖动声、线绳绷紧声。

- 音效：脚步声、开阔风声、风筝拉力声。

- 她的头发和裙摆在画面边缘随风飘动。
- 音效：线绳滑动声、风筝更轻的抖动声、海风声。

镜头8 (00:11-00:13) — 与风筝一同升空（标志性时刻）

- 蓝色丝带尾巴在镜头前方轻轻翻卷，带有轻微运动模糊。
- 随着镜头上升，视野逐渐打开：楼梯、白色小镇、花团阳台，
