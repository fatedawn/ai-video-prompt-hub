# Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second extreme speed street skating video featuring…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second extreme speed street skating video featuring the provided character “Zoe”. Maintain strict identity and outfit consistency.

CHARACTER (STRICT LOCK)

Teen female street skater, energetic and fearless.
Yellow-blue jacket, pink shorts, teal inline skates, backpack bouncing with motion.
Confident expression, sharp focus, aggressive skating posture.
No outfit changes across entire sequence.

STYLE

Ultra-fast paced cinematic sports anime × street realism hybrid
High shutter motion blur + sharp impact frames
Aggressive handheld chase cam + drone dive transitions
Vibrant neon street lighting + harsh sunlight contrast
Adrenaline, chaos, momentum-driven editing feel

SETTING

Dense urban skate district
Concrete ramps, stairs, rails, overpasses, moving traffic intersections
Graffiti walls + reflective glass buildings
Wind dust + wheel sparks + motion streaks emphasized

CAMERA LANGUAGE (IMPORTANT)
ultra low-angle tracking at wheel level
fast whip pan transitions between obstacles
drone dive following jumps
fisheye lens for airtime tricks
sudden push-in zoom during landings
unstable handheld chase camera feel (high intensity)
⏱️ TIMELINE (15 SECONDS — VERY FAST RHYTHM)

0s - 2s
Instant drop-in. She launches from a ramp into frame at full speed. Camera immediately chases from behind at wheel level. Tires screech on concrete.

2s - 5s
Side tracking whip-pan. She threads through a narrow alley at extreme speed, performs a fast ollie over stacked obstacles without slowing down.

5s - 8s
Cut-like drone follow (continuous motion feel). She jumps a staircase gap, mid-air kickflip, landing into immediate grind on rail without pause.

8s - 11s
Fisheye close-up burst. Wheel sparks on metal rail grind. Sudden acceleration out of grind into downhill street section. Motion blur intensifies heavily.

11s - 13s
Chaos intersection moment. She crosses traffic gap at full speed, dodging cars, performs aerial spin over curb barrier.

13s - 15s
Final high-speed chase. Front-facing camera runs backward as she skates directly toward lens → last second pass-by rush, jacket flares, wind blast, camera shakes violently, exit frame at extreme speed.

OUTPUT RULES
MUST feel like continuous shot (no visible cuts)
Extreme acceleration at all times (no idle moments)
High difficulty but believable skate physics
Motion blur + speed streaks emphasized
No text, no subtitles, no logos
End with motion continuation, NOT freeze frame
```
