Use the attached ANIMAL FOOTBALL director visual bible as the main reference for a cinematic animated animal World Cup football video.

Begin with a short preparation beat: blue-and-gold boots near the black-and-white football, then the cheetah steps into the light and raises his hand to the crowd.

 Transition into the finale with close bodies, bright stadium light, and gold confetti passing across the camera. As the gold veil clears, reveal a grounded blue-and-gold championship parade float inside the same stadium, with visible wheels, rails, lights, stairs, mounted golden cup, flags, confetti, fireworks, teammates celebrating on different levels, and a cheering crowd.

Use cinematic sports-film camera language, real athletic motion, natural weight shifts, crowd parallax, moving flags, tasteful motion blur, and a grand joyful championship feeling.
