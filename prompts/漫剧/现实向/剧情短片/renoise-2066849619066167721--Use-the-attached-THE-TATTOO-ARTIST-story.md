# Use the attached THE TATTOO ARTIST storyboard as exact visual reference. Create…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the attached THE TATTOO ARTIST storyboard as exact visual reference. Create a 12-second 16:9 Pixar 3D animated sequence following all 8 shots exactly. Same young male tattoo artist — sleeve tattoos on both arms, black t-shirt, black gloves, warm focused expression — small intimate tattoo studio, artwork covering every wall, warm neon sign glowing, chrome machine throughout. Tattoo being created on the customer’s upper arm every shot.

Shot sequence:

1.Wide shot. Artist stands in his studio, neon sign glowing behind him, artwork on every wall, chrome machine gleaming. He snaps on his black gloves with a sharp confident snap and looks directly at the camera with a calm focused smile.
2.Close-up. Artist pressing transfer paper against the customer’s upper arm — peeling it away slowly to reveal crisp blue and purple stencil lines. Simple clean outline design. The blueprint established.
3.Close-up. Artist holds one single tattoo machine in his gloved hand. With his other hand he tips a small ink bottle and pours a few drops of black ink into a tiny disposable ink cap on the workstation. He dips just the tip of the single machine into the ink cap. One machine only, one ink cap, nothing else.
4.Close-up. The single tattoo machine held in one gloved hand working over the blue stencil lines on the upper arm — vivid black ink being deposited in clean precise lines, replacing the blue stencil with bold black. Blue lines visible ahead, clean black lines completed behind. One machine. One arm.
5.Tight close-up side angle. Artist’s gloved hand guiding the single machine across the upper arm — more black lines building and connecting across the design. Still partially blue ahead, black where completed. Work in progress.
6.Overhead locked shot looking straight down at the upper arm. Design taking shape from above — mix of completed black lines and remaining blue stencil lines. Artist’s hand and machine visible from above, moving methodically through the design. Still in progress.
7.Close-up. Artist’s gloved hand pressing a clean white cloth across the upper arm in one confident stroke — wiping away excess ink, bold clean black lines revealed perfectly beneath. All blue completely replaced by permanent black. The transformation visible.
8.Wide warm shot. Customer looks at their upper arm in a mirror held by the artist. Sees the finished tattoo design for the first time. Eyes wide, slow smile, hand reaches toward it. Artist beside them arms crossed, deeply satisfied proud smile.

Camera: Wide for opener and reveal, close-up for stencil and ink setup, close-up for machine working over stencil lines, tight side angle for close-up draw, overhead locked for overhead draw, close-up for the wipe, wide warm for the mirror reveal.

Style: Warm moody studio lighting throughout. Neon sign background, blue stencil lines on upper arm, bold black ink replacing them gradually, chrome machine, black gloves, white cloth, artwork on walls. Pixar CGI vivid and warm.

Sound: Energetic rock or alternative music playing from the very first frame to the very last — the kind always playing in a real tattoo studio. Upbeat, raw, full of energy. Continuous, no breaks. Natural sounds underneath — glove snap, transfer paper removed, ink poured into cap, machine buzzing as it works through the design, cloth wiping, customer’s quiet satisfied exhale at the reveal. Music never stops.

Goal: 12 seconds. One artist. One design. One moment that lasts forever. Bold, precise, and deeply moving to watch.
```
