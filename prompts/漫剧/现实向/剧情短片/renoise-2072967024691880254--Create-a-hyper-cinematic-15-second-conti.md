# Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous extreme skateboarding video using…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous extreme skateboarding video using Seedance 2.0.
Use the provided character reference image as strict identity + outfit reference.
No deviation in face, proportions, clothing, backpack, helmet/cap, color palette, or skateboard design.

CHARACTER (STRICT LOCK – DO NOT CHANGE)

Teen female street skateboarder with confident, energetic attitude.
Wears:

neon yellow cap
pink-orange street jacket with teal accents
coral pink shorts
colorful knee pads and elbow pads
high-top skate shoes (pink/yellow/teal mix)
large street backpack with smiley-face design
graffiti-style skateboard

She must remain identical across all frames.
No outfit changes, no hairstyle changes, no design drift.

STYLE

High-energy cinematic street sports animation × stylized anime realism
Vibrant color grading (pink, yellow, cyan dominant palette)
Fast motion blur + sharp impact frames
Dynamic handheld chase camera + drone flyover transitions
Skate video aesthetic mixed with commercial-grade cinematic polish

ENVIRONMENT

Colorful urban street district during golden hour
Walls covered in graffiti murals and street art
Low-rise buildings with vivid painted facades
Skate-friendly architecture: stairs, rails, ledges, ramps
Warm sunlight + colorful bounce reflections from walls
Crowded but blurred pedestrians for depth

CAMERA LANGUAGE (VERY IMPORTANT)
ultra low-angle wheel-follow tracking shots
fast whip pan transitions between obstacles
fisheye lens for aerial tricks
overhead drone spiral for combo sequences
close-up grind sparks slow-motion inserts
front-facing chase cam during final sprint
slight handheld shake for speed realism

TIMELINE (15 SECONDS – HIGH SPEED TRICK FLOW)

0s - 3s
Wide establishing shot of colorful graffiti street district.
She drops in from a small ramp into frame at high speed. Camera immediately locks into wheel-level chase.

3s - 6s
Low-angle tracking shot through narrow street corridor.
She performs a fast ollie over stacked street obstacles and painted curb edges, backpack bouncing violently with motion.

6s - 9s
Side whip-pan transition.
She hits a rail and performs a clean grind → immediate kickflip dismount into ramp landing. Motion blur intensifies.

9s - 12s
Overhead drone spiral shot.
She chains combo: manual balance → jump over stair gap → mid-air spin trick. Graffiti walls streak beneath her.

12s - 15s
Front-facing chase cam.
She accelerates directly toward camera, performs final high-difficulty aerial trick over lens → lands and passes through frame at extreme speed.
Camera shakes violently, motion continues forward (no freeze).

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
Continuous motion illusion (no visible cuts)
High trick density (every 2–3 seconds action change)
Strict character consistency from reference image
Bright saturated “street color explosion” palette
No text, no subtitles, no logos
No freeze frame ending — must exit in motion
Emphasis on speed, flow, and skate realism
```

---
Seedance 2.0を使用して、ハイパーシネマティックな15秒間の連続エクストリームスケートボードビデオを作成します。

自信に満ちたエネルギッシュな態度のティーンの女性ストリートスケートボーダー。
着用：

ネオンイエローのキャップ
ティールのアクセントがあるピンクオレンジのストリートジャケット
コーラルピンクのショーツ
カラフルなニーパッドとエルボーパッド
ハイトップスケートシューズ（ピンク/イエロー/ティールのミックス）
スマイリーフェイスデザインの大きなストリートバックパック

衣装の変更、ヘアスタイルの変更、デザインのドリフトはありません。

スタイル

ハイエナジーなシネマティックストリートスポーツアニメーション × スタイライズドアニメリアリズム
鮮やかなカラーグレーディング（ピンク、イエロー、シアンの支配的なパレット）
高速モーションブラー + 鋭いインパクトフレーム
ダイナミックなハンドヘルドチェイスカメラ + ドローンのフライオーバーのトランジション
スケートビデオの美学と商業グレードのシネマティックな磨きのミックス

環境

ゴールデンアワーのカラフルな都市ストリート地区
グラフィティの壁画とストリートアートで覆われた壁
鮮やかに塗られたファサードの低層ビル
スケートに適した建築：階段、レール、レッジ、ランプ
暖かい日差し + 壁からのカラフルな反射
深みのある混雑したがぼやけた歩行者

カメラ言語（非常に重要）
超低角度のホイールフォロートラッキングショット
障害物間の高速ウィップパントランジション
空中トリック用のフィッシュアイレンズ
コンボシーケンス用のオーバーヘッドドローンスパイラル
クローズアップのグラインドスパークスローモーションインサート

タイムライン（15秒 – 高速トリックフロー）

0秒 - 3秒

3秒 - 6秒
狭いストリートコリドーを通る低角度のトラッキングショット。
彼女は積み重ねられたストリート障害物と塗られた縁石を高速でオーリーし、バックパックが激しく動きます。

6秒 - 9秒

skate estilo grafite

Câmera de perseguição dinâmica de mão + transições de sobrevoo de drone
Estética de vídeo de skate misturada com polimento cinematográfico de nível comercial

Distrito urbano colorido durante a hora dourada
Paredes cobertas de murais de grafite e arte de rua
Prédios baixos com fachadas pintadas vivas
Arquitetura amigável para skate: escadas, corrimãos, bordas, rampas

Pedestres lotados, mas desfocados para profundidade

planos de rastreamento ultra baixo nível da roda
transições rápidas de whip pan entre obstáculos
lente olho de peixe para truques aéreos
espiral de drone aérea para sequências de combo
inserções em câmera lenta de faíscas de grind em close-up
câmera de perseguição frontal durante a corrida final
leve tremor de mão para realismo de velocidade

0s - 3s

3s - 6s
Plano de rastreamento em ângulo baixo através de corredor estreito de rua. Ela executa um ollie rápido sobre obstáculos empilhados na rua e bordas de meio-fio pintadas, mochila balançando violentamente com o movimento.

6s - 9s
Transição de whip-pan lateral. Ela atinge um corrimão e realiza um grind limpo → desmonta com um kickflip imediato para pouso na rampa. O desfoque de movimento se intensifica.

9s - 12s
Plano espiral de drone aéreo. Ela encadeia combo: equilíbrio manual → salto sobre vão de escada → truque de giro no ar. Paredes de grafite passam rapidamente abaixo dela.

12s - 15s
Câmera de perseguição frontal. Ela acelera diretamente em direção à câmera, executa um truque aéreo de alta dificuldade sobre a lente → pousa e passa pelo quadro em velocidade extrema. A câmera treme violentamente, o movimento continua para frente (sem congelamento).

REQUISITOS DE SAÍDA
Ilusão de movimento contínuo (sem cortes visíveis)
Alta densidade de truques (mudança de ação a cada 2–3 segundos)
Consistência estrita do personagem a partir da imagem de referência
Paleta de cores saturadas e brilhantes “explosão de cores de rua”
Sem texto, sem legendas, sem logos
Sem final de quadro congelado — deve sair em movimento
Ênfase na velocidade, fluxo e realismo do skate
