# Generate a zero gravity level, without a floor. The background will be a flat…

```text
Generate a zero gravity level, without a floor. The background will be a flat blue color. Create 20 asteroids formed by basic shapes, with red color, that float in space. They will make rotation movements on themselves very slowly and will also move slightly in a random direction in space. Create also a dummy with basic shapes of green color, playable, that also has zero gravity. This dummy will also float and will not brake automatically when handled, in such a way that it maintains an inertial movement when it moves in one direction. The playable character will be located right in front of the asteroid cluster.
```

```text
[Hilo 4] 4. Generar una máscara de personaje en After Effects
Seedance tiene un problema: cuando le das información precisa tiende a adherirse a ella, y limita sus capacidades de imaginación.

Seedance es una herramienta diseñada para tener alta adherencia y cohesión. Esto provoca que, cuando le damos un muñeco "rígido" en una escena y le pedimos que lo reemplace, Seedance imitará la posición original del muñeco en el video.

Esto también ocurre con imágenes: si proporcionas fotogramas completamente fijos, seedance tendrá dificultades para imaginar cómo conectar los pasos intermedios de una imagen a otra.

¿Qué hacer?

OMITIR INFORMACIÓN. Generaremos un sólido con un cuadrado verde en After Effects que haga una máscara del personaje. De esta manera mantendremos las proporciones y posición de nuestro personaje, pero evitaremos dar información sobre los huesos, la estructura corporal y la pose, permitiendo así que seedance use todo su poder para imaginar la animación del personaje.

SINGLE SHOT, no multicut.

The astronaut does not perform effusive or agitated movements. Remains floating with very slow movements, almost suspended, maintaining a sensation of passive drift in space. The general sensation must be of an astronaut lost and floating adrift, but expressed with a minimal, slow and credible performance.

Very important: maintain the same relative position in frame, scale in shot and relationship with the camera indicated by the green square, but substitute that marker with the complete astronaut. The green square is only a location guide and must disappear completely in the final render.

Important: backlit illumination, not frontlit. An intense light from the background illuminates the scene, creating silhouettes, strong rim light on the asteroids and the astronaut, dramatic spatial glow and cinematic contrast.

Photorealistic style, cinematic, with a background of stars, spatial depth and credible flotation movement.
```
