# Use the attached THE KFC FACTORY storyboard image as the exact visual…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the attached THE KFC FACTORY storyboard image as the exact visual reference. Create a 12-second 16:9 Pixar-style animated KFC factory sequence. Keep the same young male worker in a red KFC uniform and black cap throughout. Preserve the giant fried chicken factory, conveyor belts, glowing fryers, stainless steel prep stations, bright industrial lighting, warm fryer glow, and vivid KFC reds.

8 shots total, ~1.5 seconds each, following the storyboard exactly.

1. Wide cinematic opener — massive KFC factory buzzing with movement, conveyor belts carrying raw chicken trays, steam rising from industrial fryers. Worker steps forward holding a red bucket confidently.

2. Extreme close-up slow motion — raw chicken tossed through seasoned flour, huge cloud of spices and flour exploding upward under bright lights.

3. Medium shot — worker rapidly breading chicken by hand at a stainless steel station while conveyors move nonstop beside him.

4. Overhead fryer shot — chicken lowered into bubbling industrial fryers, oil exploding with golden bubbles and steam, intense orange glow everywhere.

5. Side tracking shot — crispy fried chicken moving along a conveyor belt beneath warming lamps, steam rising gently.

6. Close-up — worker stacking crispy chicken into red-and-white buckets, crumbs falling naturally, heavy steam visible.

7. Overhead beauty shot — finished KFC feast with chicken bucket, fries, burgers, sauces, and cold drinks. Slow push inward while steam rises.

8. Wider emotional shot — worker seated at a break table, taking one crunchy bite of chicken, eyes closing slowly with satisfaction.

Style: Pixar CGI realism, bright industrial lighting, golden crispy textures, vivid reds, glossy stainless steel reflections, heavy steam atmosphere, smooth cinematic cuts.

Sound: Energetic commercial music plays continuously throughout. Layer fryer bubbling, conveyor hums, steam hissing, crunchy bites, and kitchen ambience underneath.

Goal: A hyper-satisfying 12-second fried chicken journey from raw seasoning to the final crunchy bite.
```
