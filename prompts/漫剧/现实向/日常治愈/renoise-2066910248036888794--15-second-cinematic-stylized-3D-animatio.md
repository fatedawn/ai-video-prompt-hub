# 15-second cinematic stylized 3D animation, 16:9, warm golden-hour neighborhood…

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second cinematic stylized 3D animation, 16:9, warm golden-hour neighborhood park, polished family-friendly look, emotional storytelling, no text, no dialogue. A 6-year-old boy with soft dark hair, big brown eyes, yellow T-shirt, blue shorts, white socks, and sneakers plays football alone on a clean paved park path. Nearby are four teenage boys with tough-kid animated styling. They look serious at first, but are kind and protective.

0–2s: Wide shot. Boy happily dribbles and kicks football along the path. Show trimmed grass, benches, trees, low fence, golden light. Teens stand in the distance, watching with calm stern faces. 2–4s: Boy kicks harder, runs after the ball, lightly trips, and falls. Football rolls forward and taps the tallest teen’s shoe. Boy gets dust on his knee and looks embarrassed. Camera moves from rolling ball to his worried face.

4–6s: Medium dramatic shot. Teens notice him and slowly walk toward him with serious faces. Boy sits frozen on the ground, nervous and expecting trouble. Keep tension through framing and expressions only, no darkness, violence, or aggression. 6–8s: Lead teen reaches him and crouches down instead of bullying him. His expression softens. Other teens stand calmly nearby like a protective circle.

8–10s: Close emotional shot. Lead teen gently helps the boy stand. Another teen brushes dust from his knee and lower leg. Their faces shift from stern to warm and reassuring. 10–12s: One teen returns the football. Lead teen gives a gentle big-brother-style head pat. Boy’s fear turns into relief.

12–14s: Close-up of boy holding football. He smiles shyly, then warmly, realizing they helped him. Soft golden light, emotional calm. 14–15s: Final wide shot. Teens walk away casually while boy watches gratefully, holding football close. One teen slightly looks back or waves. End wholesome: kindness before judgment.
```
