# Pixar-Style Muffin People Life Cycle Video Prompt

> A detailed, narrative prompt for Grok Imagine to create a warm, Pixar-style animation about little muffin-people stumbling through a vibrant playground, focusing on a time-lapse sequence of a small muffin touching a hot stove and later, as an older muffin, touching it calmly.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Warm Pixar-style animation. Vibrant messy Earth playground. Little muffin-people with legs stumble through life. A small muffin touches a glowing hot stove and screams in pain. Time-lapse: the same muffin grows older, touches the stove again, calmly...
```
