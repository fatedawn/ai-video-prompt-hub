# 15-second animated children’s storybook.

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second animated children’s storybook.

A handcrafted illustrated book rests on a wooden table.
The cover reads:
“Tiny Moments, Big Magic”

Soft watercolor and ink illustration style.
Warm cream paper texture.
Muted greens and orange accents.

0–3s:
The closed book rests on the table.
Small dust particles move in sunlight.
Slow gentle camera push-in.

3–6s:
The book slowly opens.
Pages turn naturally.
The first illustrated scene appears:
children playing in water.
Soft ripples and tiny movement.

6–9s:
Another page turn.
A forest illustration appears.
Leaves move slowly.
Very soft parallax effect.

9–12s:
Another page turn.
Children run through flowers in a meadow.
Grass and flowers sway gently.

12–15s:
Final page turn.
The book slowly closes halfway.
Warm light fills the scene.

Style:
- preserve original hand-drawn illustration style
- no realism
- no 3D rendering
- soft watercolor texture
- clean page turns
- cozy storybook feeling

Audio:
soft piano music
light page turning sounds
no voices
```

一本手工绘制的插画书放在木桌上。

“小瞬间，大魔法”

柔和的水彩和墨水插画风格。
温暖的奶油色纸张质感。
柔和的绿色和橙色点缀。

书缓缓合上到一半。
