# Ultra-realistic stop-motion animation.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra-realistic stop-motion animation.

A cute handmade crocheted girl doll with dark brown hair stands inside a cozy miniature kitchen, preparing sunny-side-up eggs. Every single object in the scene is made entirely from crocheted yarn and knitted wool, including the doll, egg tray, egg, frying pan, plate, spoon, knife, fork, table, and all kitchen accessories. Nothing in the scene is made from real materials.

Sequence:

• The doll gently cracks a single crocheted egg with both hands into a crocheted frying pan.
• The egg smoothly transforms into a crocheted sunny-side-up egg while preserving the same knitted wool and crochet texture.

CUT

• Close-up shot: the doll carefully lifts the sunny-side-up egg with a crocheted spatula and places it onto a white crocheted plate.

CUT

• The doll sits at the table and uses a crocheted knife and fork to cut the egg into small bite-sized pieces.
• She naturally opens her mouth and eats each piece one by one.
• Every piece completely disappears once it enters her mouth.
• After finishing the meal, she smiles happily.

Animation Style:

Smooth, charming stop-motion animation with subtle handcrafted imperfections, realistic fabric movement, gentle yarn compression during contact, slight head tilts, natural arm movements, and accurate hand interaction with every object.

Camera:

Cinematic medium shot with a very slow push-in toward the character, shallow depth of field, macro product-style focus, 85mm lens.

Lighting:

Soft, warm morning sunlight entering through the window, creating a cozy cinematic kitchen atmosphere with gentle shadows and premium color grading.

Materials:

Extremely detailed crochet stitches, knitted wool fibers, soft yarn texture, handcrafted appearance, and perfectly consistent crochet styling across every object throughout the entire animation.

Quality:

Ultra-photorealistic stop-motion animation, exceptionally clean visuals, 8K resolution, Pixar-quality craftsmanship, perfectly consistent character appearance, no flickering, no morphing, no extra limbs, no texture changes, and no deformation of any objects during movement.
```
