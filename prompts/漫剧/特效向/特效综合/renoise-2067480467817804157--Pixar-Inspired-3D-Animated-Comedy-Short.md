Create a colorful Pixar-style 3D animated comedy set in a charming European-style town square during a warm summer afternoon. The boy races through busy streets, crowded markets, colorful flower-lined alleys, outdoor cafés, and fountains while desperately protecting the giant cone. The vendor follows close behind, weaving through pedestrians, fruit stands, bicycles, and pigeons.

The chase grows increasingly chaotic and comedic. The boy jumps over a fountain, squeezes through crowds, and celebrates every narrow escape. People stop to watch, cheer, and laugh as the ridiculous chase unfolds. Meanwhile, the hot summer sun shines overhead.

Finally, the entire ice cream melts all over him.

Chocolate, strawberry, and vanilla drip everywhere.

The boy stops.

Defeated.

All that's left is an empty cone.

Moments later, the exhausted vendor finally catches up. The boy prepares for trouble. Instead, the vendor smiles kindly. He reaches into the cart and hands the boy a fresh ice cream cone.

The boy looks shocked.

Then grateful.

The music shifts from frantic comedy to warm friendship.

Both sit together beside the town fountain enjoying ice cream while the sunset paints the square golden. Nearby pigeons gather, children laugh, and the town returns to normal.

Final cinematic shot: the boy and vendor sitting beside the fountain, sharing ice cream and laughter as the sun sets over the colorful town.

On-screen text: "Sometimes stealing the shortcut only melts the reward."
