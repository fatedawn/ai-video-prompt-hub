# Create a 15-second cinematic comedy-horror video (16:9) featuring a realistic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second cinematic comedy-horror video (16:9) featuring a realistic 3D 👻 emoji ghost.

CUT A (0–5s) — THE PROUD GHOST

Inside a haunted mansion corridor with flickering candles and dusty chandeliers, the 👻 ghost floats in confidently, cracks his knuckles, rattles a chandelier, blows out all candles in one puff, then flexes at his reflection in a dusty mirror.

Dialogue: “Woooooo~!”

BGM: Creepy pipe organ with a cocky jazz flourish.

CUT B (5–10s) — THE UNBOTHERED HUMAN

The mansion doors slam open. The woman enters confidently. The ghost puffs himself up and unleashes a terrifying roar. She completely ignores him, calmly applies red lipstick, and finishes with a confident pout while maintaining eye contact.

Dialogue: Ghost: “BOOOOOO—!” fading into a confused squeak.

BGM: Horror sting → instant silence → low tension cello.

CUT C (10–15s) — THE KISS & LAUNCH

The woman takes one step forward. The ghost trembles in panic. She kisses him on the LEFT cheek. A bright red lipstick mark appears on the SAME LEFT cheek. The ghost freezes, then rockets through the ceiling like a missile surrounded by ⛈️ storm energy.

Dialogue: Loud “MWAH!” then Ghost: “eeeEEEEE—!”

BGM: Kiss sound → brief silence → whoosh + cymbal crash + bass drop.

CUT D (15–20s) — SKY IMPACT

Night sky. The lipstick-marked 👻 ghost shoots upward and crashes into a giant storm cloud. The cloud shakes and opens two huge cartoon eyes.

Dialogue:
Ghost: “AAAAAHHH!”
Cloud: “BWUH?!”
BGM: Fast whoosh → thunder BOOM → orchestral cymbal crash.
CUT E (20–25s) — AWKWARD CLOUD

Inside the storm cloud, the ghost and the cloud stare at each other. Both are terrified. The ghost slowly backs away.

Dialogue:
Ghost: “…hello?”
Cloud responds with a tiny nervous thunder rumble.

BGM: Awkward pizzicato strings with long pauses.

CUT F (25–30s) — THE REAL WINNER
Back inside the mansion, the woman relaxes in an armchair, legs crossed, tea in hand. Outside the window, ghost-shaped lightning flickers in the storm. She calmly sips tea, sets the cup down, and says:
“Still not impressed.”

End on a freeze-frame.
```

豪宅大门砰地打开。女人自信地走进来。幽灵鼓起勇气，发出可怕的咆哮。她完全无视他，冷静地涂上红色口红，保持眼神接触，最后自信地撅嘴。

对话：幽灵：“BOOOOOO—!” 渐渐变成困惑的尖叫。

背景音乐：恐怖音效 → 瞬间静音 → 低沉紧张的大提琴。

CUT C (10–15s) — 吻与发射

女人向前迈一步。幽灵惊慌颤抖。她在他的左脸颊上亲了一下。一个鲜红的口红印出现在同一个左脸颊上。幽灵僵住，然后像导弹一样穿过天花板，周围环绕着⛈️ 风暴能量。

对话：响亮的“MWAH!” 然后幽灵：“eeeEEEEE—!”

背景音乐：亲吻声 → 短暂静音 → 呼啸声 + 钹撞击 + 低音下沉。

CUT D (15–20s) — 天空撞击

夜空中。带着口红印的 👻 幽灵向上飞去，撞进一个巨大的风暴云。云震动并睁开两只巨大的卡通眼睛。

对话：
幽灵：“AAAAAHHH!”
云：“BWUH?!”
背景音乐：快速呼啸 → 雷声轰鸣 → 管弦乐钹撞击。

CUT E (20–25s) — 尴尬的云

在风暴云中，幽灵和云对视。两者都很害怕。幽灵慢慢退后。

对话：
幽灵：“…你好？”
云回应一个微弱的紧张雷声。

背景音乐：尴尬的拨弦乐，长时间停顿。

CUT F (25–30s) — 真正的赢家

回到豪宅内，女人悠闲地坐在扶手椅上，双腿交叉，手里拿着茶。窗外，幽灵形状的闪电在风暴中闪烁。她平静地啜饮茶水，放下杯子，说：
“仍然不感兴趣。”

以定格画面结束。
