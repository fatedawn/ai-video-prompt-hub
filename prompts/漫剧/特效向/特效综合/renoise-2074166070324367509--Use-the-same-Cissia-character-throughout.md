# Use the same Cissia character throughout.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the same Cissia character throughout.

Cissia inserts a coin into a cute vending machine to buy a snack. The machine suddenly opens giant cartoon eyes and begins eating every coin she has. She desperately tries to grab them back while the machine happily chews with exaggerated expressions. Finally, the machine spits out one tiny candy into her hand and proudly gives a thumbs-up. Cissia stares silently before collapsing dramatically. Cute anime comedy, smooth animation, expressive reactions, colorful city background, cinematic camera work, ultra-high quality.
```
