The sequence follows the exact progression and composition language of the storyboard panels.

0–3s

Wide cinematic shot of the vampire heroine standing on a cliff overlooking a gigantic gothic castle beneath a full moon while bats circle the sky.

The camera slowly pushes toward her flowing cape as moonlight illuminates the valley below.

3–6s

The camera glides through towering castle doors into a massive candlelit gothic hall.

The heroine cautiously steps inside, her cape trailing behind her while shadowy figures watch silently from the darkness.

6–9s

9–12s

The heroine discovers an ancient forbidden book inside a hidden library chamber.

The camera moves over her shoulder toward a massive portrait of a sinister vampire lord watching from the darkness with a faint smile.

12–15s

The heroine suddenly turns toward camera and extends her hand forward as if casting a spell directly at the viewer.

The sequence ends with her standing triumphantly on a rooftop beneath the enormous full moon while bats spiral around the gothic skyline.

Final frame:

the vampire heroine silhouetted against the giant moon above the gothic city, cape flowing dramatically while swarms of bats circle through the night sky.
