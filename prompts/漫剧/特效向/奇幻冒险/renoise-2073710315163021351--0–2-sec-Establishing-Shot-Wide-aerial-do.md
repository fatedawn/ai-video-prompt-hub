# 0–2 sec Establishing Shot Wide aerial dolly-in A giant cupcake sits on a table…

## 提示词（English）

```text
0–2 sec Establishing Shot Wide aerial dolly-in A giant cupcake sits on a table while dozens of tiny construction workers arrive with miniature cranes, forklifts, and trucks. Soft cheerful music, tiny vehicle sounds.

2–4 sec Frosting Scene Medium tracking shot A mixer truck pours silky pink frosting onto the cupcake as workers spread it evenly using tiny trowels. Cream pouring, tool scraping, upbeat music.

4–6 sec Toppings Assembly Close-up + slow motion Conveyor belts deliver strawberries, blueberries, chocolate chunks, and rainbow sprinkles. Workers carefully place each topping. Conveyor sounds, sprinkle drops, light magical chimes.

6–8 sec Cherry Lift Low-angle cinematic shot A miniature crane slowly lifts a giant shiny cherry toward the top while workers guide it with ropes. Crane motor, suspenseful orchestral rise.

8–10 sec Final Reveal 360° orbit close-up The cherry lands perfectly. Confetti bursts into the air as workers celebrate. Camera circles the completed cupcake before fading out. Confetti pop, cheering, uplifting music ending.
```
