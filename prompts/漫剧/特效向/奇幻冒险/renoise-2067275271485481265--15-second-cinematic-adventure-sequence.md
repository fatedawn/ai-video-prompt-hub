# 15-second cinematic adventure sequence. Multiple cuts. Dynamic editing.

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second cinematic adventure sequence. Multiple cuts. Dynamic editing.

Style: steampunk fantasy adventure, hand-drawn concept art brought to life, cinematic airship exploration, elegant Victorian engineering, warm sunlight above the clouds, detailed mechanical animation, epic sense of discovery, dynamic camera work, high-production animated feature film.

Sound design: wind rushing through rigging, creaking wood, canvas fluttering, distant propeller hum, metallic machinery, ropes tightening, occasional crew voices. Grand orchestral adventure music gradually builds.

0–2s

Wide aerial shot.

An enormous airship emerges from a sea of clouds.

Sunlight glints across the golden balloon.

The camera circles slowly around the vessel.

Clouds drift beneath it.

2–4s

Fast montage.

Close-up of spinning propellers.

Ropes straining in the wind.

Brass gauges vibrating.

Crew members rushing across wooden decks.

4–6s

Tracking shot.

The camera races alongside the hull.

Sails and rigging whip dramatically.

The ship banks between towering cloud formations.

6–8s

Wide shot.

The clouds suddenly part.

A floating city appears on the horizon.

Ancient towers suspended among the sky.

The crew stops and stares.

8–10s

Reaction montage.

Close-up of astonished faces.

A navigator drops his compass.

A captain grips the railing.

The city grows larger ahead.

10–12s

Dynamic fly-through.

The airship glides between floating rock formations.

The camera dives beneath the hull.

Sunlight flashes across brass machinery.

The propellers roar.

12–14s

Hero shot.

The vessel rises above the clouds.

The floating city dominates the skyline.

Flags snap in the wind.

Music reaches its peak.

14–15s

Final cinematic wide shot.

The airship sails toward the legendary city.

Clouds swirl around both structures.

The camera pulls back farther and farther.

Final frame:

The majestic vessel disappears into the golden sky, heading toward unknown horizons.

Negative prompt:

No cuts to modern technology, no photorealism, no science-fiction spacecraft, no war, no combat, no explosions, no AI artifacts, no low-quality animation, no distorted perspective, no text on screen, no subtitles, no extra flying vehicles, no dark dystopian elements.
```
