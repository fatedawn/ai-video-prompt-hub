SCENE 3 (0:07–0:10)

Don: "Still got a few tricks."

SCENE 9 (0:30–0:34)

Tony: "Hold on."

SCENE 10 (0:34–0:37)

Epic slow motion.

The hidden engraving suddenly projects a glowing pattern across the dashboard.

SCENE 11 (0:37–0:40)

The police are far behind.

Tony and Don stare at the glowing map hidden inside the necklace.

In the distance, a shadowy figure watches them from a rooftop.

Mysterious Voice:
