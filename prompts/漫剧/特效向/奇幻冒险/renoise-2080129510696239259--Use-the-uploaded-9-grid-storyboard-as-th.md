# Use the uploaded 9-grid storyboard as the sole storyboard reference for a

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the uploaded 9-grid storyboard as the sole storyboard reference for a
high-quality 3D animated short film.

Follow the shot order, key actions, compositions, scene transitions, and
emotional progression shown in each panel. Do not display the storyboard board
itself, panel borders, numbering, titles, captions, labels, or information bars
in the final video.

The story follows a devoted Japanese mother and her young son in a heartwarming
Olympic gymnastics fantasy adventure. The visual style should be premium cinematic
3D animation with expressive characters, sophisticated lighting, clean warm
Japanese home interiors, and an epic Olympic arena fantasy sequence.

Camera flow:

Begin in a serene Japanese home in early morning light where the mother sits
beside her son on a tatami mat, stretching with him and whispering gentle
encouragement. Cut to intimate close-ups as she carefully ties his wristbands
and adjusts his Japan team leotard in front of a mirror. The son does a
confident handstand in the living room, eyes bright with determination.

Transition seamlessly into his imagination. The living room dissolves into a
gleaming Olympic arena filled with thousands of cheering fans, brilliant
spotlights, and electric atmosphere. Show his focused expression, flowing
performance, spectacular aerial flips, and a dramatic perfect landing as the
crowd roars and the scoreboard lights up.

End by returning to the real home, where mother and son share a warm and tearful
hug, the morning sun streaming through the window, both filled with quiet pride.

Maintain consistent character appearance throughout the film. Use smooth cinematic
camera movement, graceful pacing, seamless transitions, and high-end lighting
that separates the peaceful home atmosphere from the dazzling grandeur of the Olympic fantasy world.
```
