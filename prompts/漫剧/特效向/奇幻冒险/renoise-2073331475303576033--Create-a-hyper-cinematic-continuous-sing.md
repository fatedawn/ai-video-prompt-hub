# Create a hyper-cinematic continuous single-take mystical festival sequence…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hyper-cinematic continuous single-take mystical festival sequence featuring LILY, the Orchid Priestess.

Use the uploaded character sheet as strict visual reference.
Maintain perfect consistency in face, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, lantern design, and color palette across all frames.

Do not change the character design in any way.

----------------------------

CHARACTER LOCK

LILY is a sacred orchid priestess who conducts ritual dances that awaken floating lantern spirits and blooming golden orchids.

- long black hair styled in traditional elegant buns with golden orchid ornaments
- calm expressive anime-style face, soft elegant features
- deep blue and gold ceremonial robe with flowing sleeves and floral orchid motifs
- translucent layered fabric with golden embroidery
- ornamental waist bow and structured chest design
- lantern accessory: round ceremonial lantern with red and gold patterns, glowing softly, slightly floating with magical suspension
- elegant traditional footwear with modern stylized design
- graceful dance-based movement style, controlled but expressive energy

----------------------------

ENVIRONMENT

A traditional fantasy festival street at night during a sacred lantern ceremony.

- wooden architecture with warm lantern lighting
- hanging lanterns across the street in rhythmic patterns
- floating lanterns rising into the night sky
- soft wind carrying petals and paper charms
- distant fireworks and glowing sky haze
- warm gold, red, and deep blue color palette
- festive but sacred atmosphere, quiet mystical undertone

----------------------------

CAMERA STYLE

handheld cinematic festival tracking camera
low-angle dynamic follow shots
smooth orbiting camera during dance movements
speed ramp during spins and lantern activation moments
occasional slow motion on lantern rise sequences

The entire sequence must feel like one continuous shot.
No static camera.

----------------------------

TIMELINE 15 SECONDS

0–3s FESTIVAL AWAKENING
LILY steps into a glowing festival street filled with dim lanterns.
The environment is calm, slightly dark, waiting for activation.
Camera slowly tracks forward toward her.

3–6s FIRST RITUAL STEP
LILY begins a slow ceremonial dance.
With each step, nearby lanterns begin to softly light up.
Her lantern accessory subtly levitates beside her.

6–9s LANTERN ACTIVATION
Her dance accelerates slightly.
Lanterns along the street light up in sequence, one after another.
The environment becomes progressively brighter and more alive.
Camera performs a smooth orbit around her movement.

9–12s FLOATING LANTERN ASCENSION
With a spinning motion, multiple lanterns detach from structures and begin floating upward.
Golden orchid petals appear in the air.
The entire street transforms into a glowing vertical lantern field.

12–15s RITUAL PEAK MOMENT
LILY performs a final elegant turn and raises her hand.
All floating lanterns rise rapidly into the sky simultaneously.
Fireworks bloom in the background.
Final frame: LILY standing calmly in the center of a fully illuminated festival street, lanterns ascending above her.

----------------------------

STYLE KEYWORDS

cinematic anime festival ritual
mystical lantern dance sequence
traditional eastern fantasy aesthetic
golden red warm lighting
floating lantern magic system
ritual choreography animation
ultra detailed fabric simulation
soft motion blur elegance
atmospheric night festival scene
4K anime film quality

----------------------------

NEGATIVE PROMPT

no modern city elements
no sci-fi technology
no combat or violence
no text
no subtitles
no watermark
no logo
no chibi style
no cartoon simplification
no extra limbs
no face distortion
no broken anatomy
no static camera shots
```
