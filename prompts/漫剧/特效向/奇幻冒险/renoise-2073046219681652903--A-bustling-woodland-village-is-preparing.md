# A bustling woodland village is preparing for the once-a-century Golden Acorn…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A bustling woodland village is preparing for the once-a-century Golden Acorn Festival when a gigantic enchanted acorn—large as a castle—is stolen overnight by a mischievous band of colorful trolls. But the trolls aren't evil; they believe the magical acorn belongs to their ancestors. A fearless squad of hyper-intelligent chipmunks launches a daring rescue mission, racing across mushroom cities, crystal caves, giant tree canopies, and glowing rivers.

What begins as an epic chase transforms into an unlikely alliance when both sides discover an ancient forest guardian awakening beneath the roots, threatening to destroy both their homes.

Together, the chipmunks and trolls must combine their strengths—tiny precision, wild creativity, clever inventions, and enormous brute force—to protect the magical forest.
```
