# 15-second animated magical storybook sequence.

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second animated magical storybook sequence.
The uploaded illustrations appear as pages inside a beautifully handcrafted oversized children’s book resting on a wooden table.

The camera slowly moves across the open book while pages turn naturally from right to left.
Each illustration becomes a living animated scene for a few seconds before transitioning to the next page.

The animation feels warm, cinematic and dreamlike, like a classic illustrated fairy tale coming to life.

0–3s:
Close-up of a textured storybook cover opening slowly.
Soft dust particles float in warm sunlight.
The first page reveals the colorful bird standing on the mossy stone.
Flowers sway gently.
The bird blinks and tilts its head curiously.

3–5s:
A page turns naturally.
The girl and white fox illustration appears.
Grass moves softly in the wind.
The flower floats slightly between them.
The fox breathes gently and the girl’s hair moves in the breeze.

5–7s:
Another page turn.
The giant black cat illustration fills the screen.
The cat slowly blinks.
Its tail curls lazily.
The girl on its back swings her legs slightly while colorful plants sway around them.

7–9s:
Page transition with visible paper texture.
The giraffe scene appears.
Clouds drift slowly.
The girl adjusts her hat.
Tall grass bends softly under the sunlight.

9–11s:
Another page flips.
The white forest creature appears in the moonlit fantasy forest.
The glowing plants pulse softly.
The moon emits gentle ambient light.
The creature’s fur moves slightly as if touched by wind.

11–13s:
Page turns again.
The little girl and the giant green serpent appear in the jungle.
Leaves sway dramatically.
The serpent blinks slowly and lowers its head affectionately toward the girl.

13–15s:
The camera pulls back.
Several illustrated pages rapidly flip by like memories from a fairy tale.
The book slowly closes.
Soft glowing particles drift around it.
Final frame feels cozy, magical and nostalgic.

Style:
- preserve original illustration style exactly
- painterly children’s book aesthetic
- soft textured brushwork
- whimsical fantasy atmosphere
- visible watercolor and paper grain
- no realism
- no CGI look
- no morphing between illustrations

Camera:
slow cinematic movements
macro lens feeling
gentle depth of field
smooth page-turn transitions

Audio:
soft orchestral fairytale music
gentle piano and celesta
paper page turning sounds
subtle ambient forest atmosphere
no dialogue
no singing
```
