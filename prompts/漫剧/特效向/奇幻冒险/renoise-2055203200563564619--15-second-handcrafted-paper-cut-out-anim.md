# 15-second handcrafted paper cut-out animation with visible human interaction.

## 提示词（English）

```text
15-second handcrafted paper cut-out animation with visible human interaction.

The entire illustration is transformed into a physical handmade paper puppet set placed on a tabletop.

All characters and animals are separate cut-out paper pieces with visible white borders, layered paper depth, soft shadows and watercolor textures.

A real human hand occasionally enters the scene and gently moves the paper characters like a handcrafted animated storybook.

Movement is playful, expressive and clearly handmade.

0–3s:
The paper scene is still.
A human hand enters frame and slides the little girl slightly forward across the paper landscape.
Paper layers shift naturally.

3–6s:
The girl begins “walking” in stop-motion style.
Her paper legs move separately like articulated cut-outs.
A bird is lifted slightly by hand and placed onto another hill.

6–9s:
More paper creatures animate energetically:
the black cat walks across the foreground,
birds flap in simple cut-out motion,
small rabbits hop using playful stop-motion jumps.

9–12s:
The hand returns and rotates some animals slightly.
Trees wobble softly like layered cardboard pieces.
Paper leaves drift downward.

12–15s:
The girl lifts her arms happily.
Animals gather around her.
The hand gently adjusts the final composition like finishing a scrapbook page.
Everything settles into a beautiful handcrafted tableau.

Animation style:
energetic paper puppet motion
handmade stop-motion feeling
visible handcrafted imperfections
slightly choppy but charming movement

Visual style:
- preserve original watercolor illustration style exactly
- visible paper edges and layered cut-out depth
- textured watercolor paper
- handcrafted shadows
- no realism
- no CGI look
- no morphing

Camera:
slight overhead angle
subtle handheld movement
gentle cinematic push-in

Audio:
soft playful music
paper sliding sounds
light cardboard and craft noises
no voices
```
