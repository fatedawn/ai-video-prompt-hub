# Create a cinematic animated football penalty shot scene in a realistic sports…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a cinematic animated football penalty shot scene in a realistic sports stadium.

A tense penalty kick is about to happen during a football match. The  striker has dark hair and a mustache, wearing a football kit, standing  focused behind the ball. The goalkeeper has blonde hair and stands  calmly in the center of the goal, looking relaxed and almost motionless.

The striker runs forward and powerfully strikes the ball. The ball  flies fast toward the far corner of the goal, clearly out of the  goalkeeper’s normal reach. The goalkeeper appears not to move at all,  still standing casually in the center.

Suddenly, impossibly, the goalkeeper catches the ball.

Cut to a dramatic slow-motion replay. Show the ball traveling toward  the corner of the goal. The goalkeeper’s arm secretly extends an  unnatural distance across the goal, stretching toward the corner. As the  arm extends, reveal shiny chrome robotic joints and mechanical parts  underneath, like a hidden cyborg mechanism. His robotic hand catches the  ball perfectly just before it enters the goal. The arm then retracts  smoothly back into normal human shape, the chrome parts disappearing  under his sleeve.

Return to real time. The goalkeeper stands casually in the center of  the goal, smiling confidently, holding the ball as if nothing unusual  happened. The striker stares in shock, then shakes his head in  disbelief. The crowd reacts with confusion and amazement.

Style: cinematic sports animation, realistic character movement,  dramatic camera angles, slow-motion replay, detailed stadium lighting,  suspenseful and slightly humorous tone.
```
