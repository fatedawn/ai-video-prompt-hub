## 提示词（English）

```text
SUBJECTS:
Fire Clan infiltrator inspired by Azuki aesthetics @ image1. Black oversized flight jacket with orange lining, tactical cargo pants, wrapped shinobi sandals, ignition bracer, oversized modified greatsword, wrist-mounted grappling cable launcher. Ember cracks and heat distortion appear during movement.

ENVIRONMENT:
Neo-futuristic vertical megacity mixing cyberpunk Tokyo and East-Asian architecture. Begins atop a suspended Fire Clan furnace tower surrounded by industrial shrines, neon kanji signs, elevated railways, ventilation pipes, holographic billboards, and hanging cables. Final section enters an abandoned thermal reactor connected to underground train tunnels.

CAMERA:
Single continuous cinematic shot with FPV openings, side tracking, low-angle hero shots, close-up facial push-ins, front-facing tracking shots, and over-shoulder perspectives. Camera frequently rotates around the character to reveal facial expressions, glowing eyes, and ember reflections. Heavy FOV distortion, motion blur, and lens warping throughout.

STYLE:
2.5D anime cinematic style, bold linework, halftone textures, painterly shadows, cel-shading, stepped-frame character animation, exaggerated speed smears, chromatic aberration, ember particles, manga-style impact frames, and graphic overlays.

COLOR LOGIC:
Black, charcoal gray, ember orange, crimson red, and selective cyan industrial lighting. Extreme heat moments trigger brief black-and-white inversions while preserving ember glow and sparks.

Timeline

0:00–0:03:
Close-up side profile of the character overlooking the city from the furnace tower. Ember light reflects across the face while alarms echo in the distance. Camera circles toward a front-facing close-up before the character suddenly drops backward into the abyss. FPV tracking follows closely as pipes and neon signs streak past.
SFX: alarm sirens + rushing wind

0:03–0:06:
Camera swings into a front-facing tracking shot during freefall. Character fires a grappling cable into a moving cargo drone carrying molten fuel cells. The sudden pull launches the body sideways through narrow industrial corridors while sparks explode from metal surfaces.
SFX: cable snap + metal scraping

0:06–0:09:
Low-angle shot beneath the character reveals the face illuminated by ember energy while approaching a giant rotating cooling fan. Character ignites the bracer and bursts directly through the spinning blades. Time briefly slows as shattered blades freeze midair with manga-style framing and ember distortion.
SFX: turbine rupture + ignition burst

0:09–0:12:
Inside the abandoned thermal reactor, camera tracks beside the character during a wall-run above speeding maglev trains. Electrical arcs illuminate the face during rapid turns and close passes. Camera briefly pushes into an intense eye close-up before pulling back wide.
SFX: reactor hum + electrical crackling

0:12–0:15:
Character lands onto a speeding train roof and drags the greatsword across the metal surface, generating a massive trail of sparks and fire. Camera rotates into a dramatic front-facing hero shot as the character rises while the reactor erupts behind them. Frame freezes into a manga-cover composition with halftone texture and bold Fire Clan typography.
SFX: heavy metal grind + distant explosion
```
