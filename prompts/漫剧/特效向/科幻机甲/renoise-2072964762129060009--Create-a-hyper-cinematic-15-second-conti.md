# Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous extreme skateboarding video using…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous extreme skateboarding video using Seedance 2.0.
Use the provided character reference image for strict identity consistency.

CHARACTER (STRICT CONSISTENCY)

Female street skater, athletic and agile, wearing a futuristic streetwear outfit with protective gear and a large utility backpack.
Outfit includes: sporty jacket, fitted shorts, knee pads, elbow pads, wrist guards, and high-performance skateboard shoes.
She rides a graffiti-styled skateboard with neon accents.
Maintain exact face, proportions, outfit design, and color consistency throughout.

STYLE

Cinematic industrial realism × stylized action anime hybrid
High contrast lighting with wet-metal reflections
Ultra dynamic motion blur with sharp impact frames
Handheld + drone + tracking camera hybrid cinematography
High-speed adrenaline skateboarding documentary style

ENVIRONMENT

A vast industrial cargo port at sunset transitioning into night
Massive shipping containers stacked in geometric structures
Steel cranes moving in the background
Wet concrete ground with reflective puddles
Windy atmosphere with dust particles and ocean mist
Yellow industrial lights + cold blue shadows

CAMERA LANGUAGE

Ultra low-angle wheel tracking shots
Fast whip-pan transitions between obstacles
Drone top-down dives through container corridors
Fisheye lens mid-air trick capture
Close-up grind sparks slow-motion insert shots
Chaotic handheld chase camera for speed sections

TIMELINE (15 SECONDS FAST SEQUENCE)

0s - 3s
Wide establishing shot of industrial port at sunset.
She drops from a container ramp into frame at full speed. Camera immediately locks into wheel-level chase.

3s - 6s
Low-angle tracking through narrow container alley.
She performs a fast ollie over steel debris, backpack bouncing heavily with motion.

6s - 9s
Drone overhead dive following her as she jumps between container platforms.
Mid-air kickflip with wind distortion and motion blur, landing into metal grind.

9s - 12s
Close-up grind sequence on steel rail.
Sparks fly intensely as board scrapes metal. Camera shakes with impact energy, slow-motion micro burst.

12s - 15s
Final high-speed descent through container ramp slope.
She accelerates toward open port exit, cranes passing in background.
Front-facing chase camera pulls backward as she rushes toward lens → extreme speed pass-by exit.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
Continuous shot illusion (no visible cuts)
Extreme speed and momentum maintained throughout
Industrial realism with cinematic stylization
Emphasis on sparks, wind, motion blur, and impact physics
No text, no logos, no subtitles
Ending must remain in motion (no freeze frame)
```
