# project: OROCHI_RUN_15s

## 提示词（日本語）

```text
project: OROCHI_RUN_15s
fps: 24
duration: 15s
aspect_ratio: 16:9
style: high-end Japanese anime, sharp cel linework, hard cel shading, high-contrast night lighting, controlled motion blur, theatrical 2D action

setting: >
  Night. Flooded industrial waterway, slick wet surfaces, standing water,
  drifting mist, dim sodium-orange lights reflecting on water.
  Colossal eight-headed serpent (Yamata-no-Orochi type): dark glowing scales,
  eight red-eyed heads, bodies that coil and shift as unstable platforms.

character: >
  Lean ninja-trained swordsman, dark combat garb, single katana, fearless,
  superhuman agility, always moving forward.

cut_1: # 0-4s
  action: lands hard on a moving coil, water bursts up, sprints along the scaled body, every step splashing.
  camera: close tracking behind, low, handheld shake, weaving between coils.
  fx: speed lines, flying droplets, foreground parallax.

cut_2: # 4-8s
  action: explodes into a long parkour leap to another body, mid-air twist, one diagonal slash across a snapping head, instant landing into sprint.
  camera: diagonal side angle, push-in on the slash, brief dutch tilt, snap behind on landing.
  fx: blade motion streak, impact shockwave + spark, droplet spray.

cut_3: # 8-12s
  action: nonstop run across shifting coils, chained slashes, a ringing parry against a lunging head, accelerating between heads.
  camera: high tracking diving close, weaving between heads, strong parallax, controlled shake.
  fx: speed lines, splash trails, wet highlights, mist.

cut_4: # 12-15s
  action: full acceleration, huge upward leap to the central head, descending decisive strike, blade impact bursts a shockwave ring and water plume.
  camera: fast pull-back revealing scale, then whip-in on the strike, hard dutch angle at impact.
  fx: shockwave ring, water plume, debris droplets, edge speed streaks.

motion_rules: continuous high speed, no slow motion, no pause, no retreat, intensity escalates each cut.

negative: 3DCG, CGI, plastic shading, photoreal, glossy specular, smooth gradient, blurry mush, low contrast, slow motion, extra people, crowds, text, watermark, deformed hands, warped sword, stiff robotic motion.
```
