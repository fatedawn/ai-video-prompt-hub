# Stylized 3D animation with exaggerated cartoon proportions, over-the-top…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Stylized 3D animation with exaggerated cartoon proportions, over-the-top martial-arts-inspired choreography, and highly rhythmic, almost musical energy.

CHARACTERS

Dr. Eraser: Lean, almost skeletal, with long fingers and oversized glasses that slip down his nose with every motion. Wears a bright yellow lab coat stuffed with strange tools—mini vacuum, magnifying glass, and odd gadgets. Moves in a “step → pause → spin” rhythm, like a conductor of a chaotic orchestra. Calm, fully in control, thriving in absurd precision.

Patient Plush: Huge, soft, and squishy, like a giant teddy bear. Hair sticks out in every direction, beard droops to his waist. Wears a stretched, messy sweater with funny stains. Sits like a statue, slightly trembling, only his eyes nervously following Dr. Eraser.

ENVIRONMENT

A cartoonish barbershop with oversized mirrors reflecting every exaggerated action. Warm overhead lighting, steam curling from hot towels like little clouds. Tools gleam with highlights. Hair piles grow like fluffy clouds with each cut.

MOOD

Absurd precision. Dr. Eraser = total control. Patient Plush = overwhelmed and comically terrified.

TIMELINE

0:00–0:02 (Close-up)

Patient Plush with wild hair and huge beard. Dr. Eraser pulls out giant scissors, spins them on his finger, and snaps them toward the camera. Coat flares like wings. Plush's eyes widen in cartoon shock.

0:02–0:05 (Medium shot with mirror)

Scissors fly, hair falling like confetti. Magnified glasses exaggerate every strand. Ears and neck gradually revealed. Beard untangles. Plush grips armrests; eyes dart nervously at every tool.

0:05–0:08 (Tracking shot)

Scissors return to pocket. Dr. Eraser opens a comically large straight razor, like a flying sword. Beard disappears in strips. Plush shuts eyes tight. Final shaving stroke sends foam flying like fireworks.

0:08–0:11

Hot towel tossed, spins mid-air, lands perfectly on lower face. Brief pause — then pulled off in one bold motion. Smooth skin gleams. Plush touches face in disbelief.

0:11–0:13

Final styling: pomade applied with theatrical precision. Hair shaped into a shiny, cartoonishly perfect style. Talc brush creates a glowing powder cloud. Coat flaps off instantly with a snap.

0:13–0:15

Chair spins, stopping to face the mirror.

FINAL REVEAL

Reflection shows Patient Plush fully transformed: smooth face, sharp cartoon-style hair, beard gone. He touches his face with both hands, wide-eyed. Dr. Eraser stands behind, arms crossed, spins scissors once, snaps shut, pockets them, and nods confidently.
```

Tesouras voltam para o bolso. Dr. Eraser abre uma navalha reta comicamente grande, como uma espada voadora. Barba desaparece em tiras. Plush fecha os olhos com força. A última passada de barbear envia espuma voando como fogos de artifício.

0:08–0:11

Toalha quente jogada, gira no ar, pousa perfeitamente no rosto inferior. Breve pausa — então é puxada em um movimento ousado. Pele lisa brilha. Plush toca o rosto em descrença.

0:11–0:13

Estilo final: pomada aplicada com precisão teatral. Cabelo moldado em um estilo brilhante e cartunesco perfeito. Pincel de talco cria uma nuvem de pó brilhante. Jaleco se fecha instantaneamente com um estalo.

0:13–0:15

Cadeira gira, parando de frente para o espelho.

REVELAÇÃO FINAL

Reflexo mostra Patient Plush totalmente transformado: rosto liso, cabelo em estilo cartunesco afiado, barba desaparecida. Ele toca o rosto com ambas as mãos, olhos arregalados. Dr. Eraser está atrás, braços cruzados, gira a tesoura uma vez, fecha-a com um estalo, guarda-a no bolso e acena confiante.
