# Create a 15-second 16:9 cinematic 3D arcade fighting game video in landscape…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second 16:9 cinematic 3D arcade fighting game video in landscape format only. No black bars. Style: polished modern fighting game with dramatic HUD at the top, health bars, timer, bottom power meters, and a final “K.O.” finish. Keep the action readable and game-like.

Outdoor Japanese shrine courtyard arena in daylight. Stone ground, red temple roof, trees, mountains, blue sky, and a few background spectators. Classic side-view fighting game camera.

Left fighter: a medium-build brown cat standing only on two legs like a human fighter. Not too chubby. Athletic but still cute. Soft brown fur, cream muzzle, expressive eyes, upright posture, human-like fighting stance, uses arms and legs like a person. The cat must stay bipedal the entire video and never go down to four legs.
Right fighter: a huge muscular martial artist, spiky red hair, sleeveless black gi, white belt, wrapped hands, intimidating build and confident attitude.

0:00-0:02
Wide side-view shot. Brown cat stands on the left in a human-like fighting pose, balanced on two feet only. Huge martial artist stands on the right. Both square up to fight.

0:02-0:04
The martial artist steps forward aggressively, preparing to attack. The cat looks smaller but calm. Tension builds.

0:04-0:07
The cat activates its special move. Dramatic zoom-in on the cat’s face. The cat makes an extremely cute, irresistible expression with big watery sparkling eyes, tiny pout, innocent face, soft glow, and subtle pink heart sparkles.
Add on-screen text or subtitle: “Do you still want to attack me?”

0:07-0:09
Cut to the martial artist reacting. At first he looks affected by the cat’s cuteness trap, emotionally frozen, softening, almost falling for it. His expression changes as if he’s charmed for a moment.

0:09-0:10.5
Then his face slowly changes into a smirk. He realizes it is a trap. He regains confidence and prepares a counterattack.

0:10.5-0:12.5
Fast action beat. The martial artist suddenly launches a powerful kick straight into the cat. Strong fighting-game impact flash, motion blur, and dramatic hit effect. The cat is knocked back hard.

0:12.5-0:13.5
The brown cat falls defeated. The martial artist lands smoothly and stands tall in a victorious pose.

0:13.5-0:15
Huge metallic gold “K.O.” text appears center screen. Final freeze-frame: martial artist victorious, cat defeated in the background, shrine arena visible, game HUD still on screen.

Keep the cat standing on two legs only for the full clip
Cat should fight like a human character, not like a normal animal
Brown cat should be medium build, not fat, not too tiny, not too chubby
The cute-face moment should feel funny and dramatic
The opponent should clearly pretend to fall for it, then reveal the smirk and win
Use polished arcade-fighting camera language: side-view, close-up zoom, reaction shot, strong finisher impact
No copyrighted characters or game logos
```
