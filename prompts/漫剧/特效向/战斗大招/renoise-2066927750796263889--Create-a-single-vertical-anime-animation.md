# Create a single vertical anime animation development board for an original…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a single vertical anime animation development board for an original high-energy action short film titled "Frozen Mountain Summit." The final output must be ONE combined image only, clearly divided into 2 sections: TOP SECTION: Character design sheet BOTTOM SECTION: 12-frame storyboard page Create fully original anime characters. Do not imitate any existing anime, manga, game, or copyrighted character designs. Keep everything original, cinematic, dynamic, and easy to understand. STYLE: Premium anime pre-production board, loose pencil sketches mixed with semi-rendered anime keyframes, dramatic lighting, strong contrast, speed lines, impact frames, red panel borders, blue motion arrows, handwritten notes, timing notes, and lens notes. TOP SECTION — CHARACTER DESIGN SHEET Use a light neutral paper-like background. Fighter A: Young anime ice-climate fighter, around 19, short choppy white hair, icy blue eyes, athletic muscular build, sharp determined expression. Outfit: heavy fur-collared combat jacket (torn), dark thermal gloves, reinforced boots, combat belt. Personality: aggressive, bold, high-impact strikes. Show front view, side view, back view, 3/4 view, battle-ready stance, running attack pose, uppercut pose, and a few expressions. Fighter B: Rival mountain warrior, around 22, wild long dark hair whipping in the wind, dark grey eyes, tall powerful build, stern expression. Outfit: heavy sleeveless cloak, chest wraps, thick trousers, iron-toed boots. Personality: stoic, immovable, overwhelmingly powerful. Show front view, side view, back view, 3/4 view, firm planted stance, deflect pose, downward hammer-strike pose, and a few expressions. Add small handwritten design notes and compact anime color swatches. BOTTOM SECTION — STORYBOARD PAGE Use a dark black background for the entire storyboard section. Do not use beige, yellow, or paper-colored background here. Create 12 cinematic anime storyboard panels in a clean grid. Use red panel borders, blue motion arrows, handwritten camera notes, timing notes, and action notes. Keep both fighters visually consistent. 12 STORYBOARD FRAMES: ➤ Split-screen with thick black divider: Fighter A scaling the final rocky ledge of the summit, breath visible in cold air (upper frame); Fighter B standing at the peak, arms crossed, snow swirling around him (lower frame). ➤ Split-screen close-ups: Fighter A's boot kicks into the ice as he climbs; Fighter B's eyes look down, cold and waiting. ➤ Wide shot of Fighter A reaching the summit top — both fighters face each other against the icy sky. ➤ Fighter A launches forward, boots cracking the frozen ground, snow exploding outward. ➤ Tracking shot of Fighter A closing in with a fast series of body strikes. ➤ Slow-motion: Fighter B barely shifts his weight and absorbs the strikes, barely moving. ➤ Fighter B grabs Fighter A by the collar with one hand and lifts him. ➤ Fighter B throws Fighter A hard across the summit plateau, ice and snow flying. ➤ Wide shot of Fighter A skidding across the ice toward the cliff edge. ➤ Fighter A stops himself at the edge, looks back — Fighter B is already charging. ➤ Fighter B closes the gap instantly with a thundering hammer-fist strike. ➤ Aftermath: snow blows across the summit, Fighter B stands at the center of a shattered ice crater, Fighter A down near the cliff edge, vast mountain skyline behind them. ENVIRONMENT: Frozen mountain summit, cracked glacier ice, howling wind, flying snow, frozen rock ledges, vast sky, snow clouds, cliff edges. FINAL GOAL: Make the board clean, readable, cinematic, and production-ready. The top section should clearly show the character designs. The bottom section should clearly show the 12-frame action choreography on a black storyboard background.
```
