# Crows on a rock with Celtic runes and glass mosaic jug

> A Grok Imagine prompt describing a scene with a white crow and a black crow sitting on a rock inscribed with Celtic Gaelic runes about love, next to a glass mosaic water jug that casts colored light onto the crows.

## 提示词（English）

```text
One white crow and one black crow  sit on top of rock with Celtic Gaelic runes speaking of love  , Glass mosaic water jug sits on another rock, sunshine through mosaic water jug creates colors that gently washes over the crows
```
