# Create a 15-second cinematic high-altitude volleyball action video with a…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second cinematic high-altitude volleyball action video with a strong first-second hook, clean rally structure, and a spectacular final spike.

Core concept:

Two teams play volleyball on a court stretched between two skyscraper rooftops high above a modern city. The net spans the rooftop gap, and the open sky and city drop below make every jump feel dangerous and exhilarating.

Visual style:
cinematic sports realism, polished 3D CG or premium live-action realism, modern city skyline, strong rooftop wind, bright daylight or sunset light, dramatic height, no text, no watermark.

0.0–2.0 sec:
Start with a powerful hook. Open on a volleyball rising high above the city skyline, then reveal the impossible court spanning two skyscraper rooftops. Players are already mid-rally.

2.0–5.0 sec:
One side receives and sets the ball cleanly. The rooftop wind is visible in clothing and hair. The city far below remains visible, emphasizing the dangerous setting.

5.0–8.0 sec:
The rally continues with one diving save and a controlled recovery. The ball remains easy to track. The camera alternates between court-level motion and a slightly wider rooftop view to keep the scale clear.

8.0–11.0 sec:
The ball is set high near the net for the final attacking sequence. The spiker takes a fast approach run on the rooftop court. The open air and drop beyond the net are clearly visible.

11.0–13.5 sec:
The spiker jumps high against the city skyline and hangs for a moment above the net. The camera frames the player, the net, and the distant skyline together for a big sports-movie image.

13.5–15.0 sec:
Final payoff. The player slams a decisive final spike downward. The ball lands cleanly. The team celebrates briefly as wind rushes across the rooftop. End on the spiker landing strong with the skyline behind them.

Camera:
Use one skyline reveal, court-level rally shots, a wide rooftop shot showing the impossible height, and a final heroic spike angle.

Motion:
Keep the rally simple and readable. The height and rooftop danger should enhance the tension, but the ball and player actions must remain easy to understand.
```
