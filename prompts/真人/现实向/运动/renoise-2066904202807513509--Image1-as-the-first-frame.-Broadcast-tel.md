```text
@Image1 as the first frame. Broadcast television coverage of a FIFA World Cup 2026 match, multi-camera style with clean cuts between shots, smooth professional camera work, sharp broadcast color. Live commentary plays over the footage throughout.

0–2s: Wide broadcast shot of the packed stadium, then a steady push-in on the old lady sitting calmly in her seat watching the match, hands folded, a small smile. Commentary (calm): "And the atmosphere here is electric as we head into the final minutes."
2–4s: Cut to a low pitch-side close-up — a player winds up and strikes the ball hard, tight shot on his boot connecting, the ball rockets off his foot toward the stands. Commentary (rising): "He winds up — OH, that's miscued into the crowd!"

4–6s: Cut back to the old lady. The ball flies straight toward her. The people around her flinch and duck, one shouts. Hold on her calm face. Commentary (alarmed): "Look out! That's coming straight for the stands!"

8–12s: She stands, drops the ball to her feet, and leaps impossibly high above the crowd. At the peak she volleys it back toward the pitch — slow-motion on the strike, a wind shockwave ripples off the kick, the ball becomes a blazing streak screaming toward the goal. Commentary (exploding): "She's up — and she's SENT IT! I have never seen anything like this!"
12–14s: Cut to a close-up at the far goal — the ball rockets past the diving keeper and smashes the back of the net, the net snapping back, dust flying off the post. Commentary (screaming): "GOAAAL! Unbelievable! UNBELIEVABLE!"

14–15秒：切回到老太太，她再次平静地坐下，仿佛什么都没发生，而她周围的人群沸腾——跳跃，尖叫，举起双手，拍摄她。她露出一个微小而谦逊的微笑。解说（难以置信地笑）："第十二排的奶奶赢得了世界杯！"
