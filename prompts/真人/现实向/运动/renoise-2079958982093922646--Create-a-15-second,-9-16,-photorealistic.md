# Create a 15-second, 9:16, photorealistic climbing and parkour video.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second, 9:16, photorealistic climbing and parkour video.

Use the Depth video as the primary reference for the full timeline, camera, climbing path, movement speed, body positions, jumps, weight shifts, and landings. Follow it closely without redesigning, skipping, or adding actions.

Use the image to lock the character and environment. Keep the same 20-year-old adult East Asian student with twin tails, school-uniform-inspired clothing, white sneakers, and a dark backpack throughout the video.

Character and scene continuity are the highest priority. Keep her face, body proportions, hairstyle, clothing, shoes, and backpack consistent from beginning to end. The backpack and both shoulder straps must remain attached and stable.

All grips, steps, jumps, wall contact, weight transfers, and landings must follow realistic anatomy, gravity, momentum, and physical support.

Keep every concrete wall, beam, window frame, and platform rigid and unchanged. Do not let the architecture bend, break, melt, move, shrink, or disappear.

Maintain the original camera and framing. The result should feel like a real outdoor parkour recording, not a cinematic action scene.

Avoid character deformation, missing or extra limbs, face changes, disappearing objects, broken backpack straps, body–wall fusion, floating, teleporting, clipping through surfaces, altered choreography, camera cuts, text, logos, and watermarks.
```
