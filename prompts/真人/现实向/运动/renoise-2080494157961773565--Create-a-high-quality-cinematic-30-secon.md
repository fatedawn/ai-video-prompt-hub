# Create a high-quality cinematic 30-second vertical video (9:16) for STADIUM…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a high-quality cinematic 30-second vertical video (9:16) for STADIUM SECRETS – EPISODE ONE,

exactly matching the style, color grade, lighting, stadium, and aesthetic of Episode Zero. Photorealistic live-action footage, 4K, 24fps, shot on ARRI Alexa with natural cinematic camera movement, shallow depth of field, and subtle film grain. Every element must feel 100% real: authentic jersey fabric movement, hair physics, sweat, breathing, ball spin/trajectory, and crowd reactions.

Character & World Lock (MANDATORY 100% consistency with Episode Zero):
- Mia: Exact same 25-year-old athletic woman — dark wavy hair in tight high ponytail with a few loose strands, subtle scar on left cheek, intense expressive eyes, natural sweat. Same red national team kit with yellow number 7.

- Spectral Figure: Exact same semi-transparent hooded elderly man with warm ethereal blue-white glow and smoky distortion. ONLY visible to Mia. Must appear in the exact same stadium/crowd setting style as Episode Zero.

- Alex and teammates: Exact same faces, bodies, and kits as Episode Zero.
- Stadium, sunny daylight, crowd style, and overall look must be identical to Episode Zero.

STRICT RULES:
- Follow the provided timing and story beats EXACTLY. Do not add or remove anything.
- NO sudden appearances of any character. Every appearance must feel natural and logical: the Spectral Figure must slowly materialize with gentle ethereal glow and smoky build-up, or be revealed naturally by camera movement/panning. Never have anyone “pop” into frame.
- Smooth, film-like transitions only (gentle dissolves 0.6–1s or perfect match cuts). No abrupt/jarring cuts.
- Perfect logical flow and emotional clarity so a first-time viewer instantly understands the continuation, Mia’s inner conflict, and the new stakes.
- Realistic physics and natural performances only. No teleporting, floating, or illogical actions.

SCENE BREAKDOWN (exactly 30 seconds):

0–5s | Recall & Re-hook
Direct continuation from Episode Zero’s final frame. Start with a frozen moment on Mia’s powerful shot (ball mid-air with realistic spin and motion blur) + her determined face with faint ghostly overlay. Quick flash of the text “Play… or be forgotten forever.”
The ball hits the back of the net realistically — GOAL! Sunny stadium erupts with roaring crowd and waving flags. Mia celebrates with raw, mixed emotions (joy + lingering pain). Smooth cinematic motion.

5–14s | Consequence & Glory
Teammates (exact same kits) realistically lift and celebrate with Mia on the pitch. Alex approaches with clear remorse and love in his eyes, reaching out to her.
Tight close-up on Mia: natural hesitation, tears mixing with sweat, subtle breathing, scar visible. She pulls back, visibly torn and conflicted.
Smooth dissolve transition to the Spectral Figure already present in the same stadium stands among the crowd (only Mia sees him), watching her with a proud, subtle warm smirk. The figure slowly becomes more visible through gentle glowing and smoky effect.

14–22s | Escalation & Revelation
Mia makes a brilliant, realistic football play on the pitch (natural running, ball control, physics). Her focus wavers when she glances at Alex.
Camera movement reveals the Spectral Figure already positioned closer beside her on the pitch edge (stadium setting, only she sees him) with slow ethereal materialization.
Gentle but powerful echoing voiceover (Spectral Figure): “Whether you let him go or not… the choice is yours.”

22–30s | New Cliffhanger
After the play, Alex approaches Mia again, looking remorseful. He says with emotion: “Please forgive me.”
Mia’s face hardens. She slaps him sharply across the face. She turns and walks away without looking back. All teammates and crowd around them freeze in shock and surprise.
Tight emotional close-up on Mia’s strong, conflicted face as she walks out. Hard cut to black.
Title card in the exact same style as Episode Zero: “STADIUM SECRETS – EPISODE ONE”.
```
