# Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous single-take surfing video.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a hyper-cinematic 15-second continuous single-take surfing video.

Use Image 1 as the strict identity, outfit, and surfboard reference. Preserve her exact face, pale skin, blue eyes, long straight black hair, body proportions, feminine surf outfit, and surfboard design. No identity, clothing, or board changes.

Setting: bright daylight over a deep blue open ocean, strong sunlight reflections, powerful waves, sea spray, and realistic high-speed surfing atmosphere.

The video begins with her already surfing. The camera starts very close in front of her upper body and face. Her wet hair moves in the wind, and her expression is calm, fearless, and focused.

In one smooth uninterrupted movement, the camera swings around her body to the back, then drops to surfboard level. For the rest of the video, it stays very low and close behind the board near the tail or rail, almost touching the water. Never use a high angle or drone view.

She lowers into a deep crouch and accelerates hard across the wave. She leans forward with realistic athletic balance, reaches one hand down, and skims her fingertips across the water. Spray flies backward toward the lens while the board cuts rapidly across the surface.

She approaches a stronger wave section and performs a believable aerial surfing trick. She launches into the air with the board under her feet, all in the same continuous shot. During the jump, motion shifts into dramatic slow motion. The camera remains low, close, and slightly behind or underneath the surfboard.

While airborne, she turns her upper body toward the camera, looks at us, and grabs the surfboard with one hand. Her hair lifts in the wind, water droplets hang in the air, and the board remains fully visible.

She rotates back naturally and lands cleanly on the wave with a powerful burst of spray. The camera stays low behind the board and follows the impact closely. She remains crouched and immediately accelerates toward a larger rising wave.

End with her still charging forward at high speed from the same low trailing angle, spray exploding around the lens.

Style: ultra-realistic, physically believable surfing, natural body-weight corrections, realistic water simulation, cinematic action, natural motion blur, water droplets on lens, wind noise, wave sound, and board-skimming sound.

No text, logos, subtitles, cuts, hidden cuts, transitions, or montage.

Negative prompt: identity drift, face change, hairstyle change, outfit change, surfboard change, camera teleporting, high-angle shot, drone shot, floating board, unrealistic physics, stiff movement, distorted anatomy, extra limbs, blurry face, cartoon, CGI look, fake water, duplicated body.
```
