# Use Image A as the character reference and Image B as the course reference.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use Image A as the character reference and Image B as the course reference.

Create a realistic 15-second 16:9 live-action video of a young K-pop-style woman doing a summer water obstacle challenge on a Korean variety show set.

Keep the obstacle order:
starting platform → floating light-stick steps → spinning record disc → swinging microphone bars → slippery final ramp → splash zone

Show this expression flow:
confident smile → focused effort → startled reaction → trying to recover balance → embarrassed wet reaction after falling

Key beats:
1、starts with a cheerful smile and runs from the platform
2、crosses the floating steps as they wobble under her feet
3、jumps onto the spinning disc and starts losing balance
4、gets lightly bumped by the swinging microphone bar and stumbles forward
5、runs up the slippery final ramp, nearly reaches the top, then slides back down
6、falls into the water with a big splash
7、after resurfacing, her headband has fallen off, her wet hair is messy, and she gives a funny embarrassed reaction

Requirements:
realistic motion, realistic slipping, realistic water splash, strong audience presence, visible staff and lifeguards, no empty stands, no cartoon look, no game-render look, no text, no watermark
```
