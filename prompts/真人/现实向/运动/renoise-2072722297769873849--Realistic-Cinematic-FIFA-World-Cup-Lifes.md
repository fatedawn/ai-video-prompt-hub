# Realistic Cinematic FIFA World Cup Lifestyle Vlog.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Realistic Cinematic FIFA World Cup Lifestyle Vlog.

Create a 15-second hyper-realistic cinematic lifestyle vlog featuring a confident 26-year-old female soccer content creator passionately covering the FIFA World Cup. She has athletic build, long wavy dark hair in a high ponytail, glowing warm skin, and wears a fitted white national team jersey (with subtle flag colors), black athletic shorts, white sneakers, and a colorful wristband. The atmosphere is electric yet personal, with premium influencer aesthetics inspired by high-end sports TikTok and Instagram Reels during major tournaments.
Use ultra-smooth gimbal tracking, dynamic cinematic handheld movement, quick whip transitions, speed ramps, shallow depth of field, vibrant stadium lighting with lens flares, energetic crowd ambiance audio, and realistic facial expressions with accurate lip sync. No text overlays, logos, or watermarks.

Scene 1 (0–2 sec)
Close-up sequence of her putting on wireless earbuds, checking her phone with match updates, grabbing a team scarf, and stepping out toward the bustling fan zone outside the stadium.
Dialogue: “World Cup day hits different.”
⸻
Scene 2 (2–5 sec)
Front-facing selfie shot as she walks confidently through colorful crowds with stadium lights and flags waving behind her. Gentle breeze moves her ponytail.
Dialogue: “The energy here is absolutely unreal.”
⸻
Scene 3 (5–8 sec)
Quick cinematic montage:
her shoes on the pavement, phone showing live score, flags waving in the wind, fans cheering, close-up of her excited smile.
Dialogue: “Every match feels like the final.”
⸻
Scene 4 (8–12 sec)
Elegant slow-motion shots of her jumping with joy beside the fan wall, cheering loudly, laughing naturally as golden stadium lights glow around her.
Dialogue: “This is what football dreams are made of.”
⸻
Scene 5 (12–15 sec)
She looks back toward the camera with a bright, passionate smile, gives an enthusiastic wave and thumbs-up, then turns toward the stadium entrance as the camera slowly pulls away.
Dialogue: “Who’s ready for kickoff? Let’s go!”
```
