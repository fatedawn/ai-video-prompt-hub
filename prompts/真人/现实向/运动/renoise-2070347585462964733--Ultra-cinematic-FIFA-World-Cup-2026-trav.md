# Ultra-cinematic FIFA World Cup 2026 travel film.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra-cinematic FIFA World Cup 2026 travel film.

A beautiful young female football supporter goes on a global journey across multiple iconic cities while supporting her team. Elegant, charismatic, fashionable, energetic, natural expressions throughout.

Sequence: Seoul → Paris → Rio de Janeiro → Lisbon → New York → FIFA World Cup stadium.

Every location transforms seamlessly into the next while she remains the same person.

Seoul: neon-lit streets, modern fashion, walking confidently.

Paris: stylish café district, soft golden evening light.

Rio: vibrant atmosphere, flags waving, energetic crowd.

Lisbon: colorful streets, football scarves, festive supporters.

New York: giant digital billboards, exciting matchday energy.

Final destination: massive illuminated FIFA World Cup stadium at night with thousands of cheering fans.

She wears fashionable football-inspired outfits in each city with subtle national colors, trendy sneakers, and accessories. Her hair and clothing react naturally to movement and wind.

Camera constantly evolves like a luxury travel commercial: dolly shots, tracking shots, sweeping gimbal movement, orbit shots, low-angle hero shots, cinematic push-ins, wide aerial reveals, close-up emotional moments, and smooth transitions between cities.

Visual style: ultra realistic, premium sports documentary, cinematic lighting, glossy reflections, volumetric atmosphere, realistic skin texture, dynamic crowd energy, luxury travel aesthetic, immersive world football celebration.

Final shot: she walks toward the glowing stadium while fireworks explode overhead and supporters from around the world celebrate together.
```
