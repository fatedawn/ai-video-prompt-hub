# Ultra Detailed Art Nouveau Princess Portrait with Butterflies

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to create an ultra-detailed portrait of a princess, integrating Art Nouveau and Rococo styles, focusing on a golden ratio spiral formed by butterflies around her luminous face and hyper-detailed features.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra detailed portrait of a princess surrounded by a symphony of butterflies, art nouveau flow integrated with rococo detailing, golden ratio spiral formed by butterfly movement around her face
her skin is luminous, soft blush tones, hyper detailed eyelashes, glass like eyes
her dress is partially visible, composed of layered butterfly wings with iridescent textures.
```
