# 节奏驱动的时尚变装序列

*Beat-Driven Fashion Transformation Sequence*

> 一个节奏感极强的时尚视频提示词，通过与 140 BPM 同步的特定镜头列表，追踪角色从疲惫到自信的转变过程。

## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 140 BPM / 14 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Basic → stylish
ENVIRONMENT: Room → street
MOOD: Tired → energized → confident
STYLE: Beat-driven cinematic
CORE IDEA: Music shifts her whole energy
SHOT FLOW:
Sitting bored
Puts on headphones
Presses play
Beat drops
Head nod
Energy builds
Quick outfit fix
Mirror look
Walk out
Steps sync with beat
Hair movement
Street feels alive
Confident walk
End on strong look
```
