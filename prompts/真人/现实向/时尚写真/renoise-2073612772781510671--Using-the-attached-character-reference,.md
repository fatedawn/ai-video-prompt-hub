# Using the attached character reference, create a 15-second ultra-realistic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Using the attached character reference, create a 15-second ultra-realistic cinematic GRWM (Get Ready With Me) video featuring the exact same woman with consistent facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, and identity throughout the entire sequence.

The video begins with her peacefully sleeping in a cozy modern bedroom before an alarm suddenly wakes her. She quickly sits up, rubs her eyes, stretches, and rushes into the bathroom where she splashes water on her face and brushes her teeth. She hurries into the kitchen, opens the refrigerator, grabs ingredients, prepares a quick breakfast, and eats while constantly checking the time.

She then rapidly changes from comfortable sleepwear into a stylish professional outfit, slipping on her shoes, grabbing her handbag, keys, and phone before rushing out the apartment. The scene follows her walking briskly through a hallway, entering a metro, standing among commuters while checking the station display, then arriving at a modern office where she scans her access card, sits at her desk, opens her laptop, and begins working.

As the workday ends, she returns home, unlocks her apartment, changes back into comfortable sleepwear, washes away the day's stress, relaxes in her softly lit bedroom, and gently falls back onto the bed in the same pose as the opening scene, creating a seamless visual loop.

Use smooth cinematic transitions, match cuts, whip pans, natural object wipes, realistic motion blur, expressive facial acting, authentic body movement, soft window light indoors, warm practical lighting, realistic metro and office environments, premium fashion styling, shallow depth of field, cinematic color grading, ultra-realistic skin textures, natural hair physics, glossy commercial quality, 4K HDR, highly detailed, consistent character identity across every shot, with no text, subtitles, logos, or watermarks.
```
