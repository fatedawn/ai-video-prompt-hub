# Create an ultra-premium cinematic fashion film that feels like an official…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create an ultra-premium cinematic fashion film that feels like an official luxury fashion campaign, not a standard runway recording. The entire film features the **same ultra-realistic fashion model** with identical facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, skin tone, and confident personality throughout. Maintain perfect character consistency across every shot. Present **5 different outfits** from one cohesive luxury fashion collection. Each outfit changes instantly with seamless match-cut transitions while preserving the model's identity.

**Theme:** Futuristic Luxury Streetwear

**Visual Style:** Vogue editorial, luxury fashion campaign, cinematic, ultra-photorealistic, premium lighting, flawless skin, realistic fabric physics, HDR, 8K quality, shallow depth of field, crisp details.

**Music Direction:** High-energy luxury fashion house / melodic techno / electronic runway beat with a strong bassline. Every camera movement, transition, pose, and outfit change lands perfectly on the beat. The entire film should feel hypnotic, stylish, and addictive to watch.

0–3 Seconds — Opening Hook

The film opens inside a massive futuristic fashion venue with polished black reflective floors, towering LED walls, cinematic spotlights cutting through light haze, and an excited audience barely visible in darkness.

The camera begins with an extreme close-up of the model's eyes before slowly pulling back to reveal the complete first outfit.

The model confidently raises their head and begins walking directly toward the camera.

Slow-motion fabric movement.

Subtle wind blows through the hair and clothing.

Camera performs a smooth cinematic dolly backward while maintaining perfect eye contact.

**Outfit 1**
Luxury oversized black leather jacket, premium cargo pants, designer sneakers, futuristic accessories.

3–6 Seconds — Outfit Transition

Exactly on the music beat, the scene transforms instantly with a seamless match-cut.

Lighting changes from black and gold to cool silver-blue.

The model never stops walking.

Camera switches to a dramatic low-angle tracking shot emphasizing confidence and power.

Clothing flows naturally.

Lens flares shimmer across metallic surfaces.

**Outfit 2**
High-end monochrome techwear with layered jacket, tactical trousers, luxury sneakers and chrome accessories.

6–9 Seconds — Editorial Energy

Another beat.

A flawless spinning transition changes both environment and outfit.

Camera performs a smooth orbit around the model while the model pauses briefly for an editorial fashion pose before continuing.

Beautiful cloth simulation.

Perfect realistic shadows.

Luxury commercial quality.

**Outfit 3**
White futuristic tailored suit with sculptural silhouette, luxury boots, silver jewelry.

9–12 Seconds — Peak Fashion Moment

The beat becomes stronger.

Rapid cinematic transitions between multiple camera angles.

Tracking shot.

Side profile.

Close-up of luxury accessories.

Full-body hero shot.

Everything remains perfectly synchronized to the music.

**Outfit 4**
Metallic silver avant-garde fashion with dramatic flowing cape and futuristic footwear.

The model delivers a confident runway turn before walking forward again.

12–15 Seconds — Grand Finale

Final beat drop.

The venue lights become brighter.

The camera slowly cranes upward while simultaneously pushing toward the model.

The fifth outfit appears through an elegant seamless transition.

The model performs one final iconic editorial pose.

The camera circles slowly around the model.

Confetti-like light particles float through the air.

The film ends with a dramatic freeze-frame worthy of an international luxury fashion campaign.

**Outfit 5**
Premium luxury black and gold couture ensemble with flowing long coat, designer boots, elegant accessories and striking silhouette.

The ending feels powerful, memorable, luxurious and instantly shareable.

**Overall Requirements**

Maintain identical model identity throughout all outfit changes.

Every outfit belongs to the same luxury designer collection.

Ultra-realistic skin texture.

Natural facial expressions.

Photorealistic lighting.

Premium cinematic camera movement.

Realistic cloth physics.

Luxury editorial styling.

Smooth beat-synced transitions.

No glitches.

No distorted anatomy.

No extra people blocking the model.

No text overlays.

No logos.

No watermarks.

The final result should look indistinguishable from a $1 million luxury fashion brand campaign created by an elite creative studio.
```
