# Ultra Detailed Solar Empress Portrait in Art Deco and Rococo Style

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to generate an ultra-detailed masterpiece portrait of a solar empress, combining Art Deco geometry and Rococo ornamentation. The composition strictly adheres to the golden ratio, featuring hyper-realistic facial features, a structured couture gown with golden panels, and a massive radiant halo.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra detailed masterpiece portrait of a solar empress standing in perfect frontal symmetry, inspired by art deco geometry and rococo ornamentation, strict golden ratio composition aligning her face, crown, and radiant structures behind her, her facial features hyper realistic and perfectly sharp, luminous skin with golden undertones, extremely detailed eyes reflecting radiant sunlight patterns she wears a structured couture gown made of layered golden panels, engraved with sunburst patterns, delicate filigree, and micro engraved ornamental details her crown expands outward into a massive radiant halo composed of geometric golden rays, each ray perfectly spaced in golden ratio intervals.
```
