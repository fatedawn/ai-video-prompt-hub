# Ultra Sharp Close-up Portrait of an Art Deco Queen

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to create an ultra-sharp close-up portrait of an Art Deco queen, featuring symmetrical composition based on the golden ratio, hyper-detailed facial structure, and metallic geometric patterns in gold, sapphire, and black that form a halo.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra sharp close up portrait of an art deco queen, symmetrical composition based on golden ratio, geometric patterns framing her face perfectly, hyper detailed facial structure with sharp cheekbones, perfectly lit skin, high clarity eyes reflecting light sources her outfit includes metallic geometric patterns in gold, sapphire, and black, with strong art deco lines extending outward like a halo.
```
