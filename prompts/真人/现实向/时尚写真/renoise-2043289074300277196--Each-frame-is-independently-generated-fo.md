# Each frame is independently generated for clothing. Do not maintain outfit…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Each frame is independently generated for clothing. Do not maintain outfit continuity between frames.

Ultra-realistic 4K cinematic video, 15s, 24fps.

CRITICAL: Lock identity — same female face in all frames (buzz cut, freckles, light eyes, same facial structure).
Do NOT change face, skin tone, or ethnicity. No face morphing, no identity drift.

A young woman walks toward the camera at normal speed, always facing forward, smooth backward tracking shot, subject centered.

Background morphs EXTREMELY FAST (every frame) through:
tropical beach → USA city streets → Shibuya Tokyo → European streets → Middle Eastern streets → suburban USA street (final).

Clothing changes EVERY FRAME from frame 1 (NO repetition, NO continuity, NO blending). Extreme flicker/strobe.

Outfits: WESTERN / GLOBAL MODERN ONLY (no Indian/traditional attire).
Include:
- streetwear (hoodies, baggy jeans, sneakers)
- hip-hop fashion (oversized fits, caps, layers)
- traveler outfits (jackets, boots, scarves)
- casual denim, athleisure, minimalist fashion
- modern modest wear (non-traditional)

No revealing clothing.

Enforce: per-frame outfit reset, disable temporal smoothing for clothing.

Subject remains perfectly stable; only clothes and background change.

END: tight close-up, she leans slightly forward smiling, wearing a black oversized hoodie and a dark baseball cap (like reference), suburban USA street, natural daylight, stable last 1–2 seconds.
```
