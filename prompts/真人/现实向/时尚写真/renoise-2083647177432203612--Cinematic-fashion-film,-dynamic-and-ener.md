# Cinematic fashion film, dynamic and energetic, 15 seconds, shot on ARRI Alexa…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic fashion film, dynamic and energetic, 15 seconds, shot on ARRI Alexa, bright harsh desert sunlight, deep blue sky, strong natural contrast, warm skin tones, linen fabric texture highly detailed.
Sequence based on the storyboard:
Start with a wide energetic shot of the woman walking and turning in the rocky desert landscape, the orange-and-white color-block linen dress flowing and lifting with every step and gust of wind. She moves freely, one arm raised, sunglasses on, fabric catching the air.
Cut to a close-up of her face in sunglasses as wind moves her hair across her cheek, then a quick detail of the dress fabric against the blue sky and rock.
Medium shot as she looks down and the dress moves with her body, followed by an extreme close-up of the textured linen (orange and white panels).
Profile close-up with wind in her hair, then a dynamic swirling shot where the dress flares and turns with her movement on the rocks.
Back view as she walks away, open back of the dress and hair moving in the wind.
Final shot: medium close-up, she turns toward camera, soft elegant pose with both hands near her face (fingertips lightly touching cheeks or framing the face), sunglasses still on, dress gently moving in the breeze, confident and free expression.
Camera is fluid and active — slow push-ins, subtle handheld energy, smooth tracking with the movement of the dress. Strong emphasis on how the linen follows every step, turn and breath of wind. Natural fabric physics, realistic wind interaction, high-end fashion commercial look.
Voiceover (calm, confident female voice, soft and clear):
“Freedom to move. Freedom to breathe. A dress that follows every step, every turn, every breath of the wind.”
```
