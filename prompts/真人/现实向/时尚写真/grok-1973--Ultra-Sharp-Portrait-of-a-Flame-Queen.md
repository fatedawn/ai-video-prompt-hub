# Ultra Sharp Portrait of a Flame Queen

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to create an ultra-sharp portrait of a flame queen, emphasizing hyper-realistic skin illuminated by fire glow, intense eye reflections, and attire that flows like semi-transparent, glowing fire.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra sharp portrait of a flame queen, golden ratio centered composition, hyper realistic skin illuminated by fire glow, intense eye reflections her attire flows like fire, glowing semi transparent layers.
```
