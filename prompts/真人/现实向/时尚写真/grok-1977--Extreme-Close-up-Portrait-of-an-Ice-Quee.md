Extreme close up portrait of an ice queen, hyper detailed skin with cool blue undertones, subtle frost textures delicately forming along the edges of her skin, ultra fine crystalline frost particles resting on her eyelashes, perfectly shaped eyes aligned with the golden ratio, slightly glossy natural lips with a cold reflective sheen, flawless facial symmetry, macro ultra sharp portrait lens capturing every pore and micro detail, icy blurred background filled with soft sparkling particles and frozen light fragments, cool blue cinematic lighting with sharp highlights and gentle shadows enhancing facial structure, ultra realistic rendering, shallow depth of field, frost particles slowly drifting in the air, perfectly still atmosphere, no breath vapor, no smoke, no fog from mouth, ultra clean cold environment, cinematic composition, 8k resolution, IMAX quality, ray tracing, global illumination, masterpiece, museum level artwork.
