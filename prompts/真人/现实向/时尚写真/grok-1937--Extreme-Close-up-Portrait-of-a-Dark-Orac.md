# Extreme Close-up Portrait of a Dark Oracle

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to create an extreme close-up portrait of a dark oracle, blending Baroque Gothic and Art Nouveau styles, focusing on golden ratio alignment, ultra-detailed skin with visible pores, and dramatic cinematic shadow contrast.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Extreme close up portrait of a dark oracle, baroque gothic art nouveau fusion, golden ratio alignment centered on eyes and lips, ultra detailed skin, visible pores, cinematic shadow contrast her makeup is dramatic but elegant, deep red lips, dark smoky eyes, glowing subtle highlights.
```
