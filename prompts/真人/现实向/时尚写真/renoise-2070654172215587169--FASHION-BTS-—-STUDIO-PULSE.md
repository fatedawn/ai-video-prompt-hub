```text
FASHION BTS — "STUDIO PULSE"

Reference: dark-haired female model, voluminous wavy black hair, black high-neck top, sharp cheekbones, high-fashion editorial look. Keep face and hair identical across all shots.

Global style: black & white editorial, high contrast, hard studio strobe lighting, ARRI Alexa 35, anamorphic lens, subtle film grain. Backstage photoshoot atmosphere — light stands, fan, monitor and crew elements visible. Rhythmic flash pops as transitions.

SHOT 02 — 0.8s — Over-the-shoulder of photographer holding camera, model in soft focus background striking a pose.
SHOT 03 — 1.0s — Slow-motion hair flip, strands fanning across frame, fan-blown wind, profile lit hard from the side.
SHOT 04 — 0.7s — Crew hand adjusts a softbox light stand, lens flare streak across frame.
SHOT 05 — 0.8s — Macro: makeup brush sweeps across cheekbone, eyes flick toward camera.
SHOT 06 — 1.0s — Model turns sharply toward lens, chin down, intense gaze, strobe flash.
SHOT 07 — 0.5s — Pure flash burst, near-white frame, silhouette of the pose.
SHOT 08 — 0.7s — Extreme close-up: single eye, lashes, micro-movement, catchlight from strobe.
SHOT 09 — 0.7s — Extreme close-up: lips parted, jawline, breath held.
SHOT 10 — 0.8s — Hands fix the black high collar, fingers framing the neck, low angle.
SHOT 11 — 1.2s — Medium shot, model sharp in focus at center frame, light stand and softbox softly out of focus behind her. Static locked-off camera, she holds an elegant pose.
SHOT 12 — 1.0s — Model takes one slow step toward camera, hair swaying, dolly-in.
SHOT 13 — 0.8s — Insert: photographer's monitor showing the just-shot B&W frame, hand scrolls.
SHOT 14 — 1.0s — Fan blows hair fully back, dramatic slow-motion, arms rising into pose.
SHOT 15 — 0.9s — Tight profile, edge-lit, model exhales, eyes close then open.
SHOT 16 — 0.8s — Sharp profile-to-front head turn, controlled and clean, hair settling around face, eyes landing directly on camera. Strobe flash on the turn.
SHOT 17 — 1.3s — Final pose locks, direct stare into camera, one last strobe FLASH, freeze on the look, cut to black.

AUDIO: Dark electronic, slow downtempo fashion runway beat.
