# Ultra Sharp Portrait of a Sorceress Wrapped in Flame Fabrics

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to generate an ultra-sharp portrait of a sorceress, featuring fabrics that resemble flames, golden ratio alignment, skin illuminated by a warm glow, and semi-transparent, glowing layers of attire.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra sharp portrait of a sorceress wrapped in flame like fabrics, golden ratio alignment of face and flame patterns her skin illuminated by warm glow, detailed reflections in eyes her attire flows like fire, semi transparent glowing layers.
```
