# Format: 16:9

## 提示词（English）

```text
Format: 16:9
Concept: "A Letter to My Future Self"
Create a realistic, self-recorded video diary filmed entirely on a modern smartphone using the front-facing camera. The footage should resemble a personal journal rather than a social media vlog. The camera remains handheld throughout, with natural hand movement, subtle stabilization typical of recent smartphones, realistic autofocus, accurate HDR exposure, occasional framing imperfections, and authentic ambient audio. No cinematic shots, transitions, or edits. The video is one continuous recording.
Visual Style
Natural smartphone video quality with crisp detail, realistic HDR, true-to-life colours, balanced exposure, soft natural daylight, and clean image processing. Avoid vintage filters, film grain, tape effects, excessive sharpening, or stylised colour grading. The result should look like genuine footage recorded on a flagship phone.
Character
MINJI, a Korean woman in her twenties with long black hair, light everyday makeup, and a calm, thoughtful personality. She wears a simple oversized knit sweater, relaxed jeans, and white sneakers.
Location
A peaceful botanical garden with walking paths, small ponds, wooden bridges, seasonal flowers, and quiet seating areas. A few visitors pass naturally in the distance without interacting with her.
The recording begins as MINJI smiles softly at the camera.
"I thought I'd record something for my future self today."
She strolls slowly through the garden while talking about small things she's grateful for, recent memories, and hopes for the coming year.
She occasionally turns the camera toward blooming flowers, ducks on the pond, or sunlight filtering through the trees before bringing it back to herself.
She pauses on a wooden bridge, resting her arms on the railing.
"I wonder if I'll remember this exact moment a year from now."
She continues walking, laughing quietly after spotting a squirrel running across the path.
Near the end of the walk, she sits on a bench overlooking the lake.
After a brief peaceful silence, she smiles warmly.
"If you're watching this in the future... I hope you're happy."
She waves gently, lowers the phone, and continues walking as the recording naturally ends.
Generation Requirements
Produce one uninterrupted recording with consistent facial identity, realistic expressions, accurate body movement, believable hand interactions, natural eye contact with the camera, physically correct lighting, smooth walking motion, and authentic environmental sound. Avoid jump cuts, visual glitches, duplicated objects, anatomy errors, or artificial movements. The final result should feel indistinguishable from a real smartphone video diary recorded by a real person.
```

地点
