# A hyper-realistic cinematic short video, 4K resolution, shot on a 35mm lens…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A hyper-realistic cinematic short video, 4K resolution, shot on a 35mm lens. The scene is set in a modern medical clinic with a perfect balance of soft warm lighting and clean clinical tones. The atmosphere is emotional, comforting, and heartwarming. Every detail must be highly realistic and error-free no distortions, no glitches, accurate anatomy, natural motion. This is a video generation prompt, so ensure smooth animation and perfect lip-sync.*

Character Details:
A kind female doctor wearing a clean white coat, with gentle expressions and caring eyes, treating the patient with precision and empathy.
A small fluffy kitten with ultra-detailed fur, expressive shining eyes filled with happiness and slight tears of joy, showing deep emotional connection.

The Narrative Action:
1. The doctor gently treats the kitten on a medical table with soft, careful movements.
2. The kitten slowly looks up at the doctor with sparkling, teary eyes full of gratitude.
3. Close-up on the kitten’s face as it softly says: “Thank you…” (perfect lip-sync required).
4. The doctor smiles warmly and gently pets the kitten, creating a wholesome emotional moment.

Technical Parameters:
24fps, cinematic warm color grading, shallow depth of field, soft shadows, ultra-realistic textures, smooth camera movement, emotional storytelling focus, no visual errors.*
```
