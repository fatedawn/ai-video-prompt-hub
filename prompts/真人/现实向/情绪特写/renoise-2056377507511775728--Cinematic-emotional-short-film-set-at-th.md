# Cinematic emotional short film set at the edge of a vast dark forest beside a…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic emotional short film set at the edge of a vast dark forest beside a lonely highway late at night. Cold blue moonlight filters through towering pine trees while mist drifts low across the roadside. The atmosphere feels quiet, melancholic, and deeply reflective — the kind of silence that exists far away from cities.

A lone wolf stands partially hidden at the forest edge.

Still.
Watching.
Cars rush past occasionally, headlights slicing through the darkness in brief flashes of white and red. The sound of tires on wet pavement echoes through the trees. Every passing vehicle illuminates the wolf for just a second before disappearing again into the night.

The wolf never moves.

Its fur stirs slightly in the wind.
Its eyes reflect the passing lights.
The forest behind it feels endless.

The tone is contemplative rather than threatening.
Time passes slowly.
Then one car in the distance begins to decelerate.

Its headlights sweep across the wolf as it carefully pulls onto the shoulder of the empty highway. Engine idling softly. Brake lights glowing red against the mist.

For a long moment, nothing happens.

Then the driver steps out.

A tired middle-aged traveler in a heavy jacket. Quiet. Calm. He closes the car door gently and stands beneath the dim roadside lights staring toward the wolf.

The wolf stares back.

No aggression.
No fear.
Just recognition.

The camera alternates between intimate close-ups:

the driver’s tired eyes
fog drifting between them
headlights illuminating falling mist

distant highway sounds fading away

Neither approaches.
Neither runs.
It feels like two lonely creatures briefly meeting between different worlds.

The wolf remains perfectly still.

The forest creaks softly in the wind.

Finally, the driver gives the smallest nod — almost respectful.

The wolf blinks once.

The driver quietly returns to the car.

He sits for a moment gripping the steering wheel, glancing once more toward the forest edge.

The wolf is still there.

The car slowly pulls back onto the highway and drives away into the darkness. Red taillights drift farther and farther down the empty road.

The wolf watches without moving until the lights completely disappear beyond the horizon.

Silence returns.

After several long seconds, the wolf slowly turns and walks back into the forest.

The trees swallow it completely.

Only darkness remains.
```

```text
树木将它完全吞没。
```
