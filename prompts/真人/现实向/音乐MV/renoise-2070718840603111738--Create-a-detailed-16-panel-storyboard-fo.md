# Create a detailed 16-panel storyboard for a 15-second cinematic K-pop dance…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a detailed 16-panel storyboard for a 15-second cinematic K-pop dance video featuring an original four-member girl group performing on a massive Coachella-inspired desert festival stage at sunset. Use a black, pink, gold, and white concert color palette. Keep the same four performers, same outfits, same hairstyles, same stage, same sunset desert background, and same high-budget K-pop concert style across all panels.
The performance should feel like a fierce headline festival chorus: sharp synchronized choreography, confident facial expressions, wind-blown hair, glossy stage reflections, LED graphics, spotlights, haze, crowd energy, and pyrotechnic accents.
Panel 1:
Wide low-angle stage shot. The four performers stand frozen in a diamond formation on the glossy black stage. Sunset sky, desert mountains, palm trees, LED screens, and a blurred festival crowd behind them.
Panel 2:
The chorus beat hits. All four snap their heads up and throw one arm outward in perfect synchronization. Pink LED lights flash behind them.
Panel 3:
Camera pushes toward the center performer. She steps forward half a step, eyes locked on camera, while the other three angle their shoulders outward.
Panel 4:
Full-body shot. The group performs a sharp hip-hop-inspired arm sequence: wrist flick, elbow hit, shoulder pop, then a clean pose.
Panel 5:
Side tracking shot from stage left. The dancers slide two steps to the right in formation, boots reflecting on the glossy stage floor.
Panel 6:
Medium close-up on the blonde performer. She turns her head sharply, ponytail whipping through warm sunset light, then hits a confident hand gesture.
Panel 7:
Wide shot. The group changes from diamond formation into a horizontal line across the stage. LED screens display abstract pink-gold light patterns, no text.
Panel 8:
Low-angle shot from near the stage floor. All four perform synchronized footwork, stepping forward and crossing one leg, hands cutting diagonally across the body.
Panel 9:
Camera cuts to the rose-pink-haired performer. She leads a quick body roll, then the others mirror the move behind her.
Panel 10:
Wide concert shot. Pyrotechnic sparks burst safely at both sides of the stage as the group hits a strong chorus pose.
Panel 11:
Camera orbits slightly around the group. They rotate positions: center performer moves back, rear performer moves forward, maintaining clean formation.
Panel 12:
Medium shot. All four perform a hair flip and sharp hand accent on the beat. Haze and spotlights create dramatic silhouettes.
Panel 13:
Full-stage shot with crowd energy. The dancers jump or perform a strong level change together, landing perfectly in sync.
Panel 14:
Slow-motion detail shot. Boots land on the reflective stage, confetti begins to fall, pink and gold lights ripple across the floor.
Panel 15:
The group returns to a tight diamond formation. The center performer comes forward again, all four raise their arms into a final build-up pose.
Panel 16:
Final hero frame. All four face the camera in a powerful ending pose, sunset blazing behind them, LED panels glowing pink and gold, crowd cheering in the background, pyrotechnic smoke framing the group.
```

```text
Panneau 7 :

Panneau 8 :

Panneau 9 :

Panneau 10 :

Panneau 12 :
Plan moyen. Les quatre exécutent un mouvement de cheveux et un accent de main net sur le rythme. La brume et les projecteurs créent des silhouettes dramatiques.
Panneau 13 :
Plan de scène complète avec l'énergie de la foule. Les danseuses sautent ou effectuent un changement de niveau fort ensemble, atterrissant parfaitement synchronisées.
Panneau 14 :
Plan détaillé au ralenti. Les bottes atterrissent sur la scène réfléchissante, des confettis commencent à tomber, les lumières roses et dorées ondulent sur le sol.
Panneau 15 :
Le groupe revient à une formation diamant serrée. L'interprète centrale avance à nouveau, les quatre lèvent les bras dans une pose finale de montée en puissance.
Panneau 16 :
Cadre final héroïque. Les quatre font face à la caméra dans une pose finale puissante, le coucher de soleil flamboyant derrière elles, les panneaux LED brillant en rose et or, la foule acclamant en arrière-plan, la fumée pyrotechnique encadrant le groupe.
```

```text
Painel 7:
Tiro amplo. O grupo muda de formação de diamante para uma linha horizontal no palco. Telas de LED exibem padrões abstratos de luz rosa-dourada, sem texto.
Painel 8:
Tiro em ângulo baixo próximo ao chão do palco. Todas as quatro executam passos sincronizados, avançando e cruzando uma perna, mãos cortando diagonalmente o corpo.
Painel 9:
A câmera corta para a performer de cabelo rosa-rosado. Ela lidera um rápido movimento de corpo, então as outras espelham o movimento atrás dela.
Painel 10:
Tiro de concerto amplo. Faíscas pirotécnicas explodem com segurança em ambos os lados do palco enquanto o grupo atinge uma pose forte de refrão.
Painel 11:
A câmera orbita levemente ao redor do grupo. Elas trocam de posição: a performer central se move para trás, a performer traseira avança, mantendo a formação limpa.
Painel 12:
Tiro médio. Todas as quatro fazem um movimento de cabelo e um acento de mão afiado no ritmo. Névoa e holofotes criam silhuetas dramáticas.
Painel 13:
Tiro de palco completo com energia da multidão. As dançarinas pulam ou fazem uma mudança de nível forte juntas, aterrissando perfeitamente em sincronia.
Painel 14:
Tiro de detalhe em câmera lenta. Botas aterrissam no palco reflexivo, confetes começam a cair, luzes rosa e douradas ondulam pelo chão.
Painel 15:
O grupo retorna a uma formação de diamante apertada. A performer central avança novamente, todas as quatro levantam os braços em uma pose final de construção.
Painel 16:
Quadro final heroico. Todas as quatro encaram a câmera em uma pose final poderosa, pôr do sol brilhando atrás delas, painéis de LED brilhando em rosa e dourado, multidão aplaudindo ao fundo, fumaça pirotécnica emoldurando o grupo.
```
