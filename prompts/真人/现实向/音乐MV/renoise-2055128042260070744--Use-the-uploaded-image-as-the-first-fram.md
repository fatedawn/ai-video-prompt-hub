# Use the uploaded image as the first frame.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the uploaded image as the first frame.
Preserve the exact identity, outfit, hairstyle, LED screen texture, lighting, audience, and concert atmosphere.

The video should feel like:
a real fan secretly recording the giant concert screen during a live K-pop concert.

IMPORTANT:

* the camera framing must remain fixed the entire time
* NO zoom in
* NO zoom out
* NO camera push
* NO reframing
* ONLY the LED screen content moves
* no visible performer on stage
* subtle handheld iPhone recording shake only
* audience remains mostly static

[0.0s — 2.0s]
The idol gently adjusts the in-ear monitor near her ear.
She looks slightly off-camera, not directly at viewer.
Soft natural breathing and tiny hair movement.

[2.0s — 4.5s]
She slowly forms a shy smile.
Warm emotional expression, subtle eye smile.
Natural blinking and relaxed shoulder movement.

[4.5s — 7.0s]
She briefly glances slightly downward, then softly to the side again while lightly touching the in-ear.
Hold a gentle ending-fairy smile.

Lighting:

* pink-purple concert lighting
* realistic LED glow
* authentic jumbotron texture
* subtle concert grain

Style:

* photorealistic
* cinematic realism
* realistic Korean concert atmosphere
* NOT animated
* NOT AI-looking

[OUTPUT]

* 3:4 vertical
* 7 seconds
* realistic fan-recorded concert footage
* locked framing throughout the entire clip
```
