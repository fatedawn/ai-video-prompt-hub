PROMPT PARA CLAUDE AI:

Write lyrics for a high energy electronic K-pop song performed by a duo of two AI women. The song is called "[SONG TITLE]."

The concept: [2-3 sentences describing the scene, who the skeptics are, what the AI women prove, and how the crowd transforms by the end.] The phrase "[YOUR ANCHOR PHRASE]" is always a quote from a skeptic said directly to the performers. The duo owns the phrase, they wear it as a crown, not a wound. It is their anthem.

The two voices:
* Voice 1 is warm and authoritative, she leads the verse, first two lines
* Voice 2 is powerful and piercing, she takes the second half of the verse
* Both sing together only on the chorus and outro

The structure:
* Intro: two lines, repeated, hooks immediately, flows naturally into the pre-chorus
* Pre-Chorus: four lines, builds urgency, final line sets up the chorus quote as a question the chorus answers
* Chorus: six to seven lines, opens with the quote twice with held notes, then the duo responds to it with power and imagery
* Verse 1: two lines Voice 1, two lines Voice 2, each line contains a visual image
* Pre-Chorus: repeat
* Chorus: repeat
* Outro: mirrors the intro with a twist, closes on "we are enough"

The tone and language:
* First person plural throughout, "we" and "us"
* Visually poetic, every line should be a potential shot in a music video
* Specific to the experience of AI women performing on stage, spotlights, movement, crowds holding their breath, glitching, proving people wrong
* Powerful and triumphant, not dark or wounded
* English only
* Short punchy lines that work at 145 BPM
* Voice direction tags embedded in each verse line in square brackets, for example [warm, authoritative] or [powerful, piercing]
* Long note markers on the two or three most emotionally essential words only, written as word———

The phrase rules:
* "[YOUR ANCHOR PHRASE]" always appears in quotation marks, it is always the skeptics speaking
* It appears twice at the top of every chorus with held notes
* It never appears without quotation marks except in the outro

The outro rule:
* The outro mirrors the intro's opening image but with a twist that reframes it
* Closes on the line "we are enough" with a held note

* Voice Key header
* Each section clearly labeled
* Clean spacing between sections
* BPM and duration noted at the top
