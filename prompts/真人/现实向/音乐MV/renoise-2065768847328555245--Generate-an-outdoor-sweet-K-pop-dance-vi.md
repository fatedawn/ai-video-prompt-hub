# Generate an outdoor sweet K-pop dance video based on the 16-panel storyboard…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Generate an outdoor sweet K-pop dance video based on the 16-panel storyboard and the character sheet.

Keep the same young adult East Asian female dancer, with the same face, hairstyle, body proportions, and outfit throughout the whole video.

She wears a light blue bandeau top and a matching light blue mini skirt, with white socks and white sneakers.

Scene: sunny rooftop / seaside terrace, blue sky, ocean view, bright natural light, light wind.

Follow the storyboard in order and keep the choreography dynamic:
power opening, walk-in, hair flip, side leap, drop & rise, hip hit, arm sweep, full spin, kick, lean-back wave, jump accent, low pose, rise, big open pose, high-leg accent, ending pose.

The motion should feel sweet, energetic, rhythmic, and clearly structured — not random shaking or repeated posing.
```
