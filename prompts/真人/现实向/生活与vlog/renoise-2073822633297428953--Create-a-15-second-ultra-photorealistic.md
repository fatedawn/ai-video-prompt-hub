# Create a 15-second ultra-photorealistic documentary-style video capturing…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second ultra-photorealistic documentary-style video capturing ordinary modern life in a quiet Japanese neighborhood. The video should feel like authentic everyday footage that naturally fascinates international viewers, especially those curious about life in Japan. Everything should appear spontaneous, unstaged, and emotionally warm.

Main Subject:
A young Japanese woman, 22–24 years old, natural everyday appearance, medium-length layered black hair loosely tied with soft face-framing bangs. She wears an oversized off-white cotton T-shirt, loose faded blue jeans, classic black Converse-style sneakers, a simple silver necklace, and carries a beige canvas tote bag. Healthy natural skin texture, minimal makeup, calm personality, warm genuine smile. Maintain identical identity, clothing, hairstyle, and facial features throughout the entire video.

Location:
A peaceful residential neighborhood in Tokyo, Japan, Spring 2026, around 9:00 AM. Narrow spotless streets, compact modern homes, bicycles, utility poles, vending machines, tiny gardens, flowering plants, neighborhood cats, railway crossing, small roadside shrines, trimmed hedges, maple trees, laundry drying on balconies, soft morning sunlight. No tourists, no shopping districts, no advertisements, no crowds.

Visual Style:
Ultra-realistic documentary footage.
Authentic early-2000s MiniDV home camcorder aesthetic.
Heavy handheld movement.
Natural camera shake.
Slightly imperfect framing.
Frequent autofocus hunting.
Exposure changes between sunlight and shade.
Rolling shutter.
CCD sensor bloom.
Soft faded consumer-video colors.
Subtle analog tape noise.
Occasional MiniDV glitches.
Mild compression artifacts.
No stabilization.
No cinematic camera moves.
No HDR.
No modern color grading.
Everything should feel like a forgotten family recording.

00:00–00:02
She stops at a tiny unmanned roadside flower stand, places coins into an honesty payment box, picks up a small bouquet, smiles naturally, and continues walking.

00:02–00:04
She buys a canned iced coffee from a vending machine standing alone on a quiet residential street. The camera briefly loses focus before recovering.

00:04–00:06
An elderly neighbor quietly sweeps the public sidewalk outside several homes. She politely bows while passing, and the neighbor smiles back.

00:06–00:08
She waits patiently at a neighborhood railway crossing with a few local residents. A commuter train passes as sunlight flickers through the trees.

00:08–00:10
A neighborhood cat sleeps peacefully beside an unlocked bicycle. She crouches, smiles softly, but lets the cat sleep without touching it.

00:10–00:12
She walks beneath quiet residential streets carrying her coffee and flowers while a gentle breeze moves her hair and hanging wind chimes ring softly.

00:12–00:15
She reaches a small bridge overlooking a narrow canal with koi fish below. She pauses, looks toward the camera with a warm genuine smile, gives a small wave, then walks away as the MiniDV recording abruptly cuts to black with a brief analog tape glitch.

Audio:
Only natural environmental sounds—morning birds, soft wind, rustling leaves, distant commuter train, railway crossing bells, bicycle wheels, footsteps, neighborhood ambience, faint wind chimes, subtle camcorder handling noise. No music. No narration. No sound effects.

Goal:
Create a deeply believable slice of everyday Japanese life that feels peaceful, authentic, nostalgic, and emotionally comforting. Every movement should appear completely natural, making viewers feel like they are watching a real forgotten MiniDV recording of an ordinary morning in Japan.
```
