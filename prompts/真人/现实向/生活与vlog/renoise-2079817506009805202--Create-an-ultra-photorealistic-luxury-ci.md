# Create an ultra-photorealistic luxury cinematic travel film featuring the same…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create an ultra-photorealistic luxury cinematic travel film featuring the same young beautiful Korean woman throughout. Preserve identical face, natural skin pores, realistic hair physics, soft almond eyes, subtle expressions, and perfect outfit continuity.

She wears a cream wool trench coat, beige knit sweater, ivory tailored trousers, white leather sneakers, a minimalist silver watch, and carries a taupe leather weekender bag.

Location: A real panoramic luxury train traveling through the Swiss Alps during blue hour into sunrise. Snow-covered pine forests, frozen lakes, old stone bridges, alpine villages, light winter fog, and warm golden sunlight.

Sequence:
0–2s: Helicopter-style aerial beside the moving train crossing snowy mountains.
2–4s: Camera naturally glides through the panoramic window into the cabin. She quietly watches the mountains as sunrise reflections move across the glass.
4–6s: DSLR macro of her fingertips touching the cold window. Condensation disappears beneath her fingers. Beautiful creamy bokeh.
6–9s: Stabilized handheld orbit as the train crosses a dramatic stone bridge above a turquoise river. Stunning natural parallax through the windows.
9–12s: The train stops at a hidden alpine station. She steps onto the snowy platform while the train slowly departs behind her.
12–15s: Long-lens shot of her walking beside a frozen lake toward a wooden cabin. Camera slowly cranes into a breathtaking aerial reveal of the Swiss Alps.

Camera: Sony FX3, Sony G Master lenses (24mm, 50mm, 85mm, 90mm Macro, 135mm). True full-frame DSLR look, natural handheld micro-shake, organic focus pulls, realistic motion blur, authentic exposure breathing, shallow depth of field.

Lighting: Natural sunrise, volumetric fog, HDR, realistic reflections.

Music: Emotional cinematic ambient score with soft felt piano, warm synth pads, subtle strings, gentle pulses, wordless female vocal textures, gradually building to the final aerial reveal.

Ambient: Train wheels, wind, birds, footsteps on snow.

Style: Netflix luxury travel documentary, 4K HDR, 24fps.

Negative: No CGI, no plastic skin, no AI artifacts, no oversaturation, no impossible camera moves, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```
