## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 150 BPM / 15 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Streetwear
ENVIRONMENT: Street
MOOD: Playful → stylish
STYLE: Beat-driven cinematic
CORE IDEA: Her actions create exaggerated sound effects
SHOT FLOW:
Step → bass hit
Turn → whoosh
Hand move → click
Walk synced with beat
She notices
Plays with movement
Builds rhythm
Full sequence
Stylish walk
Sync perfection
Final pose
```
