# 风向引导的街头漫步

*Wind Guided Street Walk*

> 这是一个为 Seedance 2.0 设计的结构化视频提示词，描述了主角在开阔街道上与风互动并跟随风向移动的场景序列。

## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 135 BPM / 13 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Casual
ENVIRONMENT: Open street
MOOD: Resistance → trust → flow
STYLE: Cinematic natural force
CORE IDEA: Wind guides her path
SHOT FLOW:
Walking normally
Strong wind hits
She resists
Moves opposite
Wind pushes harder
Stops
Changes direction
Wind softens
Follows it
Movement becomes smooth
Calm expression
Walk confident
End
```
