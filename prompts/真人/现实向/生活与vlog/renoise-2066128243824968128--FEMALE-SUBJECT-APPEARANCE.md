```text
FEMALE SUBJECT APPEARANCE:

Create an ultra-realistic female football travel vlogger with a cheerful, energetic personality.

Strict Identity Lock:

- Same female face throughout
- Same age throughout
- Same hairstyle throughout
- Same body proportions
- Same skin tone
- Same facial features
- No identity drift
- No face morphing
- No hairstyle changes
- Consistent appearance in every scene

WARDROBE:

Each location features a different stylish football-inspired female outfit while preserving the same identity:

- Argentina-inspired oversized football jersey with fitted jeans and white sneakers
- Brazil-inspired cropped football jacket with cargo pants and sneakers
- Mexico-inspired supporter jersey with relaxed-fit denim and comfortable trainers
- USA-inspired sporty streetwear outfit with football scarf
- Fashionable football fan dress paired with sneakers
- Casual supporter hoodie with athletic leggings
- Stadium-ready varsity jacket with jeans
- Trendy football-themed streetwear with crossbody bag
- National team scarf and supporter accessories
- Comfortable travel-vlogger outfits suitable for walking, dancing, celebrating, and stadium attendance

Accessories remain consistent and realistic throughout:

- Small crossbody bag
- Football supporter scarf
- Wristbands
- Stadium credentials where appropriate

Natural movement, realistic hair physics, authentic expressions, joyful reactions, cheering, singing, dancing, peace signs, thumbs-up, finger hearts, laughing with supporters, celebrating goals, and recording energetic selfie moments throughout the FIFA World Cup 2026 journey.
```
