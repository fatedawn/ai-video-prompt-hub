# Format: 16:9

## 提示词（English）

```text
Format: 16:9

Concept: “Walking Through Jantar Mantar”

The entire video is a single continuous self-filmed vlog captured on an early-2000s MiniDV consumer camcorder. It feels like genuine personal footage rather than professional journalism. The camera is always handheld with natural arm movement, slight walking bounce, imperfect framing, autofocus hunting, minor exposure shifts, and authentic human timing. No cuts or transitions.

Visual Style

Authentic MiniDV aesthetic: soft detail, light digital noise, tape compression, muted contrast, accurate skin tones, gentle highlight bloom, interlaced DV texture, built-in stereo microphone, no cinematic grading, no stabilization, no beauty filters.

Character

A young Indian creator in casual streetwear with a backpack, calmly documenting the surroundings without participating in the demonstration.

Location

Jantar Mantar, New Delhi, during the evening. A large peaceful student protest with handmade placards about education and exams, volunteers distributing water, journalists, TV cameras, police barricades, Indian flags, portable speakers, and a respectful, energetic atmosphere.

⸻

The recording begins outside the entrance.

The creator walks in, turning the camera toward the gathering.

“I wasn’t expecting this many people.”

The camera slowly captures peaceful demonstrators, volunteers handing out water, media interviews, and students listening to speeches.

“Everyone seems really organized.”

A volunteer offers a bottle of water.

“Thank you.”

The creator continues walking, passing TV reporters filming live and photographers documenting the event.

“Looks like every news channel is here.”

As evening sets in, the lights, crowd, and city ambience blend together. An announcement says the venue will close soon.

The creator looks around one last time.

“I think it’s time to head back.”

Walking toward the exit, they turn the camera back to themselves.

“I’m glad I came to see this for myself. I’ll definitely remember today.”

They smile, wave goodbye, step back onto the Delhi street, and the arcade-like crowd ambience fades naturally into evening traffic as the recording ends.

Generation Requirements

One continuous uninterrupted take. Stable identity, realistic handheld MiniDV footage, authentic autofocus hunting, physically correct walking, natural crowd interactions, consistent lighting, genuine ambient audio, no duplicated people, no warped anatomy, no AI artifacts, no scene changes, and no unnatural motion. The final result should be indistinguishable from a real personal MiniDV vlog documenting a peaceful public gathering.
```
