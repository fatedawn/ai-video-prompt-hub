# NO MUSIC, sound effects only, use  image1 as room reference for people and…

## 提示词（English）

```text
NO MUSIC, sound effects only, use  image1 as room reference for people and position not the first shot, In the rustic, dimly lit room  filled with heat, mud, and anger, farmers, hunters, mothers, shepherds, the priest, the butcher, voices clashing between villagers. Multiple 35mm cinematic shots. The flickering stone fireplace casts shadows on their faces as tension mounts, creating a powerful and raw atmosphere directed by an award-winning filmmaker. make sure to have these dialogues from the villagers:
Farmer: Those sheep are dowries. Rent. Winter meat. You speak of them like wool with legs.
Priest: There are older laws than hunger.
Woman: Then ask your older laws to feed my children.
```
