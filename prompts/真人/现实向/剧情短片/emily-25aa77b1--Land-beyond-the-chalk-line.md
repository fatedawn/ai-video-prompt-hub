# Land beyond the chalk line

## 提示词（English）

```text
Hold a wide eye-level view of an adult dancer in loose orange trousers beside a single white chalk line across a sunlit courtyard. At normal speed, the dancer takes two quick sideways steps, hops across the line and lands with both feet on the far side, arms opening into a broad smile. Keep the whole body and chalk line visible throughout. Sound: shoe scuffs, the landing and a short laugh. Hold the final stance long enough to see both feet settle.
```
