# Keep the tin label facing us

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use @Image1 as the product and opening-frame reference. Preserve the tin body, front label, colors and composition. Keep the body fixed while one hand lifts the removable lid straight upward and holds it just above the rim, leaving the front label unobscured. Camera stays still in the reference's front three-quarter view. Preserve the reference lighting. Sound: a quiet lid release and room tone. End with the lid held above the open tin and the label facing the viewer.
```
