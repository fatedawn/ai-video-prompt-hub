## 提示词（English）

```text
Live-action cinematic sequence. An attractive younger woman in a fitted neutral dress enters the dining room from the hallway and stops halfway down the long table. The elderly man remains seated at the head, not looking up. The camera slowly pushes in from the far end of the table. She pulls out a chair but does not sit. Silence stretches. We cut to a medium close-up of her. She says evenly: “You said seven.” He finally looks up. Calmly: “It’s six fifty-eight.” Cut to black.
```
