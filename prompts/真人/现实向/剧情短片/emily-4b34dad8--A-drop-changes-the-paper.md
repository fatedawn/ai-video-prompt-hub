# A drop changes the paper

## 提示词（English）

```text
Hold a close overhead view of a plain cream paper sheet on a gray surface. One clear water drop falls into the center and forms a small dark patch that spreads unevenly through the visible paper fibers. Keep a broad dry border in frame, with the sheet and camera still. Soft side light shows the texture without glare. Sound: one small drop impact and quiet room tone. End on the wet patch as its edge slows; no labels, graphs or additional droplets.
```
