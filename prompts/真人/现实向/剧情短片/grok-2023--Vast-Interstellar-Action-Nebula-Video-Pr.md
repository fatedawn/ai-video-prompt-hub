# Vast Interstellar Action Nebula Video Prompt

> A highly detailed Grok Imagine prompt for generating a video of a vast, action-scale interstellar nebula with vibrant colors, dramatic lighting, star clusters, and a heroic starship banking through the scene.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Vast interstellar action space nebula, an enormous swirling cosmic cloud of vibrant magenta, electric cyan, deep violet and fiery gold nebulae gases stretching across infinite void, dramatic volumetric lighting with intense god rays piercing through dense dust pillars and glowing ionized hydrogen, massive action-scale star clusters embedded like jewels with bright blue supergiants flaring, dynamic energy tendrils and plasma arcs crackling with raw power, foreground shattered asteroid fragments tumbling in zero gravity catching neon reflections, midground sleek black silhouette of a heroic starship banking hard through the nebula with glowing blue engine trails cutting dramatic arcs, background distant spiral galaxy arms and ancient pulsar beacons pulsing
```
