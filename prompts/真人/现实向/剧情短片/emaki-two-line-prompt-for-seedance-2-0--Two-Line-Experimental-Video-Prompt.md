# Two-Line Experimental Video Prompt

> This extremely short experimental prompt is closer to a one-line idea than a full direction; add one line specifying the scene or subject when you need a targeted image.

## 提示词（English）

```text
This was a 2 line prompt in seedance 2. If the hollywood is cooked guys are right maybe the hollywood is cooked guys are cooked too idk.
```
