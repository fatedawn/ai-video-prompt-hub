# Jesus vs. Devil Arm Wrestling Video Prompt

> A dramatic Grok Imagine prompt depicting Jesus Christ and the devil arm wrestling, with Jesus winning, set against a background of heaven and hell, concluding with the devil burning to hell.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Jesus Christ vs the devil, arm wrestling, Jesus Christ is winning, heaven and hell in the background, burn the devil to hell at the end
```
