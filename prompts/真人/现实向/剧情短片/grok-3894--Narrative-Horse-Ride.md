# Narrative Horse Ride

> A multi-scene narrative prompt involving a horse ride, a cheering crowd, and a prisoner.

## 提示词（English）

```text
She rides on a horse.  A crowd waves and cheers.  The prisoner is in jail watching her leave.
```
