# Cinematic Cosmic Panorama Video Prompt

> A detailed Grok Imagine prompt for generating a cinematic, ultra-wide video panorama of deep space, featuring a razor-sharp horizon, a detailed full moon, and swirling, vibrant nebulae with dramatic lighting effects.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic ultra-wide panorama from deep space, razor-sharp perfect horizon slicing the frame exactly in half, breathtaking scene of cosmic grandeur, the majestic full moon positioned just inside the horizon in the lower half with hyper-detailed cratered silver surface catching dramatic side lighting, above the horizon an explosive tapestry of vibrant swirling nebulae in rich crimson reds, electric teals, deep indigos and molten golds, countless pinpoint stars and distant galaxies glittering across the infinite black void, powerful volumetric and crepuscular beams slicing through cosmic dust clouds, subtle anamorphic lens flares and delicate bokeh highlights, immense scale and majestic silence, core emotional tone of tranquil wonder, as if rising into the nebulae sea,
```
