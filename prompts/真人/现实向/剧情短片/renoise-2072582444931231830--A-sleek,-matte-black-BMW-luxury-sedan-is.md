# A sleek, matte black BMW luxury sedan is parked in a dark, atmospheric studio…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A sleek, matte black BMW luxury sedan is parked in a dark, atmospheric studio. Tendrils of white smoke curl around the base of the vehicle and its large multi-spoke wheels, giving it a dramatic, mysterious look.
The camera transitions to a rear three-quarter angle of the car as smoke gently drifts around it. The text on the screen reads: Some things don't ask for attention.
A close-up shot focuses on the spinning front wheel with a prominent blue and white BMW logo at its center, showcasing the car's intricate, dark-alloy rim design amidst swirling smoke.
The focus shifts to the sharp, modern LED taillight of the vehicle as it illuminates into a bright red glow. The text on the screen changes to: They command it.
A close-up reveals the sleek front headlight assembly, which lights up dynamically with bright white daytime running lights.
A macro shot focuses on a textured carbon fiber component of the car. The text reads: Engineered in silence.
The camera pans across the detailed pattern of the dark-finished multi-spoke wheel and high-performance tire.
The setting shifts outdoors. The matte black BMW is shown driving smoothly along a wet coastal road during twilight, with ocean waves crashing against rocks in the background. The text on the screen reads: Then it moves.
The vehicle is back in the studio, viewed from a front three-quarter angle under a stark spotlight. A lock icon appears above the text: Book a private viewing. and a final luxury emblem logo finishes the scene.
```
