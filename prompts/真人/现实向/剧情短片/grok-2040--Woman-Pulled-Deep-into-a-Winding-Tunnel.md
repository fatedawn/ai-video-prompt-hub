# Woman Pulled Deep into a Winding Tunnel

> A detailed Grok Imagine prompt describing a scene where the camera tracks behind a woman being pulled deep into a tunnel by a violent current of water, struggling to stay above water, with specific negative instructions.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Scene Camera: Tracks behind the woman.
Action: The violent current of water pulls the woman deep into the tunnel, making sharp left and right turns, she struggles to stay above water.
Negative Prompt: No music
```
