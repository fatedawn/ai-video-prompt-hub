# Tank-Bike Transformation Video Prompt

> A complex, multi-stage video prompt for Grok Imagine detailing a cinematic transition. It starts from an initial image description (which is not the prompt) and then describes the video sequence: a man on a futuristic motorcycle transforming into a heavily armored figure on a weaponized tank-bike hybrid, culminating in an intense action scene.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Starting from the image of the man on the tank-bike, the camera tracks forward at a high speed. The motorcycle's rear tracks churn against the wet asphalt, kicking up spray and thick white smoke. The man grips the handlebars tightly, leaning into the wind. The background city blurs with motion.

mechanical armor plates begin to manifest on the rider's back. A glowing red circular lens ignites on the front of the motorcycle’s chassis, resembling a robotic eye. The bike's engine glows with internal red heat as it accelerates through the foggy city.

rider has back armor, heavy mechanical exoskeleton limbs snap into place over his arms and shoulders. The rider raises his arms as the suit locks in. A large Gatling gun barrel emerges from the right side of the motorcycle. Sparking metal and shifting gears are visible.

a sleek black robotic helmet snaps shut over the man’s head, completely hiding his face. The motorcycle’s front end expands, and a second Gatling gun barrel rotates into position. The rider hunches forward, becoming one with the machine.

his entire body is now encased in heavy, futuristic black armor plating. The bike grows larger and more aggressive, with multiple gun ports opening. The camera pans slightly to the side to show the immense scale of the tank-bike hybrid as it thunders down the road.

The Gatling guns on the bike begin to spin rapidly and fire continuous streams of orange fire and tracer bullets. Rockets launch from pods at the rear, leaving smoke trails. The environment erupts in explosions and fire as the bike speeds through the warzone. High-octane action, camera shakes, intense muzzle flashes, cinematic destruction.
```
