# Cinematic wide shot of an ancient wooden sailboat gliding across a calm, glassy…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic wide shot of an ancient wooden sailboat gliding across a calm, glassy lake during a breathtaking golden hour sunrise. Soft mist hangs over the distant rolling hills and mountains. A man with long dark hair and a beard, dressed in biblical-era robes, steers the boat while looking ahead with a serene expression. Beside him, a woman with long dark hair smiles warmly as they converse. The camera transitions to close-ups of them stepping off the boat onto a rocky shore, walking hand-in-hand along a rugged coastline.
Later, the scene shifts to a desolate, rocky desert landscape where a distressed man in tattered clothes sits shackled in heavy iron chains, looking up in despair. The robed man and woman approach him, kneeling down to touch his chained hands with compassion. The chains fall away, and they embrace in deep emotional relief. The man opens an ancient leather-bound book, reading together under the warm sunset light.
```
