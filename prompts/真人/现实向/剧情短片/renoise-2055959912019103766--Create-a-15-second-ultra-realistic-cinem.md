Storyboard:

0s – Golden sunrise over a calm ocean, cinematic drone shot with warm reflections on the water.

2s – Multiple fishing boats sail into the open sea with cinematic camera tracking shots.
3s – Massive waves crash against the boats, realistic water splashes and dramatic ocean movement.

10s – Boats struggle through powerful waves with realistic storm motion and rain splashes.
11s – Storm slowly clears and sunlight breaks through the clouds beautifully.
12s – Boats return toward the harbor during a cinematic orange sunset.
13s – Fishermen unload fresh fish at the dock while people gather around naturally.
14s – Night harbor atmosphere with glowing lights reflecting on calm water and resting boats.
15s – Final cinematic aerial shot of the endless ocean and boats fading into the horizon emotionally.

Realistic ocean waves and sea ambience
Boat engine sounds and water splashes
Seagulls flying overhead naturally
Wind atmosphere and storm thunder effects

Peaceful ocean ambience at ending
