# Grok Imagine Prompt: Closet Door Revealing Infinite Void

> A detailed prompt for Grok Imagine describing a slightly cracked open closet door that has swung wide open on its own, revealing an impossibly endless dark void beyond, emphasizing that there are no magical effects or expansion, just an unnaturally deep and infinite darkness inside the closet as if it breathes and yawns.

## 提示词（English）

```text
A slightly cracked open closet door that has swung wide open on its own, revealing an impossibly endless dark void beyond, no magical effects or expansion, just an unnaturally deep and infinite darkness inside the closet as if it breathes and yawns o...
```
