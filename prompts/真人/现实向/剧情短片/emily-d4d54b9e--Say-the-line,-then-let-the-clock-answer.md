# Say the line, then let the clock answer

## 提示词（English）

```text
Hold a medium close-up of an adult repairer seated beside a small brass clock on a workbench. Keep the face unobscured and the head steady. Looking toward the clock, the repairer says clearly, "That's the first time it's kept time." The hands rest on the bench during the line. After speaking, the repairer smiles openly and listens. Soft bench-lamp light. Sound: the exact spoken line once, quiet room tone and the clock ticking through the final hold; no music or additional voice.
```
