# Turn this complete 10-frame storyboard into a smooth, high-quality 15–20 second…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Turn this complete 10-frame storyboard into a smooth, high-quality 15–20 second product ad video.

Style: Premium cinematic, natural park sunlight, empowering motivational mood.

Motion: Realistic confident walking stride, good bounce in the cushioning, natural arm swing, light wind in trees.

Details: Show excellent grip on trail, sparkle highlights on the lavender glitter sole, comfortable stride.

Camera: Smooth tracking shots, dynamic angles, subtle slow-motion on key foot steps.

Audio: Upbeat motivational instrumental music + subtle nature sounds (footsteps on dirt, birds).

End with elegant text overlay: “Ryka Trail Walkers – Comfort in Every Step”

Keep the woman and shoe details consistent throughout. 16:9, high quality.
```
