# 电影级刺客飞刀投掷

*Cinematic Assassin Knife Throw*

> 一个 15 秒电影级片段的结构化提示词，讲述了一名刺客在昏暗走廊中进行精准飞刀投掷的故事。

## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 130 BPM / 11 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Assassin style
ENVIRONMENT: Dim corridor
MOOD: Silent → precise
STYLE: Clean assassin cinematic
CORE IDEA: One perfect throw
SHOT FLOW:
Enemy unaware
She steps quietly
Pulls knife
Measures distance
Breath control
Throws
Knife spin slow motion
Impact
Enemy drops
She walks forward
End
```
