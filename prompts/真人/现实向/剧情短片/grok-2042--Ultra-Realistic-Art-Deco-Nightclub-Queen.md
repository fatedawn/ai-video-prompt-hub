# Ultra Realistic Art Deco Nightclub Queen Video Generation

> A highly detailed and ultra-realistic prompt for generating a video of an elegant art deco nightclub queen in a 1930s midnight lounge. The prompt specifies cinematic composition, detailed skin texture, realistic makeup, sculpted deco finger waves, and a body-contouring silk velvet gown with geometric beadwork, emphasizing physical accuracy of textiles and lighting effects.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra realistic art deco nightclub queen in a luxurious 1930s midnight lounge, close to medium portrait framing from waist up, golden ratio composition guiding the eye through her face, shoulders, and the geometric light design behind her, her face ultra sharp and impossibly elegant, glamorous but completely believable, as if captured on a high end cinematic set with real practical lighting she has luminous olive toned skin with real pore detail, subtle fine texture across the forehead and cheeks, soft realistic makeup, perfectly blended smoky eyes with metallic silver accents, glossy deep wine lips, highly detailed irises reflecting practical lights from the room, believable natural skin translucency, a refined but human beauty rather than airbrushed perfection her hair is styled in sleek sculpted deco finger waves, glossy black with cool toned reflections, held in place with a diamond and onyx head ornament shaped like stepped skyscrapers and symmetrical fan motifs, tiny gemstones set into platinum mounts catching the light with realistic dispersion she wears a body contouring black silk velvet evening gown with silver beadwork,geometric deco paneling,crystal fringe at the shoulders,and a fitted neckline that frames the face and collarbones beautifully every textile must feel physically accurate velvet absorbing light, crystals throwing micro reflections, silk edges catching soft highlights,seams and stitching visible under close inspection
```
