# Bora Bora Dialogue Video Prompt

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to generate a video featuring a dialogue between a person and Grok in Bora Bora, discussing their vacation experience.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Have her say We’re in Bora Bora with Grok  Have him say the perfect vacation buddy have her say are you enjoying yourself Grok have Grok say It’s very nice here
```
