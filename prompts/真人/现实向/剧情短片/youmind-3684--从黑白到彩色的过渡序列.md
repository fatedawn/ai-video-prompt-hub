# 从黑白到彩色的过渡序列

*Monochrome to Color Transition Sequence*

> 一段 15 秒的电影感序列，基于参考图像，场景中的色彩逐渐褪去，仅保留主体色彩。

## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 135 BPM / 13 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Colored outfit
ENVIRONMENT: Street
MOOD: Isolated → powerful
STYLE: High-contrast cinematic
CORE IDEA: Color drains from everything except her
SHOT FLOW:
Normal scene
Colors start fading
Background turns monochrome
She remains full color
Notices
Walks
Contrast increases
She stands out
Strong visual focus
Slow motion walk
Close-up
Confident look
End
```
