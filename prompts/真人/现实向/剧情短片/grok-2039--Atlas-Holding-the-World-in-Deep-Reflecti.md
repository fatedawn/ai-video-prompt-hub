# Atlas Holding the World in Deep Reflection

> A detailed Grok Imagine prompt describing a scene where Atlas is sculpted in highly polished steel, holding the world in his hands instead of on his shoulders, reflecting deeply, which turns into disappointment.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Imagine Atlas sculpted in highly polished steel. Instead of holding the weight of the world on his shoulders, he is holding the world in his hands. He looks at the world in deep reflection which turns into disappointment…
```
