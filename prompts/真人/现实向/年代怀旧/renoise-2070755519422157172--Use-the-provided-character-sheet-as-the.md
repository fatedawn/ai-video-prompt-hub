# Use the provided character sheet as the sole reference for Amphifa. Preserve…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the provided character sheet as the sole reference for Amphifa. Preserve the mascot exactly across every shot. Never redesign, stylize or reinterpret the character.

Use the provided cardboard protest sign as the sole reference whenever it appears. Treat it as the exact same physical prop in every shot. Preserve its cardboard texture, folds, worn edges, paint strokes, lettering layout and imperfections exactly.

Create a 15-second archival documentary montage made of 7 hard-cut shots (≈2 seconds each, final shot 1 second). Every cut jumps to another decade, making it appear Amphifa has quietly participated in major moments of American protest history for over seventy years.

The shot should initially read as a still photograph for the first second, with the subtle natural movements revealing themselves progressively.

Every shot must feel filmed by a different camera operator. Assemble the sequence like unrelated archival reels.

Authentic found footage only: handheld 16mm news film, occasional TV news cameras, amateur documentary footage depending on the decade. Heavy organic grain, gate weave, exposure breathing, faded emulsion, dust, scratches, film jitter, slight focus inconsistencies. No digital

1955 — Bus: 50 mm. Medium close handheld from another passenger. Amphifa stands awkwardly in the aisle holding the sign.

1957 — Little Rock: 35 mm. Rough lateral tracking beside students. Amphifa quietly follows carrying the sign.

1963 — March on Washington: 24 mm wide angle. Huge crowd, Lincoln Memorial in the distance. Amphifa appears among thousands of protesters.

1963 JFK funeral: 135 mm telephoto through spectators. Heads briefly obscure the frame. Amphifa is seen only for a moment.

1965 — Selma: Slow backward tracking matching the march. Amphifa walks naturally within the crowd.

Early 1970s — Kent State: Very close handheld shoulder camera. Students rush past. Amphifa struggles to keep pace, his foam costume bouncing naturally.

Present day — Lincoln Memorial: Perfectly centered locked-off tripod shot. Gentle breeze. Amphifa stands alone facing camera with the same sign. Hold one second, then hard cut to black.

Amphifa behaves with complete sincerity. Nobody reacts to the mascot.

The historical events remain the focus; Amphifa simply exists inside history as if he had always been there.
```
