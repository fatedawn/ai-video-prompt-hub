# 1970s disco nightclub, shot on 35mm film. A group of six dancers, three men and…

## 提示词（English）

```text
1970s disco nightclub, shot on 35mm film. A group of six dancers, three men and three women, all with large voluminous afros, dressed in vibrant sequined jumpsuits, bell-bottom pants, and platform high heels, dancing energetically on a glowing multicolored light-up dance floor. A mirrored disco ball spins overhead, casting rotating shards of golden and pink light across the room. The dancers move in perfect sync to a funky disco beat, hips swaying, arms pointing skyward in classic Saturday Night Fever poses. Midway through, the couples smoothly swap partners with playful spins and hand-offs, laughing and smiling with pure joy. Camera starts wide capturing the full group, then dollies in and orbits around the dancers as they twirl. Warm saturated color palette, lens flares from stage lights, light haze in the air, film grain, anamorphic lens. Energetic, joyful, groovy atmosphere. Music-synced movement, dancers stepping and clapping on the beat. 15 seconds, 24fps, cinematic realism.
```
