# Create a seamless 15-second photorealistic live-action cinematic comedy video.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a seamless 15-second photorealistic live-action cinematic comedy video.

REFERENCE USAGE:

Image 1 = storyboard reference.
Use it only for shot order, pose flow, camera logic, pacing, and comedy timing.
Do NOT recreate the grid, borders, captions, numbers, or any text.

Image 2 = strict woman identity reference.
Keep the same real woman throughout: long straight black hair, bright blue eyes, fair skin, slim athletic body, delicate facial structure.
She wears the same black athletic sports bra / crop top and black leggings.

Image 3 = strict cat identity reference.
Keep the same real fluffy white long-haired cat with pale green eyes, pink nose, soft natural fur, cute but mischievous personality.
The cat must stay fully white and fluffy.

STYLE:
Photorealistic live-action look, real human, real cat, natural skin texture, realistic hair movement, realistic fur detail, cinematic commercial quality, warm cozy apartment, soft morning sunlight, natural body movement, subtle comedy, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, realistic camera lens, no cartoon style, no 3D animation look, no illustration, no anime, no exaggerated facial features.

SETTING:
Bright cozy yoga room with wooden floor, large sunlit window, sheer curtains, plants, wall shelves, cat tree near the window, and a yoga mat.

ACTION FLOW:

0:00–0:02
The woman kneels and opens the yoga mat in one smooth motion. The mat rolls out perfectly. She smiles peacefully, relaxed and happy.

0:02–0:03.5
She begins yoga in a calm floor pose, focused and serene. Warm sunlight fills the room.

0:03.5–0:04.5
Cut to the cat near the window / cat tree. Close-up on the cat’s real eyes and face. It suddenly notices her and gives a subtle mischievous look.

0:04.5–0:06
The cat jumps down, runs toward her, and climbs onto her back while she is in a low yoga pose. She tries to stay calm, but her smile starts to break.

0:06–0:07.5
She moves into a downward dog / plank-like pose. The cat slips underneath her body.

0:07.5–0:09
Important shot:

Low floor-level camera from directly in front of the woman, very close to the ground. The cat is directly under her, lifting its head and looking up. The woman looks down at the cat. Their faces are very close, almost nose-to-nose. Focus on both natural facial expressions. The cat gently touches her face with its paw. She laughs and loses concentration.

0:09–0:10.5
She stands up and tries to reset. She moves into a one-leg balance pose / tree pose with hands together, trying to stay focused.

0:10.5–0:12.5
The cat walks around her standing foot, rubs against her ankle, and steps over her toes naturally.

0:12.5–0:14
She wobbles dramatically but realistically, arms swinging as she tries to keep balance. Then she falls softly onto the yoga mat in a funny, harmless way.

0:14–0:15
The cat climbs onto her back and lies down peacefully like it has won. The woman laughs, relaxes, and accepts defeat. End with a warm cozy shot of both resting together.

CAMERA:
Realistic cinematic camera movement, gentle handheld feel, natural close-ups, low floor-level cat POV moments, realistic reaction shots, no hard chaotic cuts.

IMPORTANT:
Photorealistic live-action only.
Real woman, real cat, real apartment.
Do not recreate the storyboard layout.
Make it one continuous realistic cinematic short, not a slideshow.
```
