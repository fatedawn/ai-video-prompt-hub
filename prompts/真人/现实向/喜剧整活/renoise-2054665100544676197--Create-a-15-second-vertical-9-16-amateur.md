# Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 amateur smartphone video of a fail caught on…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 amateur smartphone video of a fail caught on camera. At the top of the screen, include the white text: "Bro actually fell through the roof😭💀". A shirtless young man wearing light-colored trousers climbs over a leafy balcony railing and drops onto a slanted, red and grey corrugated roof. Bright sunny daylight. He carefully walks across the brittle roof toward a small blue ball. As he squats down to pick up the ball, the roof panel directly beneath him suddenly shatters and breaks. He violently falls completely through the roof, disappearing into a dark rectangular hole, while the blue ball rolls away. The camera is handheld, tracking him slightly, with a flinch when the roof breaks. Audio includes casual background chatter, hollow footsteps on the roof, and a loud, dramatic crash when the roof gives way. Keep it fast-paced to fit exactly 15 seconds.
```
