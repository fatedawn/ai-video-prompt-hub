# Pencil Sketch Woman Holding a Vivid Moon

> A Grok Imagine prompt for generating a video of a pencil-sketched woman, maintaining the same style, but instead of a heart, she holds a vividly colored orange and gold moon, with her expression full of wonder and her eyes gently closed.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Same pencil sketch woman, same style,
instead of a heart she holds a moon
that is the only colored element,
vivid orange and gold moon bright on her fingers,
her expression full of wonder, eyes closedown gently.
```
