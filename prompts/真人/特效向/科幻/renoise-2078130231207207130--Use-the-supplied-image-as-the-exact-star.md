# Use the supplied image as the exact start frame. A single continuous shot from…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the supplied image as the exact start frame. A single continuous shot from a funny 1990s live-action sci-fi thriller. Preserve the woman, both young men, cybernetic rikidsg, CRT monitors, costumes, and practical set exactly. Real actors, practical effects, subtle 35mm grain, no CGI look.

The attractive 45-year-old blonde woman remains intensely serious and confidently alluring. Her mechanical visor flickers with green data while cables pulse and the machinery hums. She slowly turns toward the two young men and says in a low, dramatic techno-thriller voice:

WOMAN: “Time to JACK. IN. Then do some Claude-rotting on a Fable Burn.”

She strongly emphasizes “JACK. IN.” with a deliberate pause between the words.

The two men stare silently for one beat, then exchange a confused sideways glance. The first man suppresses a childish laugh:

MAN 1: “Heh-heh… she said ‘jack in.’”

The second man looks back at her, completely deadpan:

MAN 2: “Lady, we have Wi-Fi now.”

The woman remains stone-faced as her visor emits an  electronic beep-boop. She is not phased and continues on her mission with urgency

Camera: slow, subtle push-in; no cuts.
Music: tense 1990s techno-thriller synth that stops abruptly after the final line.
SFX: CRT buzz, cooling fans, cable servos, quiet computer beeps.
```
