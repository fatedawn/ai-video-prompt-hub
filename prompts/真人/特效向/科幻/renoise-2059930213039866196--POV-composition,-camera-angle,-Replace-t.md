# POV composition, camera angle, Replace the wooden bridge and water environment…

## 提示词（English）

```text
POV composition, camera angle, Replace the wooden bridge and water environment with an ultra-modern skybridge suspended between two massive futuristic skyscrapers. The bridge should feel sleek, high-tech, and architectural, with metal and glass materials. Create an extreme sense of vertigo and dangerous height, with a huge empty void below and the city visible far beneath, emphasizing the dizzying drop. Surround the scene with towering glass buildings on both sides, making it feel like the person is crossing between two spectacular high-rise structures. Preserve the immersive downward perspective, realistic lighting, and cinematic realism. Make the atmosphere intense, dramatic, and visually striking, with a strong feeling of altitude, tension, and modern urban scale.
```
