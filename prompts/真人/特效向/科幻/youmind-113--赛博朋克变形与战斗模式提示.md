# 赛博朋克变形与战斗模式提示

*Cyberpunk Transformation Video Prompt for Seedance 2.0*

> 一个详细的多场景提示，用于 Seedance 2.0 生成一个竖屏（9:16）赛博朋克视频，内容为一位女性主角从黑暗车库中的伪装形象，蜕变为雨中屋顶上身着未来感盔甲、准备战斗的天使。

## 提示词（中文）

```text
[风格] 未来科技、赛博朋克、人像 (9:16)、电影特效、慢动作升格、酷帅。
[时长] 12 秒
[主角] 一位眼神坚毅的年轻女性。
[场景] 昏暗的地下车库 -> 霓虹闪烁的未来都市屋顶。

[00:00-00:04] 镜头 1：伪装。
场景：昏暗、烟雾弥漫的地下车库。
主角：身穿一件朴素的黑色连帽衫，帽子戴着，戴着黑色面罩，只露出一双平静的眼睛。她低头看着一块普通的腕表。
动作：随着背景音乐的倒计时音效，她猛地抬起头，眼神瞬间变得锐利，抬起手腕做出一个“启动”的按压动作。
[氛围] 压抑、神秘、蓄势待发。

[00:04-00:06] 镜头 2：纳米重组（变形）。
高能特效转场。
动作：一道蓝色全息扫描环从她的脚下迅速升起，扫描她的全身。
特效：原有的连帽衫在光环内分解成无数蓝色**数字方块和机械碎片**。她的身体似乎被一层液态金属迅速覆盖。
[关键点] 强调科技感和机械结构交错的组装过程，而非脱衣。

[00:06-00:12] 镜头 3：战斗模式。
场景：瞬间切换到大雨滂沱的未来都市屋顶，背景是巨大的紫色全息广告牌。
主角：全身覆盖着一套**流线型白色科幻全身装甲**，装甲上流淌着发光的蓝色能量通道。她摘下面罩，长发在雨中飞舞。
动作：她半跪着缓冲落地（如同终结者降临），然后酷帅地缓缓站起。一把发光的**能量光剑**瞬间在她手中具现化。她向镜头挥出一道光束，定格在战斗姿态。
[表情] 冷酷、自信、无所畏惧。
```

```text
【Style】Futuristic Tech, Cyberpunk, Portrait (9:16), cinematic special effects, slow-motion upscale, cool and handsome.
【Duration】12 seconds
【Protagonist】A young woman with determined eyes.
【Scene】Dim underground garage -> Neon-flashing future city rooftop.
[00:00-00:04] Shot 1: The Disguise.
Scene: Dim, smoke-filled underground garage.
Protagonist: Wearing an ordinary black hooded hoodie, with the hood up and a black mask, only a pair of calm eyes are visible. She is looking down at an ordinary wristwatch on her wrist.
Action: Following the BGM countdown sound effect, she sharply raises her head, her eyes instantly becoming sharp, and she raises her wrist to perform a “start” pressing action.
【Atmosphere】Suppressed, mysterious, ready to launch.
[00:04-00:06] Shot 2: The Transformation (Nano Reassembly).
High-energy special effects transition.
Action: A blue Holographic Scan Ring rapidly rises from her feet, scanning her entire body.
Special Effects: The original hoodie decomposes into countless blue digital blocks and mechanical fragments within the light ring. Her body seems to be quickly covered by a layer of liquid metal.
【Key Point】Emphasize the sense of technology and the interlocking process of mechanical structures, not undressing.
[00:06-00:12] Shot 3: The Battle Mode.
Scene: Instantly switches to a future city rooftop in heavy rain, with a giant purple holographic billboard in the background.
Protagonist: Her entire body is covered in a streamlined white Full-body Sci-fi Armor, with glowing blue energy channels flowing on the armor. She is not wearing a mask, and her long hair is flying in the rain.
Action: She kneels on one knee to cushion the impact (like the Terminator arriving), then slowly and coolly stands up. A glowing energy light sword instantly materializes in her hand, she swings an afterimage towards the camera, and freezes in a battle stance.
【Expression】Cold, confident, fearless.
```
