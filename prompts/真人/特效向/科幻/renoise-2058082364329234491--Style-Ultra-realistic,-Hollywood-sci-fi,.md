# Style: Ultra-realistic, Hollywood sci-fi, dystopian yet beautiful, cinematic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Style: Ultra-realistic, Hollywood sci-fi, dystopian yet beautiful, cinematic trailer energy, seamless fast pacing (no text, no emojis)

Setting

A vast futuristic metropolis entirely ruled by intelligent robots.

Towering skyscrapers made of glass, steel, and moving mechanical structures

Roads filled with silent autonomous machines instead of humans

Neon holographic signs flickering in a coded language

Constant fog, light rain, and glowing reflections on metallic surfaces

Giant robotic statues watching over the city like guardians

Main Character

A lone human traveler entering the robot city for the first time

Worn futuristic cloak or minimal survival gear

Emotional but controlled expressions: awe, fear, curiosity

Moving cautiously through an unknown mechanical civilization

Robots & Environment

Humanoid robots performing daily city functions (transport, security, construction)

Floating drone patrols scanning everything

Massive transport robots carrying glowing energy cores

Mechanical birds flying between skyscrapers

City feels alive but silent and controlled

🎥 TIMELINE / 15 SECONDS CINEMATIC FLOW

⏱️ 0:00 – 0:03 | ARRIVAL AT THE CITY GATE

Wide cinematic shot: enormous robot city skyline emerges through fog

Massive metallic gates slowly open with deep mechanical sound

Human character stands alone, looking up in shock

Camera slowly pushes forward into the city

🎧 Sound: deep mechanical hum + distant machine movements

⏱️ 0:03 – 0:06 | FIRST STEP INSIDE

Close-up: footsteps entering glowing metallic street

Reflections of neon robots moving across wet ground

Robots pass by, ignoring human presence but scanning softly

Security drones slowly rotate and scan the newcomer

🎥 Camera: slow tracking shot at ground level + robotic POV cuts

⏱️ 0:06 – 0:10 | THE LIVING MACHINE WORLD

Wide shot: entire robotic city in motion

Giant machines constructing skyscrapers mid-air

Autonomous vehicles floating silently through traffic lanes

Human looks overwhelmed, turning slowly to observe everything

Subtle robotic eye scanners briefly lock onto him/her

🎧 Sound: layered mechanical rhythm + distant electronic pulses

⏱️ 0:10 – 0:13 | SUSPENSE MOMENT

Security drones suddenly stop and focus on the human

Red scanning beams pass across face

Close-up: human breathing intensifies, tense eyes

Robots remain motionless for a second… silent judgment

🎥 Camera: extreme close-up + slow-motion tension build

⏱️ 0:13 – 0:15 | FINAL IMPACT SHOT

Giant central AI tower activates glowing energy pulse

Entire city lights flicker in sync like a living organism

Human stands small in foreground against massive machine world

Camera slowly pulls back into a wide aerial reveal

🎧 Sound: rising synth wave → deep bass resonance → fade

🎭 VISUAL STYLE

Hyper-realistic sci-fi worldbuilding

Neon cyber-metal aesthetic

Fog, rain, reflections, volumetric lighting

Slow cinematic camera movement + sharp tension cuts

Emotional human contrast vs cold robotic order

🔊 SOUND DESIGN

Deep mechanical drones

Futuristic ambient synth layers

Robotic movement sounds, scanning beeps

Low bass tension pulses

Atmospheric silence moments for suspense
```
