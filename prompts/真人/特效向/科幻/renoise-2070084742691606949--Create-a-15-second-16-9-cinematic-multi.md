# Create a 15-second 16:9 cinematic multi-shot mini story using Image 1 as the…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a 15-second 16:9 cinematic multi-shot mini story using Image 1 as the storyboard reference for shot order, composition, apocalyptic mood, lighting, VFX intensity, and overall color grade.

Story concept:
A sudden apocalypse destroys a modern city. A lone survivor runs through the collapsing world, witnesses a massive destructive force in the sky, then ends with a final emotional moment of hope.

Shot 1, 0-3s:
Wide aerial establishing shot of a burning city under a dark storm sky. Meteors fall from above, explosions bloom across skyscrapers, smoke and debris fill the atmosphere. Camera slowly pushes forward over the destroyed skyline.

Shot 2, 3-6s:
Medium handheld tracking shot of a lone male survivor running through ruined city streets. Cars burn, dust moves through the air, small debris falls around him. Keep his face, outfit, hair, age, and body proportions consistent.

Shot 3, 6-9s:
Wide cinematic shot of a massive building collapsing in the distance. A shockwave moves through the street, dust and debris fly toward the camera. Camera shakes slightly from impact but stays readable and controlled.

Shot 4, 9-11s:
Close shot of the survivor hiding behind a broken car, breathing heavily, looking upward in fear. Firelight flickers on his face, ash falls around him, realistic facial expression and body movement.

Shot 5, 11-13s:
Epic wide shot of a gigantic alien ship or apocalyptic structure in the sky, releasing a destructive vertical energy beam into the ruined city. High-end VFX, smoke, sparks, glowing orange light, dramatic clouds, large scale.

Shot 6, 13-15s:
Medium wide emotional shot from behind the survivor as he looks toward the horizon. The clouds break and warm sunlight appears through the smoke, creating one final ray of hope.

Camera and style:
Cinematic disaster movie style, high VFX, realistic physics, dramatic scale, dark smoky atmosphere, orange fire glow, ash particles, dust, shockwaves, controlled handheld movement, fast pacing, impactful cuts, realistic motion blur.

Audio:
Deep cinematic bass impacts, explosions, falling debris, fire ambience, distant alarms, wind, heavy breathing, then a soft emotional tone during the final sunlight shot.

References:
Use Image 1 only as the storyboard guide for composition, shot sequence, visual mood, VFX density, lighting, and cinematic framing. Do not animate the written text or layout from the storyboard. Convert each storyboard panel into a realistic cinematic video shot.

Constraints:
No random text, no visible captions, no storyboard borders, no duplicated survivors, no face morphing, no warped anatomy, no chaotic camera, no excessive flicker, no unreadable action, no unrealistic physics, no sudden unrelated scene changes. Keep the same survivor, same outfit, same apocalyptic city style, same dark orange-gray color grade, and coherent continuity across all shots.
```
