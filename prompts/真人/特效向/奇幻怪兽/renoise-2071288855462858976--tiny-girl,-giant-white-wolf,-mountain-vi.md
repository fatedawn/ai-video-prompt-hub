7.4-8.4: Hold the upside-down pose for comedy. The wolf blinks once, unimpressed.

9.8-11.2: She points up at the wolf and scolds silently, stomping one foot. Her whole body bounces with tiny rage.

11.2-12.4: The wolf slowly rolls its eyes toward the sky, then exhales through its nose. Its eyelids droop.

12.4-14.0: The wolf lowers its giant muzzle to the grass with real weight; fur squashes outward, grass bends under its chin.
