# Arctic Fantasy Woman in Ice-Blue Gown Video Prompt

> A detailed Grok Imagine prompt for generating a photorealistic fantasy video of a beautiful young woman with platinum blonde hair and blue eyes, standing in a dramatic arctic landscape. She is wearing a sparkling ice-blue evening gown and a white fur shawl, set against icy mountains and crystal castles with ethereal, cool-toned cinematic lighting.

## 提示词（English）

```text
A breathtakingly beautiful young woman with long, flowing platinum blonde hair, fair skin, striking blue eyes, and elegant makeup, standing gracefully in a frozen arctic fantasy landscape. She wears a stunning, sparkling ice-blue evening gown with a sweetheart corset bodice covered in glittering crystals and intricate beadwork, sheer sparkling tulle skirt with high slit, and luxurious white fur shawl draped over her arms and shoulders. She has a delicate diamond necklace and earrings. Her pose is confident and regal, looking slightly to the side with a serene expression.

Background: dramatic icy mountains and jagged crystal ice castles under a pale blue sky, misty frozen lake in the foreground, soft cinematic lighting with cool blue tones and subtle glowing highlights, ethereal atmosphere, highly detailed, 8k, photorealistic fantasy style, cinematic composition, majestic and elegant mood.
```
