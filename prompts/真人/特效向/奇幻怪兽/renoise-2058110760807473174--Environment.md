# Environment:

## 提示词（English）

```text
Environment:

A dense futuristic megacity filled with towering glass skyscrapers, neon billboards, and elevated highways. Dark storm clouds hang above while emergency lights flash across the streets.

Action:

15.0s sequence from the first-person viewpoint of a massive kaiju towering hundreds of meters tall. The viewer sees parts of armored claws occasionally entering frame as the creature moves.

At the 2-second mark the kaiju steps forward and an entire office tower fills the frame.

The creature smashes through the building, glass exploding outward in a storm of shards.

The skyline tilts as buildings collapse around the monster’s path.

Velocity Ramp choreography: a giant claw strike slows dramatically as the building façade shatters — glass fragments and steel beams suspended mid-air — before snapping back as the structure collapses.

Camera:

Heavy ground-shaking POV with towering perspective looking down into streets far below.

Style & Constraints:

Photorealistic urban destruction physics, ray-traced reflections on glass towers, cinematic storm lighting, massive debris particle simulation, 8K.
```
