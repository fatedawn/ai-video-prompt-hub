10-second cinematic comedy sequence, hyper-realistic, 16:9.

A tiny fierce Viking warrior stands on a wooden dining table, facing a gigantic towering sandwich stacked with meat, cheese, lettuce, and sauce like a monster.
The Viking wears a horned helmet, fur cloak, and carries a small round shield and axe. He lets out a dramatic battle cry and charges forward.

The giant sandwich suddenly tilts toward him, wobbling like a living beast.
A slice of tomato slides down like an attack. The Viking ducks, jumps onto the plate, climbs the sandwich heroically, and hacks at it with his axe. Sauce splatters everywhere.

The sandwich begins collapsing in slow comic chaos. In the final moment, the Viking stands triumphantly on top of the fallen sandwich, breathing hard, then gets smacked in the face by a flying lettuce leaf.
