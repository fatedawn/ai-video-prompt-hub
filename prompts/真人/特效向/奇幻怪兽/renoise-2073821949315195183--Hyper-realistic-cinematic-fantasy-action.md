# Hyper realistic cinematic fantasy action sequence.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Hyper realistic cinematic fantasy action sequence.
An ancient stone golem awakens beneath a vast fantasy city of towers, bridges, stairs, and crowded rooftops.
The ground erupts as its giant hand bursts through the main street, smashing stone roads and sending debris flying. Panic spreads instantly.

A young warrior and a female mage sprint across rooftops trying to escape as the golem rises higher and higher behind them, tearing through buildings like they are made of paper.

Start with a dramatic street level shot as the pavement cracks open and a colossal stone hand explodes upward through dust and rubble. People scatter in all directions. Cut quickly to a fast tracking shot following the warrior and mage running across tiled rooftops, jumping over narrow gaps as shockwaves from the golem break walls and collapse balconies behind them. The golem’s upper body rises above the skyline, enormous and terrifying, glowing cracks of ancient magic burning through its stone body.

Cut to a wide cinematic angle showing the full scale of the city and the towering golem smashing through rooftops, grabbing an entire tower and tearing it apart. The heroes race across a suspended bridge between buildings just as the golem’s arm crashes down behind them, destroying the bridge section by section.

Dust, broken stone, and magical light fill the air.  For the final seconds, the mage turns while running and fires a bright magical blast at a falling tower bell. The bell crashes down into the golem’s face, buying them one second. They leap together from the collapsing bridge onto a lower rooftop as the entire structure behind them is obliterated by the golem’s hand.

Final shot shows them landing hard, looking back in shock as the giant golem stands fully risen over the ruined city, framed by smoke, dust, and glowing magical cracks.

Epic fantasy, breathless action, massive scale, dramatic lighting, clear cause and effect, no text, no logos, no comedy, no modern objects.
```
