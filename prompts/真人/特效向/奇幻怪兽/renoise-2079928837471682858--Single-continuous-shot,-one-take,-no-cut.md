# Single continuous shot, one take, no cuts, cinematic oner, photorealistic,

## 提示词（English）

```text
Single continuous shot, one take, no cuts, cinematic oner, photorealistic,
8K ultra-high-definition, hyperdetailed, 35mm film quality, professional
color grading, sharp focus, fine film grain, steadicam-to-tracking
fluidity, depth of field mastery.

SUBJECT: a lone climber in weathered mountaineering gear, ice axe in one
gloved hand, pulling themselves over a final snow ridge into thin bright
air above the clouds. Same climber throughout, no drift. Setting: a
towering mountain summit breaking through a dense cloud sea at sunrise,
jagged rock and snow, thin wind-blown ice crystals catching the light.

THE CREATURE: an original design — a colossal sky-glider, elongated
serpentine body with broad translucent membranous wings spanning far
wider than the mountain ridge, pale opaline scales catching gold light,
slow powerful wingbeats, no aggression, simply passing close overhead in
majestic silent flight.

COLOR 60:30:10 — 60% pale dawn-gold cloud sea and sky / 30% cold blue-grey
rock and snow shadow / 10% accent of the creature's opaline scale-glow and
the climber's red jacket.

PHYSICS: real wind buffeting the climber's clothing and the loose snow
around their boots, the creature's wings displacing visible air, clouds
churning and parting beneath its shadow, ice crystals drifting and
scattering in its wake.

0–4s — THE SUMMIT: low tracking shot behind the climber as they crest the
ridge, wind whipping their jacket, breath visible, golden light flooding
the frame as they look up in sudden awe.

4–9s — THE PASS: wide shot, camera pulling back and up as the colossal
creature glides directly overhead, its shadow sweeping across the climber
and the cloud sea, wings beating once, slow and unhurried, scale utterly
overwhelming against the tiny human figure.

9–13s — THE GAZE: close on the climber's face, tilted upward, lit gold by
the creature's passing glow, pure wonder, no fear.

13–15s — THE DEPARTURE: wide hero shot as the creature banks gently away
into the golden cloud sea, shrinking into the distance, the climber still
standing at the summit watching it go.

AUDIO (no music): thin high-altitude wind, the climber's breath, fabric
snapping in the wind. As the creature passes, a deep slow whoosh of
displaced air and a low resonant creature call, fading as it departs.

FORBIDDEN: no franchise or recognizable creatures, no design drift, no
morphing or extra limbs, no plastic CGI gloss, no cartoon proportions, no
readable text or logos, no desaturated grading, no aggressive or attacking
posture from the creature, no warping geometry.
```
