# Parade ending in a sugar pile with a flying dragon

> A Grok Imagine prompt describing a fantastical scene involving a parade, a huge pile of sugar, a dragon flying by, and people emerging from sugar dust laughing.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Parade  and at the end they all jump in a huge pile of sugar the dragon fly’s by and blows sugar dust over them they pop out of the dust and laugh
```
