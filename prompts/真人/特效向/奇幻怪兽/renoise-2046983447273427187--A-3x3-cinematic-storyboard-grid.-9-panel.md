# A 3x3 cinematic storyboard grid. 9 panels arranged in a perfect 3-by-3 layout…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A 3x3 cinematic storyboard grid. 9 panels arranged in a perfect 3-by-3 layout with thin black borders between each panel. Each panel is a different shot from one continuous story set in a dark fantasy gladiatorial arena built from ancient weathered marble with faint gold circuit-like veins running through the stone. IMAX film still quality, dark moody palette of deep blue-blacks, warm amber firelight, and gold accents.

Panel 1 (top-left): Ultra-wide establishing shot. A massive colosseum at dusk, marble columns with gold circuit traces, torches lining the upper tiers, dark storm clouds above, tiny silhouettes of spectators.

Panel 2 (top-center): Interior wide shot. The arena floor seen from the stands, torchlit marble arches, thousands of spectators in shadow, amber firelight reflecting off wet stone.

Panel 3 (top-right): Close-up of a heavy iron gate, rusted and ancient. Two glowing amber eyes stare from the darkness behind the bars. Wisps of smoke curl through the gaps.

Panel 4 (middle-left): Medium shot. A gladiator in weathered bronze and marble-white armor with gold circuit patterns enters the arena. He carries a round marble shield with embedded gold veins and a short sword. Firelight catches the circuits.

Panel 5 (middle-center): Wide action shot. A massive mechanical lion with obsidian plating and glowing amber joints charges across the arena floor, kicking up dust and sparks. Fire erupts from its jaws.

Panel 6 (middle-right): Dynamic close-up. The gladiator blocks the lion attack with his shield, sparks flying where metal meets stone, the gold circuits on his armor flaring bright.

Panel 7 (bottom-left): Low angle. The gladiator on one knee, battered, shield cracked, the mechanical lion circling in the background shrouded in dust and firelight.

Panel 8 (bottom-center): The decisive moment. The gladiator drives his sword upward through the lion jaw, the blade glowing with golden energy, mechanical parts shattering outward.

Panel 9 (bottom-right): Victory. The gladiator stands alone in the center of the arena, arm raised, gold circuit patterns pulsing across the marble floor radiating outward from where he stands. Torchlight and amber glow.
```
