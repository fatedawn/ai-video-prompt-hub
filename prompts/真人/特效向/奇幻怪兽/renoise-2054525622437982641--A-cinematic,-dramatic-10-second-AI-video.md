# A cinematic, dramatic 10-second AI video featuring a mysterious skeletal figure…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A cinematic, dramatic 10-second AI video featuring a mysterious skeletal figure as the central character. The skeleton has a glowing bright blue eyes, wearing a stylish black fedora hat, long black leather trench coat with tattered edges, black vest, chains and medallion, black gloves and boots.
He appears in multiple epic, cinematic scenes with fluid transitions: standing powerfully on a mechanical platform in front of a giant spiked steampunk structure, walking confidently through foggy Victorian gothic streets at night, holding an ornate sword or glowing futuristic gun, dramatic poses with smoke and fog swirling around him.
Some shots show his full skeleton body visible under the open coat, other shots show him more clothed. Backgrounds include dark cobblestone streets, towering gothic architecture, dramatic god rays / volumetric lighting, lanterns, chains, smoke, fire effects, and mysterious henchmen in trench coats and hats in the background.
Moody, dark fantasy, cyberpunk-noir aesthetic, highly detailed, epic composition, cinematic lighting, atmospheric fog, intense and mysterious vibe, smooth video transitions between iconic poses.
```
