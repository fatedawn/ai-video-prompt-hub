# Create a professional storyboard sheet image.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a professional storyboard sheet image.

Purpose: a production storyboard used as the visual reference for an AI video generator (Seedance 2.0). Visual clarity and strict character and environment consistency across panels are the top priorities.

Video format: 1920s Japanese countryside fantasy folk tale. Light-hearted adventure comedy with cute animal characters. Structure: problem appears, villagers panic, hero intervention, clever solution, celebration ending.

Layout:

A clean grid of 12 storyboard panels (3 columns x 4 rows) on a flat white sheet with even margins and thin panel borders.
Each panel is a horizontal 16:9 panel.
Sheet title: "蜂と黒熊" — bold sans-serif, dark ink, top-left corner of the sheet.
Each panel has a small circled number (1 to 12) in its top-left corner, reading order left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
Below each panel, a compact caption block with three short lines labeled "ACTION:", "CAMERA:", "DIALOGUE:" — small clean sans-serif, sharp and fully legible.

Story (12 sequential panels):

Panel 1 — Summer, 1920s Japan. An ancient village at the foot of Mount Fuji. White rabbits wearing traditional Japanese farmer clothing plant rice seedlings in lush green paddies.
ACTION: peaceful agricultural work.
CAMERA: wide establishing shot.
DIALOGUE: (none)
Panel 2 — A swarm of adorable giant-eyed bees approaches from the distant sky.
ACTION: strange visitors appear.
CAMERA: long telephoto shot.
DIALOGUE: "Buzz?"
Panel 3 — The bees fly across the rice fields. Wherever they pass, they chew away the rice stalks as if trimming hair, leaving neat bald patches.
ACTION: crop destruction.
CAMERA: aerial view.
DIALOGUE: (none)
Panel 4 — The rabbits stare in horror and frustration at the devastated fields.
ACTION: villagers panic.
CAMERA: medium group shot.
DIALOGUE: "Oh no!"

Panel 5 — An elderly white rabbit wearing a traditional Japanese kimono gasps and immediately runs toward the village.
ACTION: seeking help.
CAMERA: dynamic tracking shot.
DIALOGUE: "Quick!"
Panel 6 — Village marketplace. A black bear wearing a bright red haramaki-style chest cloth sells jars of honey from a wooden stall.
ACTION: introduce the hero.
CAMERA: medium shot.
DIALOGUE: "Fresh honey!"
Panel 7 — The elderly rabbit explains the crisis. The black bear becomes angry, grabs a plastic bottle of yellow honey, and dashes away.
ACTION: hero accepts mission.
CAMERA: dramatic low-angle shot.
DIALOGUE: "I'm coming!"
Panel 8 — In the rice field, the black bear now wears a black mesh protective beekeeping suit. His red chest cloth remains faintly visible underneath. He uses a large paintbrush to spread thick sticky honey onto a scarecrow.
ACTION: preparing the trap.
CAMERA: side medium shot.
DIALOGUE: (none)

Panel 9 — The giant-eyed bees become attracted to the honey-covered scarecrow and swarm toward it.
ACTION: trap activated.
CAMERA: wide shot.
DIALOGUE: "Honey!"
Panel 10 — Countless bees become stuck in the thick honey and cannot move.
ACTION: bees trapped.
CAMERA: close-up.
DIALOGUE: "Bzzz!"
Panel 11 — The black bear pulls the scarecrow from the field, runs toward a forest stream, and throws it into the water. The bees gradually free themselves and fly away peacefully.
ACTION: remove the threat.
CAMERA: action tracking shot.
DIALOGUE: "Goodbye!"
Panel 12 — Back in the rice field. The white rabbits cheer and applaud enthusiastically. The black bear scratches the back of his head with one paw, looking shy but proud.
ACTION: hero celebration.
CAMERA: triumphant wide shot with Mount Fuji in the background.
DIALOGUE: "Hooray!"

Consistency:
The same black bear must appear in every scene with identical facial features, body proportions, red chest cloth, and personality.
The elderly rabbit must remain visually identical throughout.
The giant-eyed bees must maintain the same cute design in all panels.
Mount Fuji, the village architecture, rice fields, and costumes should consistently reflect rural Japan during the 1920s.
Style:
Photorealistic live-action fantasy film, highly detailed animal fur, realistic natural lighting, cinematic composition, warm summer colors, authentic Taisho-era Japanese countryside atmosphere.
Constraints:
Flat white storyboard sheet background, no watermark, no extra text beyond the specified title, panel numbers and caption blocks. All text must be sharp and legible.
quality: high

Output aspect ratio: 3:2
```
