# POV handheld shot from someone in the audience filming with a phone camera from…

## 提示词（English）

```text
POV handheld shot from someone in the audience filming with a phone camera from the third row, frame slightly unsteady, raised above other people’s heads. The scene shows a massive open-air stage where a warrior holding a giant wooden brush faces off against a towering origami colossus. The origami monster stomps its feet, sending a shockwave of sharp paper folds slicing across the white stage floor. The artist rapidly spins the enormous brush, creating a swirling vortex shield of thick black ink that absorbs the paper attacks. Dramatic, fast-paced orchestral string music plays through the venue's audio system. The audience gasps, some leaning forward in shock. With a powerful, two-handed swing, the artist flings the accumulated ink shield forward as a massive, horizontal wave that completely smothers the monster, turning its pristine white body pitch-black and causing it to immediately go limp and collapse. Loud applause and amazed murmurs fill the air
```
