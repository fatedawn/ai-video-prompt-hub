# [CINEMATIC SETUP]

## 提示词（English）

```text
[CINEMATIC SETUP]

Style: Ultra-realistic cinematic urban fantasy, grounded time-freeze concept, rebellious streetwear energy, stylish and playful tone.
Camera: Smooth cinematic tracking, controlled push-ins, subtle orbit moments.
Lens: 35mm, 50mm.
Color Grade: Clean daylight tones, realistic city contrast, subtle cinematic polish.
Atmosphere: Busy modern city street, pedestrians, traffic, natural daylight, relaxed but cheeky energy.

[CHARACTER]

Main Subject: Adult male  [YOUR CHARACTER ]wearing a black t-shirt, black cargo pants, and white sneakers. Relaxed, confident, stylish, playful, slightly rebellious attitude.
Pedestrians: Everyday city people walking naturally in different directions, later frozen in place.
Environment: Urban street with sidewalks, storefronts, passing cars, and realistic daytime movement.

[TIMELINE]

0–2s: [Medium frontal tracking shot, 50mm]
The subject is already walking down the busy city street. He suddenly gives one sharp clap, and at that exact moment time freezes completely around him. Pedestrians, traffic, and all background motion stop instantly in place.
2–6s: [Lateral tracking shot, 35mm]
Now the only person able to move, he keeps walking with relaxed swagger through the frozen street, looking around with a smug, amused expression as he realizes everything is suspended around him.
6–11s: [Smooth tracking + subtle orbit, 35mm]
He starts interacting with the frozen pedestrians in a playful rebellious way. He grabs a frozen passerby’s soda and casually takes a sip, stands directly in front of another person and waves his hand in front of their eyes to test if they can see him, then leans in close to inspect someone’s face.
11–15s: [Frontal tracking shot, 50mm]
He keeps walking forward through the frozen city with total confidence, still amused, lightly adjusting a passerby’s pose as he passes, holding the stolen drink for a moment while the entire street remains perfectly suspended around him.

[STYLE & QUALITY]
Photorealistic 8K, realistic urban motion, clean time-freeze effect, coherent frozen pedestrians, cinematic movement, grounded visual realism, stylish pacing.
```
