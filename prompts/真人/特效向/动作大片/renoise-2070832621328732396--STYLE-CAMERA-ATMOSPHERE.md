```text
[STYLE + CAMERA + ATMOSPHERE]
Ultra-photorealistic cinematic 15-second action sequence from a grounded urban thriller film. Nighttime on a wet city street with moderate traffic. Asphalt, streetlights, reflections on the wet road and car headlights. The visual language is raw and realistic. Camera is handheld-dynamic with urgent, motivated moves.

[IMAGE REFERENCES]
No reference images provided. Generate a fully consistent lead character: a woman in her late 20s running for her life, focused and scared expression. She wears a dark jacket, jeans and sneakers. Maintain exact same appearance and clothing throughout the clip.

[TIMELINE SECOND BY SECOND]
0-3s: [Dynamic handheld tracking shot from side-rear] The woman runs desperately down the middle of the road trying to escape someone chasing her. A car coming from the opposite direction at high speed is about to hit her. The headlights are bright and the car is very close. The camera tracks her urgently as the vehicle approaches.

3-7s: [Slow continuous orbiting camera moving around the frozen scene] At the exact moment the car is about to hit her, everything freezes in perfect realistic physics. The car, the woman’s body position, flying water from the wet road and small debris hang completely motionless in the air. The camera performs a smooth, continuous orbit around the frozen moment, moving around the suspended car and passing close to the woman while showing the dangerous proximity of the vehicle.

7-15s: [Dynamic tracking shot as time resumes] At the 7-second mark time snaps back to forward motion with a slight realistic temporal residue. Some water droplets and small debris show a very subtle reverse lag before continuing. The woman has used the frozen moment to quickly step to the side and avoid the car. When motion resumes, the vehicle passes right next to her without hitting her. She keeps running toward the sidewalk as the car continues down the road. The camera tracks with her as she reaches the pavement.

[STYLE & QUALITY BOOSTERS]
Photorealistic 8K, hyper-realistic physics with correct material properties (car, water, asphalt), perfect frozen mid-air physics, seamless transition from freeze to resume, natural wet road and lighting interaction, heavy cinematic motion blur on fast movement, stable character performance, movie-level practical action quality.
```
