# An army jeep is stuck on a narrow log bridge crossing a deep jungle gorge in…

## 提示词（English）

```text
An army jeep is stuck on a narrow log bridge crossing a deep jungle gorge in bright daylight. A mega crocodile, huge as a truck, has grabbed the back of the jeep and is pulling at it, the whole bridge shaking under the weight. At the far end of the bridge, a soldier stands at a winch truck — this winch is used every day to pull out stuck or broken-down vehicles on this route. Sound: creaking logs, groaning metal, croc hissing, shouting, radio voices.

Shot 1 (0–3s) — THE HOOK: Low shot along the log bridge. The mega crocodile has its jaws locked on the back of the jeep, shaking it side to side, the old logs cracking under the strain. The driver grips the wheel and shouts into his radio: IT'S GOT THE BACK! THE BRIDGE WON'T HOLD!

Shot 2 (3–6s): At the winch truck, the soldier hooks the winch cable onto the front of the jeep, checking it twice, the way he does every time he pulls a stuck vehicle. He calls on the radio: Hold tight! Pulling you out now!

Shot 3 (6–9s): The soldier throws the winch lever and the cable snaps tight, yanking the jeep forward hard across the bridge. The sudden pull swings the whole jeep sideways — and the crocodile, still holding on, is thrown off balance and flung off the edge of the bridge.

Shot 4 (9–13s) — BIG SLOW-MOTION MOMENT: The crocodile falls from the bridge. Bullet time — its huge body twists through the air in slow motion, legs and tail thrashing, the gorge walls rushing past on either side as it drops toward the rocks far below.

Shot 5 (13–15s): Time snaps back. The crocodile crashes down into the gorge with a heavy thud far below. The jeep skids to a stop safe on solid ground, the driver breathing hard, looking back at the bridge still standing.
```
