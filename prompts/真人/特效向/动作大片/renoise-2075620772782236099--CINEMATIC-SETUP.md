# CINEMATIC SETUP:

## 提示词（English）

```text
CINEMATIC SETUP:
Handheld shaky cam, found-footage style, third-person close follow,
dynamic natural camera shake with the ship's gentle rocking, motion blur,
quick pans, 35mm lens look, flickering red emergency lighting, high ISO
grain, cold desaturated palette, wet reflective surfaces.

CHARACTERS:
A man in his 30s, soaked waterproof jacket, exhausted and frightened —
running. Behind him, three zombies: pale grey skin, slow lurching movement,
one dragging a leg.

LOCATION:
The interior of a research vessel at night in a storm — a narrow steel
corridor with exposed pipes and dripping water, leading through a heavy
bulkhead door onto the open deck lashed by rain and sea spray, metal
railings, a lifeboat station, a tilting horizon as the ship rolls on the
waves.

TIMELINE (15s):
[0-3s] Camera follows the man running down the flooded steel corridor from
behind, red light flickering, water sloshing with the ship's roll, dynamic
handheld movement.
[3-6s] He turns his head to look back at the zombies closing in — quick pan
catches their pale faces, motion blur across the frame.
[6-9s] He faces forward and keeps running, reaching a heavy bulkhead door,
pushing it open with his shoulder.
[9-12s] He steps out onto the rain-swept deck, wind and spray hitting the
lens, and moves toward the lifeboat station, hand reaching out.
[12-15s] The three zombies close in around him from the corridor and the
deck, surrounding him against the railing; camera settles low and canted as
they close the circle, freeze on his face.

SFX:
Heavy breathing, running footsteps on wet steel, groaning metal, storm wind,
rain, distant alarm klaxon, low zombie groans.

QUALITY ANCHOR:
Ultra-realistic, cinematic, film grain, natural low-light exposure,
photorealistic detail, 4K.
```
