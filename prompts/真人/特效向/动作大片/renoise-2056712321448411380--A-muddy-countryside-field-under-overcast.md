# A muddy countryside field under overcast skies, wind moving through grass. A…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A muddy countryside field under overcast skies, wind moving through grass. A woman in a black croptop and shorts sprints frantically, gunshots cracking behind her, bullets kicking up dirt.

Cut 1 (0–3s): Handheld tracking — she runs full speed, breathing hard, glancing back in fear as distant soldiers fire. Footsteps splash through wet ground, urgency high.

Cut 2 (3–6s): Medium shot — she suddenly skids in thick sludge near a group of calm jersey cows, slipping forward and catching herself. She urgently taps something on her wristwatch.

Cut 3 (6–9s): Close-up to medium — one jersey cow beside her glitches mechanically, its head and back panels splitting open with smooth, hidden machinery revealed. Subtle metallic unfolding.

Cut 4 (9–11s): Fast action shot — she quickly climbs inside the hollow mechanical cow as the panels close seamlessly, restoring it to a normal appearance.

Cut 5 (11–14s): Wide shot — armed soldiers rush in with rifles and a hovering drone scanning. One speaks over radio, “She’s not here… just cows.” The herd stands still, blending perfectly.

Cut 6 (14–15s): Interior close-up — inside the cow, dim bunker-like space. The woman watches through a small slit, breathing quietly, eyes intense.

Cinematic thriller style, natural diffused lighting, grounded realistic physics, shallow DOF. Sound of gunshots echoing, muddy footsteps, subtle mechanical transformation, radio chatter, drone hum, quiet breathing. 4K Ultra HD, rich details, sharp clarity, cinematic texture, stable picture. Maintain face and clothing consistency, no distortion, high detail. Generate video without subtitles.
```
