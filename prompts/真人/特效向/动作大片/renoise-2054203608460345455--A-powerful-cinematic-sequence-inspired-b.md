# A powerful cinematic sequence inspired by ancient Rome and the spirit of…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A powerful cinematic sequence inspired by ancient Rome and the spirit of gladiators, set inside a massive stone coliseum at sunset. A lone armored warrior stands in the arena, breathing heavily after battle, his helmet partially lifted, dust and sweat visible on his face and armor. The crowd around the coliseum is roaring in the background, blurred by depth of field, while banners ripple in the wind and torches flicker along the walls. The camera slowly pushes in from a wide shot to a dramatic close-up, capturing the warrior’s determined expression as he raises his sword slightly and looks toward the sky.

Use warm golden-orange lighting, floating dust particles, realistic metal reflections, cracked sand on the arena floor, and epic, emotional tension. Include a strong heroic atmosphere with slow-motion movement, subtle camera shake, and a film-grain, high-budget historical epic look. The tone should feel legendary, emotional, and victorious, matching the phrase: “What we do in life echoes in eternity.” Ultra-realistic, dramatic, immersive, 15 seconds, 16:9.
```
