# A muddy logging clearing beside a wide river, deep in thick rainforest in grey…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A muddy logging clearing beside a wide river, deep in thick rainforest in grey daylight. Cut logs stacked everywhere, tyre ruts in the mud, and a tall log-loader crane holding a huge stack of cut logs in its grab. A mega crocodile, huge as a truck, chases an army jeep across the open mud and bites onto the back of it, dragging it toward the river. A soldier sits at the crane controls. Sound: roaring jeep engine, sliding mud, groaning metal, croc hissing, crane motors, radio voices.

Shot 1 (0–3s) — THE HOOK: Low rear shot in the mud. The mega crocodile has its jaws clamped on the back of the jeep, dragging it backward across the clearing toward the water, the wheels spinning and throwing mud. The driver fights the wheel and shouts into his radio: "IT'S GOT THE JEEP!"

Shot 2 (3–6s): Up at the log-loader crane. A soldier sits at the controls, both hands on the levers, the huge stack of logs hanging ready in the grab above the clearing. He watches the jeep being dragged and calls on the radio: "Pull it past me! Drive under the logs!"

Shot 3 (6–9s): The jeep guns its engine and breaks free of the jaws, lurching forward across the mud and past the crane. The crocodile lets go and lunges after it, sliding onto the open mud right below the hanging stack of logs. The soldier's eyes lock on it. He pulls the lever.

Shot 4 (9–13s) — BIG SLOW-MOTION MOMENT: The crane grab snaps open. Bullet time — the whole huge stack of cut logs drops straight down in slow motion, a wall of heavy timber falling onto the crocodile below, mud and splinters hanging in the air — the logs crashing down on top of it, pinning it flat in the mud.

Shot 5 (13–15s): Time snaps back. The logs settle into a big heap in the mud, the croc buried underneath, only its tail showing. The jeep skids to a stop past the crane, the driver looking back. Mud and dust drift across the clearing.
```

샷 3 (6–9초): 지프가 엔진을 가동하고 악어의 턱에서 벗어나 진흙을 가로질러 크레인을 지나 앞으로 나아간다. 악어는 놓아주고 그 뒤를 쫓아가며 매달린 통나무 더미 바로 아래 열린 진흙 위로 미끄러진다. 군인의 눈이 그것에 고정된다. 그는 레버를 당긴다.
