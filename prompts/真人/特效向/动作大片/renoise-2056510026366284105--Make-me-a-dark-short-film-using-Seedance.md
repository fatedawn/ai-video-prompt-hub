Make me a dark short film using Seedance 2 - 720p - Film has 5 - 15 second scenes.

Tone: Classic Friday the 13th raw B-movie slasher — tense, fun, chaotic, practical-looking but smartly censored gore.

Camera & Action: Smooth wide drone shot glides forward low over the misty lake at dusk, golden light turning cool blue. Priya and Neha are waist-deep in the water, laughing and playfully splashing each other. Priya (red bikini top + denim shorts), Neha (white bikini top + denim shorts). Karan stands shirtless on the muddy shore, filming them on his phone and cracking jokes.

Camera & Action: Quick cut to low-angle shot from behind Karan’s shoulder. Tall reeds part silently. The massive Killer (6'4", burlap sack mask, filthy overalls) slowly rises into frame holding the rusty pitchfork. The pitchfork tip touches the water edge.

Camera & Action: Sudden violent upward low-angle thrust with slight handheld shake. Cutaway to water surface — a huge splash and blood spray erupts across the lens. Karan’s phone drops into the mud (still recording).

Camera & Action: Fast reaction cut to medium shot of the girls in the water. Priya and Neha freeze, then scream in horror as Karan collapses on the shore.

Audio: Girls laughing → loud stabbing impact sound + terrified screams. Emotional Beat: Instant shift from fun to nightmare.

---------

SHOT 2 (15-30 sec) – PANIC IN WATER

Camera & Action: Medium shaky handheld with quick cuts between the two girls. Priya and Neha frantically swim toward the muddy shore, arms splashing wildly, eyes wide with terror, screaming Karan’s name.

Camera & Action: Tight close-up on Priya’s panicked face, then quick cut to Neha’s terrified face looking back.

Camera & Action: Medium side angle on the shore. The Killer yanks the pitchfork out of Karan (seen from behind/side — no direct penetration). He slowly turns and begins walking toward the girls. Karan is lying on the ground, struggling.

Audio: Heavy panicked breathing, loud splashing, distant heavy footsteps. Emotional Beat: Helplessness — the killer is now coming for them.

--------

SHOT 3 (30-45 sec) – THE CHASE

Camera & Action: Fast tracking shot from behind the girls, slight Dutch angle. Priya and Neha scramble out of the lake onto the muddy shore, barefoot and covered in mud, and start sprinting through tall grass.

Camera & Action: Quick shaky close-up inserts. Extreme close-ups of their panicked faces — heavy breathing, wet hair stuck to skin, eyes full of fear. They scream each other’s names (“Priya! Run!” / “Neha, faster!”).

Camera & Action: Wide tracking shot pulling back. The girls run desperately through the tall grass field. In the background, the Killer stalks relentlessly after them (never runs), pitchfork in hand. Karan is visible lying on the shore behind him.

Camera & Action: Dynamic side tracking shot. Neha slips hard in the mud. Priya immediately grabs her arm and yanks her back up. They keep sprinting toward the decaying farmhouse.

Audio: Heartbeat + heavy running footsteps + girls screaming names + distant killer footsteps. Emotional Beat: Exhaustion and growing hopelessness.

------------

SHOT 4 (45-60 sec) – ALL IS LOST

Camera & Action: Low shaky tracking following their feet. Neha trips violently over old barbed wire near the farmhouse door. She screams in pain as blood appears on her thigh.

Camera & Action: Tight handheld medium shot, claustrophobic. Priya drags the injured Neha inside the dark, dusty farmhouse and slams the old wooden door shut. Moonlight beams cut through broken windows.

Camera & Action: Tight close-ups + slow push-in. Neha slides down the wall leaving a blood trail, going pale. She weakly says, “I can’t… I can’t run anymore…” Priya panics, looking around desperately.

Camera & Action: Low angle on the door. The Killer arrives. Heavy BANG on the door. Wood splinters. The pitchfork tip punches through the door inches from Priya’s face.

Audio: Heavy breathing, Neha crying, violent door smashing. Emotional Beat: Total hopelessness — they’re trapped.

---------------

SHOT 5 (60-75 sec) – THE PAYOFF

Camera & Action: Heroic low-angle on Priya. She spots the rusty scythe on the wall and grabs it with determination.

Camera & Action: Dynamic medium-to-wide with whip pan. Door bursts open. Killer steps in, silhouetted. Priya swings the scythe hard — cutaway to strong blood spray across the wall. The Killer staggers.
