SCENE:
A crowded football stadium during the final moments of a tense match. Bright stadium lights, loud chants, packed stands. A couple sits side by side, arguing during the match. Then a gigantic meteor falls beside the stadium.

0:00–0:03
Handheld cinematic medium two-shot of the woman and man sitting side by side in the stands. The woman must match Image 2 exactly. She looks annoyed and says:
“I didn’t even want to come to this match.”
The man, frustrated, replies:
“We’re just trying to enjoy ourselves.”
Natural dialogue, tense faces, realistic breathing, shallow depth of field, loud crowd ambience, film grain.

0:03–0:05
The argument grows. The woman looks upset and disappointed, turning away from the pitch. The man gestures irritably, trying to defend himself. The camera slowly pushes closer with subtle handheld tension.

0:05–0:06
The woman says firmly:
“I’m leaving.”
She stands up and tries to leave the row. Her skirt and outfit stay clearly visible. The man stands up behind her, reaching as if to stop or follow her.

0:06–0:08
A deep metallic rumble begins above the stadium. Both freeze mid-argument. Stadium lights flicker. Orange glow spreads across their faces. They look upward. The handheld camera tilts up past the stadium roofline. A gigantic flaming meteor appears, far larger than normal, tearing through the clouds toward the stadium. Its huge fire trail fills the sky.

0:08–0:10

Before impact, the earth shakes violently. Seats, railings, concrete steps, and the stadium structure tremble. The couple loses balance and instinctively grabs each other. Fans panic and scream.

0:10–0:12

They embrace tightly.

0:12–0:15

The woman’s face must still match Image 2. They look into each other’s eyes, then kiss passionately in a desperate end-of-the-world embrace. Dust, rocks, and debris engulf them. End with their faces partially visible as the cloud swallows them into darkness and impact haze.

Ultra-realistic cinematic disaster-romance, football stadium atmosphere, handheld camera, shallow depth of field, bright stadium lights, orange meteor lighting, earthquake-like shaking, volumetric dust, flying rock debris, realistic shockwave, natural skin tones, film grain, 4K HDR.

SOUND:
Crowd chants, match ambience, argument dialogue, heavy breathing, metallic sky rumble, fans screaming, violent shaking, meteor roar, colossal impact, concrete cracking, flying rocks, rushing dust cloud.

NEGATIVE:
No text, subtitles, captions, watermark, distorted faces, duplicated characters, broken anatomy, gore, dismemberment, cartoon physics, comedy exaggeration, direct impact on the couple, meteor hitting inside the stands, sudden cuts, static reference recreation.
