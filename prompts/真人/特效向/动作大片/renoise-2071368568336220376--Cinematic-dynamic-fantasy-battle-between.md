# Cinematic dynamic fantasy battle between two war chiefs inside a moonlit ruined…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Cinematic dynamic fantasy battle between two war chiefs inside a moonlit ruined cathedral overtaken by darkness. Highly dynamic camera always centered on the shadow weaver: smooth tracking shots, dramatic low angles, fast whip pans on tendrils, slow circling during power build-up. Dark energy effects, moving shadows, black particles and void explosions. Cold blue-purple lighting with strong contrast. Professional action look.

No reference images. Create original: Elegant female shadow weaver with pale skin, long silver-white hair, dark flowing robes with glowing purple runes. Rival war chief: massive armored warrior with heavy axe. Armored soldiers with dark weapons.

0-4s: [Dynamic Low-Angle Tracking] Shadow weaver faces rival war chief and soldiers inside the dark cathedral. Chief charges with his axe in slow motion. She sidesteps and summons black shadow tendrils from the ground that grab and slam him backward. Camera tracks low and smooth around her.

4-8s: [Fast Whip-Pan Combat] They exchange blows. She creates a spinning shield of darkness that blocks his attacks, then releases chain shadow tendrils that grab and pull two soldiers into the air before slamming them down. Camera whips rapidly with the moving shadows, locked on her.

8-11s: [Dynamic Push with Effects] More soldiers rush in. She creates a shadow portal on the ground that swallows two soldiers and spits them out violently against a pillar. Camera pushes in dynamically through swirling darkness and particles.

11-15s: [Slow Orbiting Build-Up + Explosion] War chief recovers and charges. The shadow weaver raises her arms as darkness swirls violently around her. A massive void sphere forms above her head. Camera slowly orbits her as the sphere grows, then she hurls it forward in a huge dark explosion that consumes the enemies in black energy. Camera pulls back dramatically while orbiting, revealing the destruction and floating shadow particles.
```
