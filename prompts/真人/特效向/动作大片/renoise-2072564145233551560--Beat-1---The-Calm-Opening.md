The reference image character is a Western woman with long wavy blonde hair casually drinking a Coke in the middle of a chaotic urban street.

The street erupts into chaos around the character.

Beat 3 - The Transformation

The young woman stays calm and completely unfazed.

She lowers the Coke.

She raises one hand.

Vapor rolls off the arm as the ice transformation builds.

Camera: close-up on the arm transformation, slow and deliberate.

Beat 4 - The Touch - Slow Motion

She touches the monster with her frozen hand.

DRAMATIC SLOW MOTION at the moment of contact.

Beat 5 - Full Freeze

A tense pause holds on the frozen monster.

Deep cracks spread through the frozen monster.

Cracks split across its icy body with sharp crystalline sounds.

Detailed cracking ice - realistic frost and crystal texture throughout.

Beat 6 - The Walk-Off

The young woman casually takes another sip of Coke.

She turns away without looking back.

She walks off calmly as the frozen monster cracks behind her.

Confident calm hero energy. No celebration. No reaction. Just casual.

Camera: low angle tracking shot as she walks away, monster in background.

Cold blue-white glowing energy across frozen skin.

Ice rapidly spreading across monster body from point of contact.

Detailed cracking ice with sharp crystalline fracture lines.

Character appearance: a beautiful Western woman with long wavy blonde hair flowing naturally beneath the cap, maintaining the same calm, confident, and unfazed presence throughout the entire sequence.
