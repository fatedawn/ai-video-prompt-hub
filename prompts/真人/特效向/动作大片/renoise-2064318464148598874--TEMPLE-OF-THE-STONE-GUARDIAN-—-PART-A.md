Subject: An athletic Indian female adventurer in a rugged leather explorer outfit, mud-streaked, carrying a relic satchel, sprinting for her life through an ancient jungle temple.

Action/Emotion: Pure survival. Adrenaline. Constant motion. No time to think.

Environment: Massive forgotten jungle temple deep inside a rainforest. Giant stone corridors, collapsing pillars, hanging chains, ancient carvings, shafts of sunlight piercing through cracks in the ceiling, dust and debris everywhere.

Camera: Handheld chase camera, low-angle tracking shots, speed ramps, bullet-time moments during near misses, drone-style fly-throughs, whip pans, crash zooms.

Lighting & Mood: Golden jungle sunlight mixed with dark temple shadows. Dust clouds. Epic adventure-horror atmosphere.

Technical: Ultra-realistic, 4K HDR, 35mm anamorphic lens, cinematic motion blur, practical creature realism, blockbuster adventure-film energy.

SHOT 1 — 0–3s

The sequence begins mid-action.

The woman is already sprinting through a giant stone corridor.

Behind her—

A colossal stone guardian crashes through a temple wall.

Ancient blocks explode outward.

Dust fills the corridor.

The camera races backward in front of her as she runs full speed.

The guardian gains ground with every thunderous step.

SHOT 2 — 3–6s

She glances behind.

The guardian swings a gigantic stone arm.

A row of massive pillars collapses.

Speed ramp.

Stone fragments fly past her face.

She ducks one chunk at the last second.

The camera whips through the debris field with her.

SHOT 3 — 6–9s

Ahead—

A giant stone gate begins dropping from the ceiling.

The exit is closing.

She accelerates.

Slides across the floor.

Bullet time.

The gate slams down inches above her body.

She clears it.

The guardian doesn't stop.

Its fist punches straight through the gate.

Ancient stone explodes everywhere.

SHOT 4 — 9–12s

A massive chasm opens beneath the temple.

Only a hanging chain crosses the gap.

Without slowing down, she jumps.

Grabs the chain.

Swings out over the abyss.

SHOT 5 — 12–15s

Instead of stopping—

It leaps.

The ancient structure shakes.

The chain jerks violently.

Cracks begin spreading through the rusted metal links.

The woman looks upward.

The chain is snapping.

One link breaks.

Then another.

The guardian is almost on top of her.

抓住铁链。

没有停下—

女子向上看。

铁链正在断裂。

一个链环断裂。

守护者几乎就在她上方。
