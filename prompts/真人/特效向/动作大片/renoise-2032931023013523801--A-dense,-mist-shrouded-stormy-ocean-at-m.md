# A dense, mist-shrouded stormy ocean at midnight. A jet-black ghost ship…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A dense, mist-shrouded stormy ocean at midnight. A jet-black ghost ship thunders through towering waves, kicking up spray. The camera weaves rapidly through the dense, twisted rigging, closing in on the speeding vessel. The ship's weathered wood exterior comes into focus, an ornate stained-glass stern window rattling on its hinges. Matching the galloping speed, the camera passes seamlessly through the gap in the glass into the dark interior. A pale, tentacled man calmly plays a massive coral-encrusted pipe organ, completely isolated from the chaotic ride. The camera drifts past his barnacle-covered coat and exits seamlessly out the opposite window. It sweeps up through the mast canopy to the full moon. The frantic thunder crashes fade to silence
```
