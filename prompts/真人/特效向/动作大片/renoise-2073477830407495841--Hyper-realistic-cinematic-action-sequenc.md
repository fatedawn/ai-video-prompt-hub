Hyper-realistic cinematic action sequence
Night. Inside a futuristic multi-level parking tower, a sleek performance car speeds through spiraling ramps while one pursuing vehicle follows close behind.

The structure is modern and dramatic, with glowing strip lights, concrete floors, metal barriers, glass sections, support columns, reflective surfaces, and open edges revealing the neon city outside. The atmosphere is tense, fast, and dangerous.

Wide opening shot: the main car races down the spiral ramp at high speed, tires screeching as it whips around a tight curve. The pursuing car appears just behind, closing the distance. City lights flash through the open sides of the parking tower.

Side tracking shot: both cars descend through the tower in a fast chase. Cracks begin spreading across sections of the floor and ramp. Concrete fragments break loose, barriers bend, and dust falls from the ceiling as the structure starts failing under the pressure and speed.

Front three-quarter shot: the main car drifts around a sharp turn while a section of ramp behind it collapses. The pursuing car barely makes it across before part of the floor drops away. Debris scatters and the tower feels increasingly unstable.

Final 5 seconds: the action becomes extreme. A large section of the spiral ramp breaks apart ahead. The main car accelerates, skids across a cracking floor, and drops from one parking level to the next through a collapsing section, landing hard but continuing forward.

The pursuing car slides sideways through debris as more of the ramp gives way. Concrete dust, sparks, and shattered barriers fill the frame while both vehicles spiral deeper through the failing structure.

Final moment: the main car bursts out onto a lower level and powers through the curve as sections of the parking tower continue collapsing behind it, neon city lights flashing through dust and debris.

Style: hyper-realistic, cinematic, fast-paced, intense, clear and readable action, strong sense of speed and danger, futuristic parking tower, collapsing ramps, cracking floors, dust, sparks, dynamic camera movement, no text, no logos, no cartoon style, no slow motion. Keep proportions. Keep style and features. Aspect ratio 16:9.
