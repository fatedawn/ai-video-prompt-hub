At night in a crowded open-air market, one smuggler in dark street clothes runs through a narrow lane lined with food stalls, hanging lanterns, stacked crates, and glass fish tanks, clutching a small glowing package. A few security pursuers rush in from behind, but keep the focus on the smuggler and his escape.

The action starts immediately. The smuggler sprints through the market as a sudden gravity anomaly begins lifting the market into the air. Make the effect very clear and literal: lantern strings pull upward, small objects rise first, then full wooden stalls start floating off the ground, and large glass fish tanks lift into the air with water sloshing inside them. The smuggler dodges under a stall that is rising too low above him, knocks aside a swinging lantern pole, and slides across the wet ground under a floating table while security agents behind him are blocked by drifting crates and hanging ropes.

A row of lanterns tears loose and rises overhead, showering sparks. Final action: the smuggler runs up the side of the floating stall, leaps from it across open space, and lands hard on a low rooftop at the edge of the market, barely keeping hold of the glowing package. Behind him, the market continues lifting into the sky - lanterns, fish tanks, awnings, and entire stalls floating upward while the pursuers are stranded below among the chaos.
