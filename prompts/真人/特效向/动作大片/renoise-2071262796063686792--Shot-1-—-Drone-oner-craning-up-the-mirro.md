# Shot 1 — Drone oner craning up the mirrored-glass face of a BKC skyscraper in…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Shot 1 — Drone oner craning up the mirrored-glass face of a BKC skyscraper in harsh daylight, Mumbai sprawl far below — finding the woman with the high ponytail (Aly) free-climbing the glass exterior near the top, wind tearing at her. Anamorphic lens flare raking across the glass.

Shot 2 — High above her on the roof, a black military helicopter flares in and a masked merc team fast-ropes down onto the rooftop beside a tall tower crane. Low angle craning up past Aly to the chopper and the crane, rotor wash blasting.

Shot 3 — A row of top-floor windows blows out in a chain of explosions, glass erupting outward over the city. Crash-zoom into a hard speed-ramp, glass shards freezing mid-air.

Shot 4 — Bullet-time: Aly swings sideways across the facade and crashes in through the blown-out gap, glass suspended around her — then snaps back to full speed as she rolls onto the office floor.

Shot 5 — Far below in the lobby stairwell, the bald man in the leather vest (Rahul) drops two masked mercs and starts powering up the stairs, shouting into a comm. Low tracking shot rising with Rahul up the stairwell.
```
