CINEMATIC SETUP:
Handheld chase cam, dynamic parkour tracking shot, aggressive whip pans, motion blur on fast movement, anamorphic lens flares from neon signage, shallow depth of field, high-contrast blade-runner color grade (teal shadows, magenta/orange highlights).

CHARACTERS:
[Image 1] — young man, textured coiled updo hairstyle, cropped grey tactical jacket with orange sleeve patch, oversized black cargo pants, chunky off-white armored boots, black gloves. Athletic, precise, controlled movement — a rooftop runner.

LOCATION:
[Image 2] — dense futuristic megacity at night, towering neon-lit skyscrapers, holographic billboards, layered elevated highways, thick atmospheric haze, distant flying traffic.

TIMELINE:
0:00–0:03 — Character sprints across a rooftop edge, low-angle tracking shot, builds speed, plants foot and launches into a full-extension leap off the ledge.
0:03–0:06 — Mid-air freefall between two skyscrapers, camera whips downward following him, neon reflections streak across his jacket, city lights blur past.
0:06–0:09 — He lands in a hard roll on the roof of a passing flying car, absorbing impact, immediately pushes up into a sprint across its hood.
0:09–0:12 — Leaps from the flying car to a second one weaving through elevated traffic, mid-air rotation, grabs the edge and vaults forward without breaking stride.
0:12–0:15 — Final leap onto a higher flying platform, sticks the landing in a low crouch, camera pulls back into a wide hero shot revealing the full neon skyline behind him.

SFX:
Wind rush on freefall, metallic impact thud on landings, distant flying-traffic hum, faint synth pulse building through the sequence, city ambience (sirens, hover-engine drones).

QUALITY ANCHOR:
Photorealistic, cinematic 4K render, sharp character detail against motion-blurred environment, consistent character identity and outfit across all shots, seamless continuous motion, no morphing or drift between beats.
