# Epic desert train escape, 15-second single continuous shot, photorealistic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Epic desert train escape, 15-second single continuous shot, photorealistic cinematic action style. Begin with a high-altitude glide over endless golden sand dunes at sunset, long shadows stretching across the desert under glowing orange skies. Camera dives rapidly toward a speeding armored train cutting through the dunes, sand blasting behind the wheels. Transition into a fast side-tracking shot inches from the train, weaving past steam, sparks, and whipping cables while riders cling to the exterior during the chase. Camera races forward along the tracks into a dramatic frontal lead shot as a colossal ancient desert city carved into canyon cliffs emerges through a swirling sandstorm ahead. Finish with a massive vertical pullout above the canyon, revealing the train disappearing into the forgotten city surrounded by endless dunes and storm clouds. Cinematic realism, seamless long take, dynamic FPV drone motion, atmospheric lighting, realistic sand physics, high-speed energy, ultra-detailed cinematic visuals, IMAX composition.
```
