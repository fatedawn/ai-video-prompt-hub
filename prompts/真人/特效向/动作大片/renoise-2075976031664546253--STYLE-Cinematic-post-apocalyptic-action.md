# STYLE: Cinematic post-apocalyptic action trailer, high energy, warm filmic…

## 提示词（English）

```text
STYLE: Cinematic post-apocalyptic action trailer, high energy, warm filmic color, soft film grain.

SCENE: Desolate salt flat wasteland at twilight, low sun bleeding orange along the horizon, distant jagged mountains silhouetted, dust haze catching the light, design language: rusted metal and cracked white earth.

CHARACTER / SUBJECT: A wild raider driver — fierce figure with windswept hair, goggles, and scavenged armor plates — piloting a brutal custom dune buggy with massive off-road tires, exposed engine, and spiked roll cage.

CAMERA: Dynamic tracking shot racing low alongside the buggy with subtle arcs around it.

ACTION: The buggy rockets across the wasteland kicking up a towering tail of dust, the driver grinning as she jerks the wheel and the vehicle bucks over ruts and ridges. Then she yanks into a wide power slide, wheels throwing a storm of sand as the camera swings slightly around the front to catch her intense expression framed by the setting sun. Then the buggy snaps straight and charges toward the glowing horizon even faster, dust plumes twisting behind while the camera keeps surging beside the roaring machine and the sky darkens into deep blue.

ON-SCREEN TEXT: clean frame, no on-screen text.

SOUND DESIGN: Deafening engine roar and turbo whine, grit hammering the chassis, wind howling past, no dialogue, driven by a pounding percussion and distorted guitar cue that swells through the slide and continues as the acceleration grows.

NOTES: consistent identity for the driver; realistic sand, dust, hair, and fabric motion.
```
