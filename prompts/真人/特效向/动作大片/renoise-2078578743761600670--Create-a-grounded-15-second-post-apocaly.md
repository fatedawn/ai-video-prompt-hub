# Create a grounded 15-second post-apocalyptic race between the three reference…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a grounded 15-second post-apocalyptic race between the three reference cars for one canyon ramp.

Lock vehicles:
Image 1: low black interceptor = hero.
Image 2: six-wheeled spiked armored car with front plow = heavy pursuer.
Image 3: extremely long dark muscle car = fast rival.
Never merge, duplicate, recolor or exchange them. Each has a distinct driver.

GEOGRAPHY: Two disconnected cliffs face each other across 25 metres of open air. A short six-metre wedge ramp sits entirely on the near plateau and ends at its cliff edge. The far cliff is bare landing ground. Nothing spans the gap: no bridge, track, deck, beam, cable or supports.

0.0-1.5 - Reveal danger
Locked 18mm extreme-wide side view: short ramp left, deep canyon centre, separate landing plateau right. Hold before cars appear. One massive low-frequency impact hits with a metal groan and canyon echo. No music. Brutal white sun and heat shimmer bend the horizon.

1.5-2.6 - Race arrives
Long 135mm view. Three distant vehicles charge through heat distortion, each trailing separate pale dust. Engines rise from rumble to violent roar.

2.6-5.0 - Present the machines
Three rapid 0.8-second pairs:
Hero: 24mm beside front wheel looking back along its body; cut to supercharger shaking under torque.
Truck: beside spiked front tire; cut to huge intake, belts and plow vibrating.
Rival: wheel-level along its long body; cut to engine bank and pulsing exhaust.
Tires spin and suspensions work.

5.0-6.3 - Present the drivers
Three tight cuts. Hero watches ramp and mirrors, sweat crossing his dusty face. Truck driver looks right, snarls and steers inward. Rival driver looks left and steers inward. Show each decision before impact.

6.3-8.3 - Delay the hero
Low front tracking: truck left, hero centre, rival right. Truck rams hero while rival blocks escape. Hero is forced into loose salt, fishtails and drops three lengths behind. Rivals surge ahead. Hero counter-steers without panic.

8.3-9.8 - Chase back
Rear three-quarter tracking. Hero straightens, downshifts, drafts behind the truck, then pulls right through dust. He closes to one length behind the rival's outside rear quarter.

9.8-15.0 - Rear-quarter strike and jump, one continuous wide shot
Locked elevated 18mm three-quarter side view containing approach, complete short ramp, empty canyon and landing ledge. Truck leads in the left lane, rival beside it right, hero behind and outside the rival on far right.

Hero's left front corner strikes the rival's right rear quarter, yawing its nose left into the truck. The truck's plow catches the rival's left flank. Both pursuers lock together and slide left, completely outside the ramp line. They pass beside the ramp, leave the bare cliff edge and fall into the canyon.

Only after both pursuers clear the ramp line, hero releases contact, counter-steers left and aligns all four wheels with the ramp centre. Hero hits squarely, crosses open air and lands on the far plateau. Keep the strike, collision, cleared ramp, takeoff, flight arc and landing readable in the same shot. Neither pursuer touches the ramp. Only Image 1 crosses.

After the opening impact use engines, tires, metal, wind and canyon echoes. Harsh sun, bleached salt, heat shimmer, sweating faces and tire-driven dust. No bridge, extra cars, slow motion, fireballs, floating debris, weightless flight or mid-air steering.
```

固定された18mmの極端な広角サイドビュー：左に短いランプ、中央に深い峡谷、右に分離した着地用台地。車が現れる前で止める。巨大な低周波の衝撃が1発、金属のうめきと峡谷の反響を伴って響く。音楽なし。苛烈な白い太陽と熱の揺らぎが地平線を歪ませる。

1.5-2.6 - レース到来
長い135mmビュー。3台の遠い車両が熱の歪みを突き抜けて突進し、それぞれが別々の淡い砂塵を引く。エンジンは低いうなりから暴力的な咆哮へと高まる。

2.6-5.0 - マシンを見せる
0.8秒の短いカットを3組：
Hero: 前輪の横、24mmで車体に沿って後方を見る；カットして、トルクで震えるスーパーチャージャー。
Truck: スパイク付き前輪の横；カットして、巨大な吸気口、ベルト、プラウが振動。
Rival: 車体の長さに沿ってホイールレベル；カットして、エンジンバンクと脈動する排気。
タイヤは回転し、サスペンションは働く。

5.0-6.3 - ドライバーを見せる
3つのタイトなカット。Heroはランプとミラーを見つめ、埃まみれの顔を汗が流れる。Truckのドライバーは右を見る、唸って内側へ切る。Rivalのドライバーは左を見て内側へ切る。衝突の前に、それぞれの判断を見せる。

6.3-8.3 - Heroを遅らせる
低いフロント追従：左にTruck、中央にHero、右にRival。TruckがHeroに体当たりし、Rivalが逃げ道を塞ぐ。Heroはゆるい塩の上へ押し出され、尻振りしながら3台分後退する。ライバルたちは前へ押し出す。Heroは慌てずカウンターステアする。

8.3-9.8 - 追い上げ
後方3/4追従。Heroは姿勢を立て直し、シフトダウンし、Truckの後ろでスリップストリームに入り、その後、砂塵を切って右へ抜ける。Rivalの外側後方1台分まで詰める。

9.8-15.0 - 後方3/4への打撃とジャンプ、1つの連続したワイドショット
固定された高所からの18mm 3/4サイドビューで、接近、完全な短いランプ、空の峡谷、着地用の縁をすべて収める。Truckが左レーンで先行し、Rivalがその右に並び、HeroはRivalのさらに右外側後方にいる。

Tracking frontal baixo: caminhão à esquerda, herói no centro, rival à direita. O caminhão acerta o herói enquanto o rival bloqueia a fuga. O herói é forçado para o sal solto, derrapa de traseira e cai três comprimentos atrás. Os rivais avançam. O herói corrige a direção sem pânico.

8.3-9.8 - Perseguição de volta
Tracking traseiro em três quartos. O herói endireita, reduz marcha, pega vácuo atrás do caminhão e então puxa para a direita pela poeira. Ele se aproxima até ficar a um comprimento atrás do quarto traseiro externo do rival.

9.8-15.0 - Golpe no quarto traseiro e salto, um único plano geral contínuo
Vista lateral em três quartos, elevada e travada em 18mm, contendo a aproximação, a rampa curta completa, o cânion vazio e a borda de pouso. O caminhão lidera na faixa esquerda, o rival ao lado à direita, o herói atrás e por fora do rival, bem à direita.

O canto dianteiro esquerdo do herói atinge o quarto traseiro direito do rival, girando sua dianteira para a esquerda em direção ao caminhão. O aríete do caminhão prende a lateral esquerda do rival. Ambos os perseguidores se travam juntos e deslizam para a esquerda, completamente fora da linha da rampa. Eles passam ao lado da rampa, deixam a borda nua do penhasco e caem no cânion.

Somente depois que ambos os perseguidores saem da linha da rampa, o herói solta
