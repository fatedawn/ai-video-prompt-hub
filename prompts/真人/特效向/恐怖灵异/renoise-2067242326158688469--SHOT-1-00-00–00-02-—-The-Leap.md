# SHOT 1 (00:00–00:02) — The Leap

## 提示词（English）

```text
SHOT 1 (00:00–00:02) — The Leap

EFFECT: Speed ramp (deceleration) + directional motion blur
Low-angle hero shot: pirate launches off a rope mid-air toward the looming cursed ship, coat billowing, glowing blue energy sword trailing a luminous arc
Camera swings upward in an arcing crane move, sea spray flying past lens
Decelerates from fast launch into floating slow-mo at apex (~30% speed)
Exits as the blade catches light — bloom flare bridges to next shot

SHOT 2 (00:02–00:04) — Deck Impact

EFFECT: Camera shake + impact dust burst
Pirate lands hard on the rotting deck, plank splinters and embers scatter; zombie pirates lurch from the fog around him
Whip-tilt from sky down to deck level, hard jolt on landing
Heavy handheld vibration on impact frame
Cuts on a blade-raise gesture

SHOT 3 (00:04–00:06) — First Strike

EFFECT: Speed ramp + blue energy light trail
Sword swings in a wide horizontal arc, slicing through two zombies; glowing blue cuts leave persistent light streaks across frame
Orbiting camera circles the pirate 180° during the swing
Ramps fast through the slash, snaps to slow-mo on contact
Whip-pan exit smears into next shot

SHOT 4 (00:06–00:08) — Overhead Chaos

EFFECT: Top-down God's-eye + digital zoom (scale-in)
Bird's-eye looking straight down: pirate at center, ring of zombies closing, blue blade carving glowing trails
Camera plunges downward, punching into the scene
Normal speed, kinetic
Bloom flash transition out

SHOT 5 (00:08–00:10) — Parry & Counter

EFFECT: Slow-motion (~20%) + sparks
A zombie's rusted cutlass clashes with the energy blade — explosive blue sparks erupt in deep slow-mo, debris frozen mid-air
Extreme close push-in on the locked blades, rack focus to the pirate's eyes
Holds slow, then snaps back
Hard cut on the shove

SHOT 6 (00:10–00:12) — Whirlwind

EFFECT: Spin camera + frame rotation + motion blur
THE SIGNATURE VISUAL: pirate spins a full 360° cut, blade carving a complete glowing blue ring of light around him, zombies flung outward
Camera rotates with him, image rolls ~180°, heavy blur
Speed ramp through the spin
Light-trail ring blooms into white flash transition

SHOT 7 (00:12–00:14) — Final Thrust

EFFECT: Speed ramp (deceleration) + dolly-in
Pirate lunges, driving the glowing sword through the last zombie; body dissolves into blue embers and ash
Low dramatic dolly-in on the impaling thrust
Decelerates to near-freeze on the kill, embers drifting
Slow fade-out

SHOT 8 (00:14–00:15) — Hero Hold

EFFECT: Clean slow-motion + atmospheric bloom
Pirate stands amid drifting ash and dying embers, blue sword lowered, glowing softly against the wrecked ship and stormy sky
Slow pull-back, slight upward tilt
~40% speed, no other effects — energy resolves
Final frame settles
```
