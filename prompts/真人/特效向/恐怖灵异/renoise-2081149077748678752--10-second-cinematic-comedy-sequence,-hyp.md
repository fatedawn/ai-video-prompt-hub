## 提示词（English）

```text
10-second cinematic comedy sequence, hyper-realistic, 16:9.

One continuous unbroken shot, no cuts. In a foggy forest clearing at night under a full moon, a rugged man in torn clothes stumbles forward and begins a dramatic werewolf transformation. He growls, bends over, and his body starts changing continuously on screen.
His arms shorten, his hands shrink into tiny paws, his claws soften, his body rapidly becomes smaller instead of larger, his face stretches forward then compresses into a cute snout, thick fur bursts out and becomes soft fluffy white curls, his ears reshape, and his whole body transforms in a funny but fluid way without any camera cut or jump.
The transformation must be fully visible from start to finish. By the end of the same shot, he has completely become a small fluffy white poodle with a pink bow collar.
The poodle lands, blinks proudly, gives a cheerful bark, then does a tiny elegant prance toward camera. Serious horror buildup, ridiculous payoff, clear visual morphing, playful horror-comedy tone, highly detailed fur, no text on screen, no captions, no famous celebrity faces, no recognizable actors, no public-figure likenesses.
```
