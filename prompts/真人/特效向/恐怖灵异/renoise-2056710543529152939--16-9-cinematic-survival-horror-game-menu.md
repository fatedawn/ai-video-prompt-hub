# 16:9 cinematic survival horror game menu screen, inspired by early 2000s…

## 提示词（English）

```text
16:9 cinematic survival horror game menu screen, inspired by early 2000s psychological horror games. Dark forest at night with deep black shadows, faint fog, subtle film grain, CCD camera texture, harsh flashlight bloom and soft chromatic aberration.

On the right side stands a young blonde woman with pale skin and delicate facial features, resembling an indie horror game protagonist. She wears a dark navy school-style cardigan with a white Peter Pan collar, short pleated skirt, black sheer tights, knee-high boots, silver cross necklace, and a small vintage digital camera hanging at her waist. Her expression is emotionless, tense, and cautious. She holds a flashlight near her face while slowly walking forward through the darkness, scanning the forest carefully. The flashlight beam cuts through the darkness with cinematic glow. Her hair moves slightly from the wind. She looks nervous but controlled, creating strong psychological horror tension.

On the left side is a realistic game UI overlay. Large distressed white title text reads:
“SILENT EVIL”
“OBSCURE MEMORIES”
Below the title is a vertical menu:
continue
new game
load game
settings
credits
exit
ONLY “continue” is selected with a soft glowing white highlight bar behind it. The other menu items remain dim gray. Minimalistic PlayStation-style UI icons appear at the bottom corners for “Select” and “Confirm.”

The overall atmosphere is eerie, quiet, tense, cinematic, and realistic, like a paused gameplay screen from a psychological horror game. High contrast lighting, dark blue-black tones, realistic shadows, subtle UI blur, immersive horror mood.
```
