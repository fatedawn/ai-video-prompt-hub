Start the video from a provided reference image (@ image1).
Maintain the same character identity, outfit, lighting, and composition consistency throughout the sequence.
A cinematic 15-second continuous shot featuring a young, cute Japanese woman in a soft late-Heisei aesthetic interior, holding a retro handheld gaming device inspired by 1990s Japanese portable game consoles.
No logos, no text, no branding on the device or screen.
Scene & Camera Movement
From the starting reference image, the camera begins in a high-angle shot already established in the image.
The camera slowly and smoothly dollies forward and slightly downward toward the subject, maintaining cinematic stability and natural motion.
The woman is actively playing a fictional handheld fighting game.
As the camera gets closer, her facial expression subtly evolves:
she starts with a bright cheerful smile → gradually transitions into a cute concentrated slight frown, showing playful frustration and immersion in gameplay.
Device Close-up & Transition
The camera continues a seamless forward push-in until it reaches an extreme close-up of the handheld device screen.
The screen fills the entire frame.
A fully seamless transition occurs without a cut: the camera appears to pass through the screen surface.
Game World Transition
The handheld display transforms into full-screen gameplay footage of a fictional arcade fighting game.
Inside the game world: stylized retro-inspired fighting game visuals, dynamic motion, combo attacks, energy effects, health bars (no readable text).
Fast-paced but clean composition, slightly nostalgic arcade aesthetic.
The camera continues moving forward inside the game world, as if entering the gameplay space itself.
Style & Motion
Cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field at the beginning (based on input image), transitioning to full sharp focus in-game.
Ultra smooth motion, no cuts, no jitter.
Mood
Late 1990s Japanese nostalgia, cozy indoor atmosphere evolving into immersive arcade game intensity.

초매끄러운 움직임, 컷 없음, 흔들림 없음.
분위기
1990년대 후반 일본의 향수, 아늑한 실내 분위기가 몰입감 있는 아케이드 게임 강도로 진화합니다.
