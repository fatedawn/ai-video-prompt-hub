# Create a cinematic live theater stage performance video using ONLY the provided…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Create a cinematic live theater stage performance video using ONLY the provided single reference image as the visual reference. Use it only for stage layout, audience POV, character appearances, costumes, lighting, and composition. Do not reference any storyboard, grid, panels, or other images. The video must be one continuous uncut handheld smartphone recording from the audience.

The camera is slightly unsteady, raised above people’s heads. In the foreground, keep dark audience silhouettes, hands, and several smartphones recording. The stage is the same outdoor Japanese-style theater set: large wooden gate, stone walls, flame banners, wooden structures, warm lanterns, bamboo, trees, smoke, and dramatic night lighting. It must feel like a real live-action stage performance, not animation.

On the left is a flame-haired swordsman: wild yellow hair with red-orange tips, dark fitted uniform, white cape with red-orange flame patterns, holding a katana. On the right is a pink-haired martial artist: short spiky pink hair, pale skin, blue stripe markings across face, chest, and arms, loose white pants, open sleeveless martial-arts vest, wide defensive stance. Do not use character names.

0s–3s: The scene begins exactly like the reference image. Both performers stand far apart, facing each other. The swordsman holds his katana forward in a low aggressive stance. The martial artist watches from the right with one hand raised defensively. The audience waits silently, phones held high, with subtle handheld camera shake.

3s–6s: The swordsman lowers his body and pulls fire from within himself, as if an inner flame is awakening. Orange light glows from his chest and shoulders, then spreads through his arms, cape, hair, and legs. Fire ignites beneath his feet and spreads across the stage floor. His katana catches fire from handle to tip until the whole blade burns intensely. The audience gasps, then screams and cheers.

6s–9s: The swordsman raises the flaming katana and rotates it in a powerful circular motion. Fire gathers into the blade, then bursts outward. As he swings toward the martial artist, the flames form a huge dragon-shaped attack with a blazing body, bright head, open jaws, and flying embers, rushing from left to right. The camera stays in audience POV, shaking as the crowd shouts.

9s–11s: The dragon-shaped flame crashes into the martial artist. He lowers his stance and raises both hands to defend. The impact hits his guard with sparks, smoke, and orange light. He slides slightly backward but stays standing. The audience screams louder, cheers, and applauds.

11s–13s: Without any cut, the swordsman charges forward through smoke and fire. His body remains wrapped in flames, his cape trails behind him, and the burning katana leaves a bright streak. He leaps toward the martial artist and swings downward in a circular slash. The blade creates a glowing ring-shaped arc of fire.

13s–15s: The final clash happens at close range. The martial artist catches and stops the flaming katana with both hands. The swordsman pushes forward, engulfed in flames, fierce and determined. The defender strains against the blade, focused and slightly shocked. Sparks explode between the sword and his hands. Smoke drifts across the stage floor. The circular flame arc lingers as the audience reaches peak excitement, screaming and roaring.

Technical specs: single continuous uncut shot, no camera cuts, no montage, no storyboard panels, no extra reference images, audience POV, handheld smartphone footage, vertical video, realistic live-action performers, practical stage fire mixed with cinematic VFX, warm orange firelight, smoke, sparks, embers, shallow focus, visible crowd silhouettes and phone screens, no subtitles, no text overlay, no watermark, no gore, no animation style.
```
