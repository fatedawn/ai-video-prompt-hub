SHOT 2 (00:01–00:03) — First Strike

Low tracking. Shahy snaps a curling kick — glowing ball rockets, slams a Ninja's chest, he flies back. Ball ricochets off wall.

SHOT 3 (00:03–00:04) — Return & Reload

EFFECT: focus pull
Ball boomerangs back; she traps it on her boot. Orbit-cam circles her ~90°.

SHOT 4 (00:04–00:06) — Counter Flow

EFFECT: slow-mo (~25%) → ramp out
Ninja lunges with katana. Shahy weaves, backbends under the blade, rises and volleys the ball into his helmet. Handheld snap-zoom.

SHOT 5 (00:06–00:07) — Wall Bounce

EFFECT: motion-blur transition
Ball pinballs alley wall to wall, dropping 2 Ninjas. Whip-pan follows the ball's path.

SHOT 6 (00:07–00:08) — Pressure

EFFECT: camera shake + speed ramp
Two Ninjas rush. She juggles ball knee-to-knee, parrying a strike, sidesteps a shuriken.

SHOT 7 (00:08–00:10) — Setup

EFFECT: digital zoom punch-in
She flicks the ball skyward, plants, eyes lock. Low hero angle, neon backlight.

SHOT 8 (00:10–00:13) — SIGNATURE "SKY CUT"

EFFECT: deep slow-mo (~20%) + 360° orbit + light trails — THE SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT
Shahy launches into an aerial backflip bicycle-kick. Camera orbits fully around her mid-air. Boot smashes the glowing ball downward — it streaks in a blue energy arc, knocking down Ninjas one by one in a chain.

SHOT 9 (00:13–00:14) — Impact Chain

EFFECT: speed ramp (accel) + shake
Whip-fast cuts: ball strikes helmet, helmet, helmet — bodies drop in sequence. Sparks, fog burst.

SHOT 10 (00:14–00:15) — Landing

EFFECT: slow-mo settle + gentle zoom-out
Shahy lands in a crouch, ball spinning back into her hand. She rises, alley silent, fog drifting. Hold on her face.
