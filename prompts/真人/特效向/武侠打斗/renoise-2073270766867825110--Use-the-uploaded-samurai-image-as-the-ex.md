# Use the uploaded samurai image as the exact visual reference for the warrior…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use the uploaded samurai image as the exact visual reference for the warrior, pose language, monochrome brush-sketch style, motion trails, costume silhouette, and overall minimalist composition.

Single continuous cinematic shot, no cuts, no edits, no morphing, no duplicated limbs or weapons.

A lone samurai performs a fierce and elegant sword movement in an open empty space, as if practicing a deadly master-level kata. The motion begins in stillness, with calm control and intense focus. Then the samurai explodes into action with a powerful forward pivot, sweeping the katana in a wide circular arc. The movement should feel fluid, disciplined, and precise, not chaotic. His robes, sleeves, and trailing ink-like motion strokes flow dramatically with every turn, creating beautiful calligraphic energy in the air.

Begin from a low side angle near the ground, slightly behind the samurai. The camera immediately starts a fast, smooth orbit around him in one uninterrupted continuous movement. As the samurai spins, slashes, steps, and repositions, the camera circles dynamically, revealing different silhouettes of the blade, torso, and flowing garment. Keep strong parallax and momentum, with the sword arcs leaving elegant smoky brush trails in space.

During the middle section, the samurai performs a sequence of sharp gestures: a rising slash, a turning horizontal cut, a brief pause in guarded stance, then a final decisive forward strike. The camera stays fast and fluid, pushing closer during the strikes and widening slightly during turns, while always remaining one continuous orbiting move.

At the climax, the samurai lands in a final powerful finishing pose with the katana extended and his robes settling slowly. The last moment should feel intense, controlled, and iconic. Maintain the exact monochrome ink-brush aesthetic throughout, with a clean pale background, black expressive sketch lines, smoky motion trails, and minimalist cinematic elegance. Landscape composition, high contrast, dramatic motion, meditative yet fierce.
```
