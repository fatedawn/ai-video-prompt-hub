# 电影级竞技场决斗对决

*Cinematic Arena Duel Showdown*

> 这是一个高强度、结构化的视频提示词，用于创作一场发生在竞技场中的电影级对决，包含饱经风霜的角色和动态的动作序列。

## 提示词（English）

```text
FORMAT: 15s / 150 BPM / 15 SHOTS
SUBJECT: @[image1]
WARDROBE: Battle-worn outfit
ENVIRONMENT: Open arena
MOOD: Tense → epic
STYLE: Cinematic showdown
CORE IDEA: One final duel
SHOT FLOW:
Two figures facing
Wind blowing
Close-up eyes
Grip weapon
Silence
Sudden clash
Fast strikes
Sparks flying
Step back
Final move
Slow motion hit
Opponent drops
Breath
She stands alone
End
```
