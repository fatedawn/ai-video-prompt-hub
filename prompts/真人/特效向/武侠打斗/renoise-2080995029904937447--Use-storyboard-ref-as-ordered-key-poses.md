# Use @[storyboard ref] as ordered key poses for a cinematic dark-fantasy alley…

## 提示词（English）

```text
Use @[storyboard ref] as ordered key poses for a cinematic dark-fantasy alley fight. Pass through P01–P12 in order with matched action, purposeful cuts, and clear continuity. Ignore storyboard borders, numbers, captions, and line artifacts.

Use @[character ref1] for the Ashen Revenant and @[character ref2] for the Ember Ronin.

Preserve exact silhouettes, armor, hairstyle, wardrobe, weapons, accessories, proportions, and colors. The Revenant is a towering warrior in fractured black armor with glowing red seams, a white slotted helmet, torn crimson cloth, chains, clawed gauntlets, and bone-white smoke tendrils. The Ronin is an agile warrior with a wild dark topknot, intense eyes, red face wrap, layered white and tan robes, red cords, metal armor, and a curved flame-edged blade.

Exactly two fighters. Never duplicate them or swap identities, weapons, powers, costumes, or scale.

LOOK + WORLD:

Cinematic dark-fantasy live-action style with realistic materials and physically believable movement. Narrow neon alley at night with reflective pavement, stacked crates, concrete walls, pipes, cables, vents, drifting steam, weathered signs, and distant city structures.

Magenta, cyan, violet, and blue neon reflect across the ground and armor. The Ronin’s orange fire and the Revenant’s red glow cast practical light on faces, fabric, weapons, smoke, and nearby surfaces.

Gritty high-contrast night grade, rich blacks, cool shadows, saturated highlights, atmospheric haze, and controlled motion blur. No crowds, vehicles, extra fighters, comedy, anime outlines, captions, logos, or UI.

CAMERA + ACTION:

Follow P01–P12 chronologically. The Revenant starts frame left and the Ronin frame right. Maintain screen direction unless a motivated cut establishes reversal.

Use 24mm wides, 35mm handheld tracking, 50mm medium combat coverage, and 85mm impact close-ups. Handheld but readable, with brief shake only on hard hits.

The Revenant moves with crushing weight and supernatural force. The Ronin moves with speed, precision, grounded technique, and controlled fire-enhanced attacks.

The Ronin uses his curved blade for fast slashes and parries, then transitions to close-range strikes and a final armored haymaker. The Revenant uses armored fists, claws, a chained weapon, and a heavy jagged blade. Weapons never vanish, change shape, switch owners, or pass through bodies.

Use sparks, scraping metal, swinging chains, cloth motion, steam, splinters, dust, and debris. No beams, portals, lightning, gore, or dismemberment.

SOUND:

No music. Heavy breathing, boots on pavement, fabric snapping, armor shifting, chains rattling, blades striking metal, punch impacts, body thuds, grunts, fire crackle, steam hiss, crates breaking, debris falling, distant traffic, neon hum, and ventilation fans.

MOTION PHRASES:

P01: Wide standoff. Revenant left, Ronin right. Hold one tense beat.

P02: Ronin explodes forward, blade igniting as scarf and robes trail.

P03: Ronin throws a heavy punch at the helmet. Revenant raises an armored block.

P04: Tight impact close-up. Punch meets forearm. Sparks burst; brief camera jolt.

P05: Ronin converts into a horizontal flaming slash. Revenant parries.

P06: Revenant counters with a chained weapon passing close to camera.

P07: Track beside them as the chain assault drives the Ronin backward.

P08: Revenant swings high. Ronin ducks; camera drops with him.

P09: Ground-level sweep to the Revenant’s lead leg. Revenant stumbles.

P10: Revenant recovers, red energy brightening, then lunges.

P11: Low-angle close-up. Ronin pivots, plants, and draws back his armored fist as orange fire compresses around it.

P12: Flaming haymaker connects with the helmet or upper torso. Hold briefly at maximum compression, then release. Revenant crashes through stacked crates as splinters, sparks, chains, smoke, and debris fly past camera. End on the peak impact frame.
```
