Film Style: Surreal dark romance, photorealistic, heavy filmic grain, degraded film texture, dreamlike. Visual anchor: Wong Kar-wai meets vertigo dream sequence.
Color Grade: Deep desaturated blue-teal dominant, crushed blacks. Warm orange-amber light filtering through ahead — bright blue void visible through the last trees. They burst out of the treeline to the cliff edge. She stops. He doesn't.
[3–6s] He steps off the cliff — and doesn't fall. He floats, suspended in the void over the panoramic valley, weightless, hair drifting upward, shirt billowing slowly. Camera begins orbiting. He turns back to her. Extends his hand. She stands on the edge, wind pulling her hair forward, toes curling over the rock. The wide frame emphasizes the terrifying scale — her on solid ground, him floating against an infinite misty valley. She looks down at the void. Looks back at him. Steps off.
They meet in midair, then inverted, embracing in impossible orientation. The forest canopy and the valley trade places around them. The wide frame makes the rotation hypnotic.
[9–13s] They embrace fully, floating in the void, slowly rotating together. Her arms around his neck, his arms around her waist. Foreheads touching. Eyes closed. Camera continues its orbit — the misty valley, the distant mountains, the dark forest all spiral gently around them. They are the still center of a rotating world. Weightless, peaceful, surrendered. Slow motion. Mist wraps around them. Frame fades into soft blue-grey haze.
[SOUNDS]
Forest: running footsteps on earth, heavy breathing, branches snapping, wind through leaves. Cliff edge: footsteps stop, sudden silence, only wind. Float: deep ambient drone, wind softening to a whisper, slow ethereal music — reverb piano or strings. Heartbeat fading in and out. No dialogue. Final: wind dissolves, heartbeat slows, complete silence.
