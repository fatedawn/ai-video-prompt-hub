# Surreal Cinematic Whales Flying Over a City at Sunrise

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to create a surreal, cinematic scene of gigantic whales flying slowly through the sky above a city during sunrise, illuminated by warm golden light, capturing a dreamlike realism and poetic atmosphere.

## 提示词（English）

```text
A surreal cinematic gigantic whales flying slowly through the sky above a city while the sun rises behind them, illuminating their bodies in warm golden light. People stand on rooftops watching the impossible scene.. Dreamlike realism, poetic cinematic atmosphere, cinematic, realistic
```
