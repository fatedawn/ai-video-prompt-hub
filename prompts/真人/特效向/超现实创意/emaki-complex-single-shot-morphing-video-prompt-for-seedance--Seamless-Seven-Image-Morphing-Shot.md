# Seamless Seven-Image Morphing Shot

> Create a continuous one-shot morph through seven images; keep subjects in similar positions and colors to make transitions natural and reduce flicker.

## 提示词（English）

```text
【Basic Settings】 structure: Single continuous shot (no cuts) progression: Morphing 7 images sequentially visibility: Each image is clearly recognizable for only an instant (no stopping required) transition: Always smooth and continuous style: Cinematic, high-definition, dynamic, no flicker 【Prompt Body】 Start from <<<Image1>>>. The footage proceeds in a comp
```
