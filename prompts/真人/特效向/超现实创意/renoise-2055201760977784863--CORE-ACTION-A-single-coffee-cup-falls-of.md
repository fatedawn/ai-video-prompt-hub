# [CORE ACTION] A single coffee cup falls off a high-rise balcony. As it…

## 提示词（English）

```text
[CORE ACTION] A single coffee cup falls off a high-rise balcony. As it plummets, the camera locks onto it, and the cup continuously transforms into different objects based on the environment it falls past, finally landing perfectly in a businessman's hand on the street.
[SUBJECT] The falling object. It morphs continuously: Ceramic Coffee Cup → Potted Plant → Paint Can → Bowling Ball → Fishbowl → Briefcase → Paper Airplane → back to a Coffee Cup.
[ENVIRONMENT] The vertical face of a sprawling, chaotic metropolis. High-rise glass balconies, neon billboards, rusted fire escapes, clotheslines, scaffolding, down to the crowded street level.
[CAMERA] 15-second continuous free-fall tracking shot. The camera is locked directly below the object, looking straight up as the city rushes past. Perfect center-frame tracking.
[LIGHTING & MOOD] Vertigo-inducing, high-energy. Sunlight at the top shifts into deep, shadowed, neon-lit concrete canyons as it falls.
[VFX & DYNAMICS] The morphs happen instantly when the object passes environmental thresholds (e.g., passing a billboard turns it neon; passing a clothesline turns it into a fabric bundle). The background motion blur is extreme to convey terminal velocity.
[SOUND CUES] A rising, roaring wind tunnel effect. Sharp, percussive whipping sounds as the object passes scaffolding. A sudden, dead-silent smack at the end when the cup is caught.
[TECHNICAL] Photorealistic live-action, 16:9, continuous unbroken shot, 15s.
```
