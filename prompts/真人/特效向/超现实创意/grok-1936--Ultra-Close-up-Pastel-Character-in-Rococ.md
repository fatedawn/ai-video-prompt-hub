# Ultra Close-up Pastel Character in Rococo Kawaii Surrealism Style

> A prompt for Grok Imagine to generate an ultra close-up portrait of a dreamy pastel character, blending Rococo, Kawaii, Surrealism, and Art Nouveau softness, focusing on golden ratio framing, smooth skin, expressive eyes, and silky pastel hair.

## 提示词（English）

```text
Ultra close up dreamy pastel character, inspired by rococo kawaii surrealism art nouveau softness, golden ratio framing centered on face, ultra smooth skin with realistic texture balance large expressive eyes with layered reflections, soft gradient makeup, silky pastel hair.
```
