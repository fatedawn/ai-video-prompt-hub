# A black steam train moves slowly across an endless ocean at night, its rails…

## 提示词（English）

```text
A black steam train moves slowly across an endless ocean at night, its rails floating just above the water surface. The moon is enormous, cracked, and reflected perfectly in the sea. Each train window contains a different dream: a burning house, a childhood bedroom, a city underwater, a faceless wedding, a field of floating chairs.

Opening frame: wide shot from above the ocean. The train cuts through mist, leaving no wake, only drifting silver sparks.

At the 2-second mark, one carriage door opens by itself.

Dreams begin leaking out.

A school of glowing paper fish swims from the doorway into the night. A hallway spills out of a window and stretches across the ocean like a bridge. A sleeping child made of smoke walks along the roof of the train.

The camera tracks beside the train, moving from window to window, each window transforming the environment around it. When the burning-house dream appears, rain falls upward. When the underwater-city dream appears, jellyfish drift through the air.

VFX escalation: the ocean surface becomes a mirror of all dreams at once, overlapping like transparent worlds. The train begins dissolving into steam, carriage by carriage.

Velocity ramp: the moon reflection cracks across the water , fragments float upward, each reflecting a different passenger’s face.

Final moment: the last carriage disappears, leaving only the sound of wheels on invisible tracks.

Surreal dream cinema, moonlit ocean reflections, floating dream fragments, slow poetic VFX, analog film grain, 4K.
```
