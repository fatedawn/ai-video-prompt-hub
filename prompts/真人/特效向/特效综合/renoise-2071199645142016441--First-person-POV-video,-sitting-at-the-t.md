```text
First-person POV video, sitting at the top of a transparent glass water slide built into a massive brown rocky mountain canyon, wearing fluffy blue shark slippers with open mouths and jagged teeth. Rushing blue water flows beneath the legs.

[00:00 – 00:02] — The Dread

Camera is completely still. Legs stretched out on the slide, shark slippers visible, water trickling beneath. Slow inhale heard. Hands grip the edges of the slide. Sudden violent lunge forward. Camera jerks downward aggressively. A loud gasp then instant scream erupts. Shark slippers snap upward from the G-force. Canyon rock walls on both sides begin blurring past. Water sprays up from the slide surface. Motion blur kicks in hard.

[00:04 – 00:07] — Full Speed Descent

[00:07 – 00:10] — Cloud Break

Slide passes through a layer of thin mist and cloud. Brief white-out for half a second. Camera wobbles left and right on a slight slide curve. Screaming continues, laughter breaking through. The desert valley and splash pool far below become briefly visible through a gap in the mist — terrifyingly far down. Lens flare from overcast light.

[00:10 – 00:12] — Final Drop

The steepest section. Camera tilts nearly vertical. Pure screaming. Shark slippers fully airborne, barely on feet. Rock walls give way to open air on both sides. Ground rushing up fast. Camera shake at peak intensity. Water rooster-tailing behind the slide.

[00:12 – 00:14] — Impact Incoming

Blue splash pool fills the entire frame. Screaming hits highest pitch. Camera braces — slight pull-back motion. Shark slippers pointed forward. Everything in motion blur.

[00:14 – 00:15] — Splash
