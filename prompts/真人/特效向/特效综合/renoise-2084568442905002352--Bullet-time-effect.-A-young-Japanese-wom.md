[Thread 1] Rules for time-stop videos
You only need to remember three things

① Declare from the very first line: “This is a time-stopping video”
→ Put “Bullet time effect.” at the start of the prompt

② List the things that stop in concrete detail
→ Petals, water droplets, champagne bubbles...
→ At least three. The more you include, the denser the image becomes

③ Reinforce at the end that “only the camera moves”
→ Without this, the AI will make everything move

In other words,
if these three aren’t all there,
it just becomes slow motion
